*A correction first: the email that reached you earlier today ran Sep 5–11, and two of those days had already passed. This is the right window — Monday the 7th through Sunday the 13th. Apologies for the duplicate.*

Three weeks now with nothing on the drives: no race, no charity walk, no permit touching East, West or Center Drive on any of the twenty-one days. Labor Day opens the week sunny and almost entirely unpermitted, and Saturday closes it with four Conservancy walks and tours stacked between 10 AM and 2 PM.

## Weather this week

Sunny and 79° for Labor Day, low 80s and clear Tuesday and Wednesday, a hot and showery 87° Thursday, then 79–80° and sunny Friday and Saturday. Monday morning and the back half of the week are the good efforts; Thursday is the one to shorten or move indoors.

## Mon Sep 7 — Labor Day: clear road, empty calendar

Two permits in the whole park, neither anywhere near a drive. Sunny, 79°, no crowd window to plan around. This is the best combination of road and weather since the season started — take the long one whenever suits you.

## Tue Sep 8 – Thu Sep 10 — three empty days, then the heat

Nothing on the drives and nothing on a path you use. The only closures are lawns: East 106th Street is out Monday through Thursday, Cedar Hill goes out all day Wednesday and stays closed through Friday. Neither changes a route.

Thursday is the week's limiter at 87° with a real shower chance — a dawn day, or a rest day. That evening the [New Dendrology opening reception](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/10/opening-reception-new-dendrology-at-the-arsenal-gallery) at the Arsenal Gallery runs 6–8 PM, free. An exhibition about trees is the right indoor option for this list.

## Fri Sep 11 — the break in the weather

79° and mostly sunny after Thursday's heat. Bowling Green is closed for maintenance until 2 PM, and the WTC Mental Health 9/11 National Day of Remembrance Gathering holds East Meadow 11 AM–1:30 PM. Neither touches the loop.

## Sat Sep 12 — four walks and tours between 10 and 2

The best morning of the week to be on foot:

- **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM at Belvedere Castle. Fall migration is running and the Ramble is filling with birds moving south.
- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)**, 11 AM — behind the scenes with the staff who keep it.
- **[Chess lecture and simul with IM Kassa Korley](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/chess-lecture-simul-with-kassa-korley)**, 11 AM at the Chess and Checkers House.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, 2 PM from Mariners' Gate — the village that stood from West 82nd to 89th before the park did.

Evening brings the [Marcus King Band](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/marcus-king-band/) to Rumsey Playfield 7–10 PM, the first announced SummerStage show in weeks, so the 72nd Street approaches carry crowds from late afternoon. Worth knowing on a bike, not worth avoiding. East Meadow closed all day, and thirty private bookings make the scenic south end busy from mid-morning.

## Sun Sep 13 — pollinators, folkdancers, and a second concert night

The **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)** leaves Boys' Gate at 10 AM. The summer flowers are nearly done, so this is close to the last useful weekend for it. The [Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour) follows at 2 PM.

International Folkdancers hold King Jagiello 11 AM–6 PM and [Young Thug](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/young-thug/) plays Rumsey 8–10 PM — an evening crowd on the same approaches as Saturday. East Meadow closed a second day.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is under an all-day permit titled only "Closed" every day again** — fourteen consecutive days now, still with no reason anywhere in the record. If the Terrace is on your walking line, assume it isn't available.

[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/) runs five mornings, meeting at Strawberry Fields this week, 7:30 and 8:30, $10 at the end.

## Quick recap

- **Nothing on the drives for a third straight week** — twenty-one clear days.
- **Labor Day Monday is the week:** sunny 79°, two permits in the entire park.
- **Four Conservancy walks and tours Saturday** between 10 AM and 2 PM.
- **Pollinator Walk Sunday 10 AM**, near the end of its season.
- **Thursday is the one hard day:** 87° and showery. Friday and Saturday come back to 79–80°.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed a fourteenth straight day**, unexplained.

Enjoy the open road,
