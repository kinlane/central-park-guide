*A correction first: the email that reached you earlier today covered Sep 5–11, two days of which had already gone by. This is the right one — Monday the 7th through Sunday the 13th. Sorry for the second message.*

A slow week that ends in a rush. Monday is a nearly empty Labor Day, the midweek is three quiet evenings, and then Saturday arrives with a chess master playing thirty people at once, three Conservancy tours, a guitar man at Hernshead and the Marcus King Band at Rumsey. SummerStage is back after two weeks of empty permits, with a second show on Sunday.

## Weather this week

Sunny and 79° for Labor Day, clear low-80s Tuesday and Wednesday, a hot and showery 87° Thursday, then a comfortable 79–80° and sunny Friday and Saturday. Both concert nights and all of Saturday's daytime programming fall in good weather; Thursday is the day to be indoors, which is conveniently when the Arsenal opens its new show.

## Mon Sep 7 — Labor Day, and two permits in the whole park

**Central Park Skate Circle** runs at Dead Road North 2–7 PM, and the **Public Works Picnic rain date** sits at the Delacorte 5–6 PM. That is the entire board. The skate circle on a sunny 79° holiday afternoon is the reliable free show.

## Tue Sep 8 – Wed Sep 9 — picnic, public art, evening basketball

The **Public Works Picnic** takes the Delacorte Tuesday 5–6 PM. Wednesday brings a **Public Art Fund opening** at Doris C. Freedman Plaza 6–7 PM and basketball at James Weldon Johnson Playground 6:30–10:30 PM. Cedar Hill closes all day Wednesday through Friday.

## Thu Sep 10, 6–8 PM — New Dendrology opens at the Arsenal Gallery

The **[opening reception](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/10/opening-reception-new-dendrology-at-the-arsenal-gallery)** for New Dendrology — a show about trees, at the Arsenal, free — is the week's headline for four of your interests at once, and it lands on the hottest evening of the week at 87°. Indoors is the right place to be Thursday. The gallery opens Friday 9 AM–5 PM if the reception doesn't suit.

## Fri Sep 11 — remembrance at East Meadow

The **WTC Mental Health 9/11 National Day of Remembrance Gathering** holds East Meadow 11 AM–1:30 PM. Bowling Green is closed for maintenance until 2 PM, and the weather breaks to a mild 79°.

## Sat Sep 12 — the fullest day in a month

**[Marcus King Band with Penelope Road](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/marcus-king-band/)** takes Rumsey Playfield 7–10 PM — SummerStage's first announced show since the season paused, and worth planning the evening around.

The day ahead of it is dense:

- **[Chess lecture and simul with IM Kassa Korley](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/chess-lecture-simul-with-kassa-korley)**, 11 AM at the Chess and Checkers House. He teaches, then plays up to thirty people simultaneously — the best free spectacle of the week for a family.
- **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere Castle, 10 AM, with fall migration underway.
- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 AM, and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs, 9 AM–3 PM. **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM, and **Santa's Knights and Gladiators** on the lawn behind the Met, noon–4 PM.
- **Tango at the Shakespeare Statue**, 6–9 PM.

East Meadow is closed all day, and thirty private ceremony bookings — the heaviest day of the season — have the scenic spots spoken for.

## Sun Sep 13 — folkdancers, harp at Cop Cot, and Young Thug

**[Young Thug with NAV](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/young-thug/)** closes the weekend at Rumsey, 8–10 PM.

Earlier and gentler: **International Sunday** on the Great Hill Oval 10 AM–2 PM, a **Celtic Harp Circle** at Cop Cot noon–2 PM, the **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, and the **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)** from Boys' Gate at 10 AM, near the end of its season.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** sits under an all-day permit titled only "Closed" every day again — fourteen consecutive days, no reason recorded anywhere.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings, from Strawberry Fields this week, 7:30 and 8:30.
- **CP Sports Summer Program** at South Dead Road, Tuesday through Friday.
- **144 field permits** across the week — roughly ninety on the Great Lawn, 34 at Heckscher, 17 at North Meadow. North Meadow is back up from five last week; the leagues have not finished after all.

## Quick recap

- **SummerStage returns:** Marcus King Saturday 7 PM, Young Thug Sunday 8 PM.
- **Chess simul with IM Kassa Korley,** Saturday 11 AM — thirty players at once.
- **Labor Day Monday:** two permits in the park, skate circle 2–7 PM.
- **New Dendrology opens Thursday 6 PM** at the Arsenal, free and indoors on the 87° day.
- **Sunday is the gentle day:** harp at Cop Cot, folkdancers, pollinator walk.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed a fourteenth straight day**, still unexplained.

Enjoy the turn toward fall,
Central Park Guide
