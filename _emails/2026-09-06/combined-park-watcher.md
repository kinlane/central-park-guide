*A correction first: the digest that reached you earlier today ran Sep 5–11, two days of which had already passed. This is the right window — Monday the 7th through Sunday the 13th. Apologies for the duplicate.*

Two readings from the last fortnight get revised this week. The empty SummerStage permits at Rumsey — flagged here twice as holds with nothing behind them — resolve into two announced shows, Marcus King on Saturday and Young Thug on Sunday. And North Meadow, which fell from 23 field permits to 5 and looked like the leagues winding down, is back to 17. That call was premature.

What has not changed: nothing on any drive for a third consecutive week, and Bethesda Terrace closed every single day.

## Weather this week

Sunny and 79° for Labor Day, clear low-80s midweek, a hot and showery 87° Thursday, then 79–80° and sunny Friday and Saturday. Dry weather through the weekend is what lets both concert nights run as scheduled and keeps the lawn-restoration calendar on its stated dates.

## The Bethesda permits, week three

Seven more all-day permits titled nothing but **"Closed"** at Bethesda Fountain Terrace, one per day, midnight to 11:55 PM. That is **fourteen consecutive days** across two weeks with no organizer, no reason and no event recorded against any of them.

Last week I offered two readings — sustained work on the Terrace or arcade, or an administrative hold ahead of something unannounced. A second unbroken week weakens the second: a hold for an event would normally resolve into a permit with a name by now, the way Rumsey's just did. Physical work on the Terrace is the likelier explanation, and it is the thing to confirm on the ground.

## Rumsey resolves — the pattern completes

The two empty SummerStage permits are now filled: **[Marcus King Band with Penelope Road](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/marcus-king-band/)** Saturday 7–10 PM, and **[Young Thug with NAV](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/young-thug/)** Sunday 8–10 PM, with all-day festival permits running Thursday through Sunday.

This is worth filing as a rule rather than a one-off: **the venue permit precedes the announcement by roughly two weeks.** Empty Rumsey permits are not holds that lapse — they are the leading indicator of a run. Next time a bare "Summerstage Festival" permit appears with nothing behind it, expect a show about fourteen days out.

## Mon Sep 7 — the emptiest Labor Day on record here

Two permits in the entire park: the Skate Circle at Dead Road North 2–7 PM and the Public Works Picnic rain date at the Delacorte. **Two private bookings**, against thirty on Saturday. A sunny 79° holiday with a board this bare is not a normal Labor Day — the event calendar has genuinely stopped rather than thinned.

## The restoration block, unchanged in shape

- **East 106th Street lawn** — Monday through Thursday.
- **Cedar Hill** — Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, all day.
- **Bowling Green** — Friday, until 2 PM.
- **East Meadow** — Saturday and Sunday, all day.

The East Side concentration persists into a third week. Nothing touches a drive or a major path.

The **[opening reception](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/10/opening-reception-new-dendrology-at-the-arsenal-gallery)** runs 6–8 PM, public Friday 9 AM–5 PM. The week's one institutional event.

The **WTC Mental Health 9/11 National Day of Remembrance Gathering**, East Meadow 11 AM–1:30 PM.

## The permit load, in aggregate

**144 field permits**, up from 105 last week: roughly ninety on the Great Lawn, 34 at Heckscher, 17 at North Meadow, 3 on the tennis courts. **North Meadow going 23 → 5 → 17 across three weeks** is the number to watch; last week's dip now looks like a scheduling artifact rather than a seasonal wind-down, and I called it wrong.

**79 private bookings**, up from 66 and 50 in the two prior weeks — the early-September ceremony season is still climbing, and steeply. **Saturday alone carries 30**, the heaviest single day of the season. The Great Lawn leads at 11, Ladies' Pavilion at 10, Wagner Cove at 9, Cop Cot at 8. The midweek load has normalized, so this is now a conventional weekend-weighted shape rather than last week's oddity.
