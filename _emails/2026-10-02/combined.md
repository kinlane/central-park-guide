The best week of weather in months, and the park has filled it. **The Central Park Conservancy Film Festival screens four nights at Cherry Hill**, Tuesday through Friday at 7 PM, opening with Carl Sagan. Saturday brings the Terry Fox Run and City of Forest Day in the North Woods. And by Thursday the **Big Apple Triathlon** has the drives.

## Weather this week

Saturday is 71° and sunny, Sunday turns showery at 64° and 54%, and then it is sunny every single day — 70°, 62°, 66°, 71°, 71° — with a 0% rain chance Tuesday through Friday. Nights drop into the 48–56° range, so the film festival will want a blanket.

## Sat Oct 3 — the Terry Fox Run, then City of Forest Day

**The [Terry Fox Run](https://centralpark.guide/events/terry-fox-run-2026-10-03/) holds Bandshell Plaza and the Lower Loop, 9:15–11 AM.** The southern loop is unusable in that window — run or ride before 9 or after 11, or take the bridle path. Earlier still, the **Best Buddies Challenge** runs the Central Park South sidewalks 7–8 AM.

Then the day opens up, 71° and sunny:

- **Wonder in the Woods** — the Conservancy's **City of Forest Day**, a family morning of discovery in the **North Woods** from 10 AM at Gottesman Pool at the Davis Center, with a second session at Arsenal Plaza 11 AM–2 PM.
- **Community Wellness Walk** on the Great Hill Oval, 11 AM–2 PM.
- **Kingman Classic Singles Tournament** on Bowling Green, 10 AM–4 PM — lawn bowls.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9–3; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7; **That Guitar Man** at Hernshead noon–6.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.

East Meadow is closed and **39 private ceremony bookings** land Saturday.

## Sun Oct 4 — a duathlon at dawn, then showers

The **Central Park Solo Duathlon Boat Relay** stages at the Loeb Boathouse 7–9 AM. After that, 64° with a 54% chance of showers:

**Bagels in the Park** at the East 72nd Street Playground 9–11 AM, **Yoga at Dalehead Arch** 10–11:30, **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10, **Vision Boarding** at Cherry Hill 1–3 PM, the **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–6, and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2.

## Mon Oct 5 — nothing at all, and 70° of sunshine

Not one scheduled event in the park. If you want it to yourself, this is the day.

## Tue Oct 6 – Fri Oct 9 — four nights of film at Cherry Hill

**The [Central Park Conservancy Film Festival](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/carl-sagan-we-are-made-of-star-stuff-at-the-central-park-conservancy-film-festival)** screens at Cherry Hill at 7 PM, four nights running:

- **Tuesday — Carl Sagan: We are Made of Star Stuff**
- **Wednesday — Time and Water**
- **Thursday — Blink**
- **Friday — LION**

The permit holds Cherry Hill and Frisbee Hill from morning, so expect the lawn to be set up well before the gates matter. Clear skies all four nights and temperatures falling into the 50s — bring something warm.

The Conservancy's **Women's Committee Game Event** fills the Chess and Checkers House Tuesday and Thursday, and **New Dendrology** is still at the Arsenal Gallery weekdays 9–5, free.

## Thu Oct 8 – Fri Oct 9 — the Big Apple Triathlon takes the drives

**The [Big Apple Triathlon](https://centralpark.guide/events/big-apple-triathlon-2026-10-08/) holds all-day permits across Cherry Hill, Wagner Cove, Bandshell Plaza and every drive** — Center, West, East, the 72nd Street Cross Drive and the Lower Loop — from Thursday, and it runs through the following weekend.

**Cyclists and runners: the loop is committed from Thursday on.** Check before you go out each morning; this is a multi-day build with the race itself next weekend.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 2**.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse. Migration is tailing off but still worth the early start.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday; Columbus Circle Food Market all week.
- **189 field permits** — 81 at Heckscher, 77 at North Meadow, 20 on the tennis courts. **Still none on the Great Lawn.**

- **Big Apple Triathlon takes the drives from Thursday** and runs into next weekend.
