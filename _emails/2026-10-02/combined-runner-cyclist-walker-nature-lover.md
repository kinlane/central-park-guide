Two closures to work around and one perfect day in between. **Saturday the Terry Fox Run takes the Lower Loop 9:15–11 AM**, and **from Thursday the Big Apple Triathlon holds every drive in the park** through the following weekend. Between them sits the best stretch of riding weather this autumn: sunny every day from Monday, 0% rain Tuesday to Friday.

## Weather this week

Saturday 71° and sunny. Sunday 64° with a 54% shower chance. Then Monday 70° mostly sunny, and Tuesday through Friday sunny with a **0% rain chance** at 62°, 66°, 71°, 71°. Nights fall to 48–56°, so mornings are finally cold — dress for the first chilly rides of the year.

## Sat Oct 3, 9:15–11 AM — the Terry Fox Run has the Lower Loop

The **[Terry Fox Run](https://centralpark.guide/events/terry-fox-run-2026-10-03/)** stages at Bandshell Plaza and runs the **Lower Loop** from 9:15 to 11. The southern loop is unusable in that window — **go before 9 or after 11**, or use the bridle path and the Reservoir.

The **Best Buddies Challenge** also runs the Central Park South sidewalks 7–8 AM, so the Merchants' Gate approaches are busy very early.

Once it clears, 71° and sunny with nothing else on the road. Worth knowing: the **Community Wellness Walk** holds the Great Hill Oval 11 AM–2 PM and **City of Forest Day** brings families into the **North Woods** from 10 AM — if you run the north end, expect company in the Ravine and around the Loch.

39 private bookings and East Meadow closed.

## Sun Oct 4 — a duathlon at dawn, then rain

The **Central Park Solo Duathlon Boat Relay** stages at the Loeb Boathouse 7–9 AM — small, but it is on your line if you go early and counterclockwise.

After that, 64° and a 54% shower chance. **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** goes at 2. **Yoga at Dalehead Arch** 10–11:30 if you want something slower on a damp morning.

## Mon Oct 5 — nothing booked, 70°, clear drives

**Not one scheduled event in the park.** Mostly sunny, 70°, a 7% rain chance, and the whole loop to yourself.

This is the day. Take it if you can.

## Tue Oct 6 – Wed Oct 7 — sunny, cold, and still clear

62° and 66°, sunny, 0% rain, nothing on any drive. Nights down to 48–52°, which means genuinely cold starts for the first time this season.

Cherry Hill fills in the evenings from Tuesday — the Conservancy's film festival screens there at 7 PM nightly — so the 72nd Street approaches and the Lower Loop carry foot traffic after dark. Not a closure, but worth lights.

## Thu Oct 8 – Fri Oct 9 — the triathlon takes the drives

**The [Big Apple Triathlon](https://centralpark.guide/events/big-apple-triathlon-2026-10-08/) holds all-day permits on Cherry Hill, Wagner Cove, Bandshell Plaza, and Center, West and East Drive, the 72nd Street Cross Drive and the Lower Loop** — from Thursday, continuing through the following weekend.

**Assume the loop is unavailable from Thursday.** The permits are 24-hour holds rather than a timed race window, so there is no reliable early gap this time. Check on the ground each morning, or move your long effort to Monday through Wednesday, which is what I would do.

Both days are 71° and sunny, which makes the timing a genuine shame.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is closed all day, every day, and the permits have extended again — they now run to **November 2**. That is seven weeks elapsed with a month still on the board.

[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/) runs five mornings from Loeb Boathouse at 7:30 and 8:30. Migration is tailing off, so these are the last good weeks.

East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday; East Meadow closed both weekend days.

## Quick recap

- **Saturday 9:15–11 AM: Terry Fox Run on the Lower Loop.** Before 9 or after 11.
- **From Thursday: Big Apple Triathlon holds every drive**, all-day permits, into next weekend.
- **Monday is the week** — 70°, sunny, zero events, clear drives.
- **Tuesday to Friday: sunny, 0% rain**, 62–71°, cold nights.
- **Film festival at Cherry Hill nightly from Tuesday** — evening foot traffic on the Lower Loop.
- **Bethesda Terrace extended again, now through November 2.**

Take Monday through Wednesday,
