Last week I reported that the Great Lawn had lost four-fifths of its field permits with nothing in the record to explain it, and said the cause was not in the data. **It is now.** Eleven permits for the **Global Citizen Festival** landed in the feed overnight, holding the Great Lawn Oval and South West Lawn from September 20 through September 30, noon to 11 PM daily. The festival itself is **Saturday the 26th, 2–10 PM**.

That answers the question. It also raises a better one about how little warning the public record gives.

## Weather this week

Monday is 70° and partly sunny with a 7% rain chance — the only clear day. Tuesday turns at 63° with 54% light rain, and the rest of the week runs mid-60s with 17–35% shower chances. Festival Saturday is 69° with 34%.

## The Great Lawn, resolved

Field permits by venue, six weeks of this digest:

| Venue | Sep 5–11 | Sep 7–13 | Sep 12–18 | Sep 14–20 | Sep 19–25 | Sep 21–27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heckscher Fields | 24 | 34 | ~60 | 92 | 103 | **111** |
| North Meadow | 5 | 17 | 24 | 38 | 75 | **83** |
| Great Lawn | ~70 | ~90 | ~80 | 55 | 14 | **0** |

The Great Lawn has gone to zero. Heckscher and North Meadow have absorbed all of it and then some — total volume is 200, roughly double where this series started. The leagues did not stop; they were moved off one venue to clear it for a festival.

**What is worth sitting with is the sequencing.** The Great Lawn began emptying in mid-August — the decline runs back through every column above — but the permit that explains it did not appear in the public feed until **September 20, six days before the event**. For weeks the record showed a major venue quietly clearing with no stated reason. Anyone reading the permit feed alone, as this digest does, could see the effect and not the cause.

I named autumn turf protection last week and was wrong; I said I should not have, and that still stands. But the more useful lesson is structural: **the permit feed reports bookings, not intentions.** A festival of this size is negotiated months out and surfaces in public data the week of. The absence of a reason is not the absence of a plan.

## Sat Sep 26 — the festival, and the heaviest booking day on record

Global Citizen draws tens of thousands to mid-park for eight hours. Both transverses and every Great Lawn approach carry it.

Saturday also holds **38 private ceremony bookings** — a new high for this series, past the 40-booking Saturday two weeks ago only in that it lands the same day as a mega-event. Weekly total is 101, the sixth consecutive rise (50 → 66 → 79 → 89 → 91 → 95 → 101), and the curve has now clearly flattened into the high nineties.

## The programming calendar turns over

**SummerStage effectively ends this week.** Young the Giant Tuesday and Of Monsters and Men Wednesday are the last two shows bar Charley Crockett on October 1; after that the series is dark until May 2027. The venue has cleared the weather backlog it was carrying in August and is closing on schedule.

**Climate Week takes the park across three days** — the Tuesday concert is explicitly framed around climate and tick-borne disease, and Thursday brings a cross-sector climate panel to the Arsenal. A themed week landing on both the concert venue and the Arsenal is the pattern I flagged last week; it has held.

Monday carries **one** event in the entire park: free opera at the Bandshell. Two weeks running now with a near-empty Monday.

## Bethesda Terrace, week five

Unchanged: closed all day, every day, permits still running to **October 13**. The end date has now held steady across three consecutive weeks of data, which is the signature of a schedule rather than a rolling extension.

## A note on our own instrument

The digest builder collapsed the Global Citizen Festival into its "steady all week" bucket — one line, alongside the birding walks — because the permit spans seven days. That heuristic is right for a weekly class and wrong for a one-day mega-event with a ten-day build-out. Worth fixing before something similar gets buried.

- **Great Lawn field permits hit zero**; Heckscher 111, North Meadow 83, total volume up to 200.
