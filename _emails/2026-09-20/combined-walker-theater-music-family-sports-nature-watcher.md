The biggest music week of the park's year, and most of it is free. **The Global Citizen Festival takes the Great Lawn on Saturday, 2–10 PM** — artists and world leaders on the global food crisis, free by ticket lottery. Around it: free opera at the Bandshell on Monday, SummerStage's last two shows Tuesday and Wednesday, and salsa on Friday.

## Weather this week

Monday is the pick at 70° and partly sunny with almost no rain risk. Tuesday turns damp at 63°, and the rest of the week runs mid-60s with shower chances of 17–35%. Saturday, festival day, is 69° with a 34% chance — worth a waterproof layer for an eight-hour show.

## Mon Sep 21, 6–7:30 PM — free opera at the Bandshell

**Opera Italiana Is in the Air** plays Bandshell Plaza. Free outdoor opera, 70° and partly sunny, and the only thing booked in the entire park that day. If you like the park at its most civilised, this is the evening.

## Tue Sep 22 — Climate Week: Young the Giant at Rumsey

**[Music for Action](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/music-for-action/)** brings **Young the Giant, St. Lucia and a DREEEMY DJ set** to Rumsey Playfield 7–10 PM — a Climate Week concert raising awareness of climate change and tick-borne disease.

Earlier: **Brigadoon on the River** at Bandshell Plaza 2–3 PM, and a financial vision-boarding workshop at Dana Discovery Center at 6.

## Wed Sep 23 — Of Monsters and Men, and the season's end

**[Of Monsters and Men](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/of-monsters-and-men-august-ponthier/)** play Rumsey 7–10 PM with August Ponthier — the Icelandic band behind "Little Talks."

**This is effectively SummerStage's closing week.** Only Charley Crockett on October 1 remains after Wednesday, and then the series is dark until May 2027. **Kate Farms Yoga** takes East Meadow 5–6 PM beforehand.

## Thu Sep 24 – Fri Sep 25 — a climate panel, then salsa

Thursday the **[Building Cross-Sector Ecosystems for Climate Action panel](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/24/building-crosssector-ecosystems-for-climate-action-panel-dis)** runs at the Arsenal 6–8 PM — free, indoors, the thinking half of Climate Week.

Friday, **Carlos Konig Salsa** plays Bandshell Plaza 5–7:30 PM, with the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Sat Sep 26, 2–10 PM — the Global Citizen Festival

**[The Global Citizen Festival](https://www.centralpark.com/events/global-citizen-festival/)** on the Great Lawn: top artists and world leaders together on the global food crisis and debt relief. Free, by ticket lottery — and if you have not entered, it is worth knowing you cannot simply walk in.

Tens of thousands will be in mid-park for eight hours. **Both transverses and every Great Lawn approach will be dense from noon onward**, so plan any other park business for the morning.

And the morning is good: **[Free Yoga in Central Park](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/free-yoga-in-central-park)** on East Meadow at 11, the **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11, **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9 AM–3 PM, and the **Farmstand and Unity Market** at Dana Discovery Center 9 AM–8 PM. **Tango at the Shakespeare Statue** runs 6–9 PM well away from the crowd.

## Sun Sep 27 — greyhounds on the Dairy Lawn and a new marionette show

The best family day of the month, and the park is quiet again:

- **Greyhounds of NYC Meetup**, Dairy Lawn, 10 AM–1 PM. Precisely what it sounds like.
- **Pinocchio and Geppetto's NYC Adventure** at the **Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre**, 2–3 PM — a new production, and indoors if the weather turns.
- **International Sunday** at Bandshell Plaza 10 AM–2 PM; **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–6; **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7.
- **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10 and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2.

## Steady all week

- **The Great Lawn is under permit every day through September 30**, noon to 11 PM, for the festival build-out. Zero field permits remain on it — league play has moved to Heckscher and North Meadow.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** closed all day, every day, through October 13.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free and indoors.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Strawberry Fields.

## Quick recap

- **Global Citizen Festival, Saturday 2–10 PM**, Great Lawn, free by lottery.
- **Free opera at the Bandshell Monday**, 6–7:30 PM — and the best weather of the week.
- **SummerStage's last two shows:** Young the Giant Tuesday, Of Monsters and Men Wednesday.
- **Sunday: greyhounds on the Dairy Lawn, and a new marionette Pinocchio.**
- **Salsa at the Bandshell Friday**, 5–7:30 PM.
- **The Great Lawn is held through Sep 30**; Bethesda Terrace through Oct 13.
