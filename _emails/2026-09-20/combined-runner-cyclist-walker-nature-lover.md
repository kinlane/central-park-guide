Two things to plan around. Monday is the only genuinely good day — 70°, partly sunny, a 7% rain chance and nothing booked anywhere. And on Saturday the **Global Citizen Festival** takes the Great Lawn from 2 to 10 PM, pulling tens of thousands into the middle of the park; the permit behind it holds the Great Lawn every day from now through September 30.

## Weather this week

Monday: 70°, partly sunny, essentially dry. Tuesday turns at 63° with a 54% chance of light rain, and the rest of the week sits at 64–69° with shower chances between 17% and 35%. Saturday is 69° with a 34% chance. Cool running weather throughout — genuinely pleasant, just damp.

## Mon Sep 21 — take Monday

Nothing is permitted anywhere in the park except an evening opera at the Bandshell. The drives are clear, the air is 70° and dry, and the shower risk is 7%.

**This is the best riding and running day in this email, and the margin is not close.** Move what you can.

## Tue Sep 22 – Thu Sep 24 — damp, cool, clear on the road

Nothing touches a drive. Two Rumsey concerts draw evening crowds onto the 72nd Street approaches — Young the Giant Tuesday, Of Monsters and Men Wednesday, both 7–10 PM — and Kate Farms Yoga takes East Meadow Wednesday 5–6 PM.

Temperatures in the low-to-mid 60s with light rain around. **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** runs five mornings from Strawberry Fields at 7:30 and 8:30, and fall migration is at its peak — a cool damp week is exactly when the Ramble fills with birds moving south.

## Fri Sep 25 — the last easy day before the crowd

67°, 35% shower chance, drives clear. The **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** goes at 2 PM, and salsa plays the Bandshell 5–7:30 PM.

## Sat Sep 26 — go early, then stay out of mid-park

**[The Global Citizen Festival](https://www.centralpark.com/events/global-citizen-festival/)** runs 2–10 PM on the Great Lawn. The loop itself is not closed — but the crowd has to cross it, and for eight hours it will. Both transverses and every Great Lawn approach will be dense from around noon.

**Ride or run before noon.** After that, the south end and the west side are still usable, but the middle of the park is not a place to be moving quickly.

The morning is genuinely good: the **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 — behind the scenes with the staff who keep it, and the garden is turning — plus **free yoga on East Meadow** at 11 if you want something gentler.

East Meadow is closed, and 38 private bookings have the scenic spots taken.

## Sun Sep 27 — the quiet one

The park recovers. **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 with migration still running, and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** goes at 2 from Mariners' Gate.

If you want something odd and cheerful on a walk, the **Greyhounds of NYC Meetup** fills the Dairy Lawn 10 AM–1 PM.

## Steady all week

**The Great Lawn is under permit every day through September 30** — noon to 11 PM, Oval and South West Lawn. Not one field permit remains on it; everything has moved to Heckscher and North Meadow. If the Great Lawn loop road or its paths are part of your route, expect infrastructure and fencing all week, not just Saturday.

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** stays closed all day, every day, through **October 13**.

## Quick recap

- **Monday is the week** — 70°, dry, nothing booked, clear drives.
- **Saturday: Global Citizen Festival 2–10 PM on the Great Lawn.** Ride or run before noon.
- **The Great Lawn is held through Sep 30** — fencing and build-out all week.
- **Fall migration is peaking:** Birding Bob five mornings, Birding Basics Sunday 10 AM.
- **Cool and damp midweek**, low 60s with light rain — good running, bad sunbathing.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed through October 13.**

Take Monday,
