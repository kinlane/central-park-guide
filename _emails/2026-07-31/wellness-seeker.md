Hi — two things to put in the diary and one to plan around. Pilates comes to the Graywacke Arch Lawn Saturday morning, PAGNY holds a wellness morning on Great Hill Thursday, and Bethesda Terrace — the place most people go to sit and be still near water — is fenced off for the entire month of August. Wednesday, meanwhile, has nothing scheduled anywhere in the park.

## Weather this week
Hot and dry through the weekend at 86°F and 83°F, then storms Sunday night, a wet Monday at 81°F, and a humid Tuesday-to-Thursday in the mid-80s with afternoon thunderstorm chances near half. Practice at first light every day this week — the temperature and the crowds both arrive together around ten — and let Monday's rain give you the Ramble and the North Woods almost entirely to yourself.

## Fri Jul 31 — lap swim at 7, and the Terrace goes offline
The **Gottesman Pool at the Davis Center has lap swim 7 to 8:30 AM**, which is the best-kept early-morning fitness slot in the park and runs every weekday this week. Beyond that, nothing programmed. **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain closes today and stays closed through August 31** — if that's where you go to sit, the substitutes with the same quality of stillness are Wagner Cove, the Ladies' Pavilion, and the Conservatory Garden's south allée.

## Sat Aug 1, 10–11:30 AM — pop-up pilates at the Graywacke Arch Lawn
**Fia Atelier brings pilates to the Graywacke Arch Lawn, 10 to 11:30**, on the east side just below the Met — a shaded, low-traffic lawn that most people walk past. It's the only formal movement class on the calendar this week. Everything else Saturday is loud and southern: a half marathon at the north end in the morning, markets at two gates, a skate circle from 2 and a concert at Rumsey from 6. **North of the 97th Street Transverse is where the quiet is** — the Ravine, the Loch, and the Pool all stay untouched by any of it.

## Sun Aug 2 — take the west side, leave the Meer to the festival
83°F and cloudy, the more comfortable of the two weekend mornings. The north end is busy for once — Summer on the Harlem Meer runs 9 to 6 with music at 2 — and the Mall has a road race crossing it 9 to 11:30. Which leaves the **west side genuinely empty**: Summit Rock in the morning, the Pool, and the Great Hill before the afternoon heat. Storms after dark.

## Mon Aug 3 — the rain practice
81°F, showers and thunderstorms, nothing on the calendar anywhere. If you can practice outdoors in weather, this is the single quietest day of the week — the Ramble empties completely, the Gill runs, and the Hallett Sanctuary in the southeast corner will have nobody in it at all. Lap swim runs 7 to 8:30 as usual, indoors and unaffected.

## Tue Aug 4 — humid morning, loud evening
84°F with storms possible. Nothing programmed until the Bandshell fills at 7:30 for the free Naumburg concert, which is a lovely thing but does mean the Mall and the surrounding paths carry a crowd from 6:30. National Night Out takes Merchants' Gate 2 to 6. Practice north or west of 79th after mid-afternoon, or come at dawn — Summit Rock holds cool air longer than anywhere in the mid-park.

## Wed Aug 5 — nothing scheduled, park-wide
84°F. **Not one event on the calendar anywhere in Central Park today** — no permits worth naming, no concerts, no closures, no crowds. This is the day to take the long slow morning you've been meaning to: Conservatory Garden at opening, the north end after, and back down through the Ramble.

## Thu Aug 6, 9–11 AM — PAGNY Wellness Day on Great Hill
**A wellness morning on the Great Hill Oval, 9 to 11** — the north end's best open lawn, and a rare piece of programming up there. Come for it or come around it; either way the Great Hill is the place to be Thursday morning. The evening is the opposite: Rumsey has the week's biggest concert at 8, so everything south of 79th is loud and full from 6 onward. Lap swim 7 to 8:30 as usual.

## Quick recap
- **Bethesda Terrace closed all August.** Wagner Cove, the Ladies' Pavilion and the Conservatory Garden are the substitutes.
- **Sat 10–11:30: pop-up pilates on the Graywacke Arch Lawn.**
- **Thu 9–11: PAGNY Wellness Day on the Great Hill Oval.**
- **Wednesday has nothing scheduled anywhere** — the week's open day.
- **Lap swim at Gottesman Pool, 7–8:30, every weekday.**
- **Monday's rain empties the Ramble and the Hallett Sanctuary.**
- **Evening noise:** Rumsey Sat, Sun and Thu; the Bandshell Tuesday from 6:30. Stay north of 79th after 5:30 on those days.

Breathe easy this week,
The Central Park Guide
