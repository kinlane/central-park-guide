The week's structural story is a month-long one: **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain carries a closure permit that runs from today through August 31.** Thirty-two consecutive days at the single most-visited built landscape in the park, in peak season. Everything else this week is the ordinary August pattern — a heavy weekend, a wet Monday, and a mid-week trough — but that closure will shape foot traffic all month.

## Weather this week
A dry, hot weekend at 86°F and 83°F gives way to storms Sunday night, a wet Monday at 81°F, and then a humid, unsettled Tuesday-to-Thursday in the mid-80s with daily thunderstorm chances between 40 and 50 percent. No heat advisory in the forecast and no sustained extreme — this is a normal early-August pattern, not an event.

## The pattern this week
**Both weekend mornings are permitted to competition.** The NYC Summer Half Marathon and 5 Miler hold East Drive and the 102nd Street Transverse Saturday 7:30–11; CRCA's racing series holds East Drive Sunday 4–8:30 AM; the Comic Run 5K/10K goes off from Bandshell Plaza Sunday 9–11:30. Three separate permits on the drives in twenty-four hours is on the high side for a non-marathon weekend, and worth noting after a week with nothing on them at all.

**The permit load is climbing into August.** 585 events fall inside these seven days against a running collection of 2,593. Thursday alone carries 110 — 79 of them field-sports permits, with Heckscher at 28 and the Great Lawn at 26. That's the heaviest single day in the current window.

**Rumsey is under continuous permit.** The SummerStage festival permit covers Rumsey Playfield from 8 AM to midnight on Saturday, Sunday and Thursday, with a separate all-day production permit Tuesday. Four days of the seven, the playfield is not a public space.

**Landscapes offline:** Bethesda Terrace all month; East Meadow Saturday and Sunday; the East 102nd Street Landscape Tuesday; the East 72nd Street Playground and the East 106th Street Lawn on rolling maintenance from Monday. Fewer simultaneous closures than last week's four, but the Bethesda one outweighs the count.
