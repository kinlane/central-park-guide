Hi — a quiet week for programming and a good one for going out alone. The Conservancy has the Seneca Village walk Saturday morning and Iconic Views on three mornings, and Monday's storms hand you the Ramble and the Ravine with almost nobody in them. Late summer means the dawn chorus is thin but the first southbound warblers are due any week now.

## Weather this week
Dry and hot through the weekend at 86°F and 83°F, then storms Sunday night and a soaking Monday at 81°F, with Tuesday through Thursday humid in the mid-80s and afternoon thunderstorms possible each day. Go at first light every day this week — the heat and the crowds both arrive by ten — and take Monday's rain as an invitation rather than a cancellation, because the Loch and the Ravine only run properly after a soak.

## Fri Jul 31 — the Terrace goes behind fencing for the month
Nothing scheduled in the natural areas. Worth knowing before you plan any walk this month: **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is closed through August 31**, which pushes the usual crowd west toward the Lake shore and Cherry Hill — so the Ramble's southern entrances will feel slightly busier than they should for the next four weeks. Come in from Bank Rock Bridge or the Belvedere side instead.

## Sat Aug 1, 11 AM — Seneca Village Tour from Mariners' Gate
The Conservancy's **Seneca Village walk leaves Mariners' Gate at 11** — landscape archaeology as much as history, reading a nineteenth-century village out of the ground west of the Great Lawn. The **Iconic Views tour** also runs at 10 from Columbus Circle. Otherwise the day belongs to the water: Conservatory Water has model yachts from 9, the Lake will be busy from mid-morning, and the Hallett Nature Sanctuary is your reliable escape from all of it in the southeast corner.

## Sun Aug 2 — the Meer, and the north end at its best
83°F and cloudy — the better of the two weekend mornings for a long walk. **Summer on the Harlem Meer runs 9 to 6 at the Dana Discovery Center**, which means the Meer's shoreline is busy but the Ravine and the North Woods immediately behind it are not. Do the loop anyway: Meer, then up the Loch, then out at Great Hill. Herons and egrets have been working the Meer's edges all summer. Storms arrive after dark.

## Mon Aug 3 — the day to be out in the rain
81°F with showers and thunderstorms through the day. **This is the week's walk.** The Ramble empties, the canopy holds the rain off the paths, the Gill runs hard, and the Loch's little waterfalls come back for a few hours. Nothing at all is scheduled anywhere in the park. Bring a jacket, skip the binoculars if it's really coming down, and go anyway.

## Tue Aug 4 — humid, and quiet everywhere green
84°F with a 50% storm chance. No nature programming. If you want stillness in the heat, Summit Rock and the Hallett Sanctuary both hold cool air longer than anywhere in the mid-park, and the Conservatory Garden's north end is in full late-summer flower — the perennial beds peak right about now.

## Wed Aug 5 — nothing scheduled, anywhere
84°F. The single emptiest day on the calendar this week: no permits, no tours, no events of any kind. Turtle Pond in the early morning, then the Ramble, then out through the North Woods if you have the time. This is the day to go looking for the first migrants — early August is when the first waterthrushes and warblers start moving through.

## Thu Aug 6 — the last quiet morning before the weekend builds
86°F with storms possible. The **Iconic Views tour** runs from Columbus Circle at 10. Everything else on the calendar is concentrated in the evening at Rumsey, which means the natural areas stay quiet all day — but the mid-park paths south of 79th fill from 6 PM onward as the crowd walks in, so plan your exit north or west.

## Quick recap
- **Seneca Village walk Saturday 11 AM from Mariners' Gate.** Iconic Views at 10 Fri, Sat and Thu.
- **Monday's rain is the week's best walk** — the Ramble, the Gill, the Loch, and nobody else.
- **Wednesday has nothing scheduled anywhere in the park.** Go looking for early migrants.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed all August** pushes crowds toward the Lake shore; enter the Ramble from the north.
- **Conservatory Garden's perennial beds are at peak.** Early morning, before the heat.
- **The Meer is busy Sunday 9–6** — the Ravine and North Woods behind it are not.
