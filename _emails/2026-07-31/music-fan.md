Hi music fans — three free nights worth clearing the calendar for, and they're about as far apart stylistically as the park gets: a Latin triple bill at Rumsey Saturday, the Naumburg season's baroque program at the Bandshell Tuesday, and Andrew Bird playing *The Mysterious Production of Eggs* with the Wordless Music Orchestra on Thursday. All free.

## Weather this week
Saturday and Sunday are dry and warm, 86°F and 83°F, and then the pattern turns humid and unsettled — Monday wet, Tuesday through Thursday in the mid-80s with afternoon and evening thunderstorm chances between 40 and 50 percent. Only Tuesday's Naumburg concert and Thursday's Rumsey show carry real weather risk; check before you leave, and if the Bandshell gets rained out the Naumburg series traditionally announces the call late in the afternoon.

## Sat Aug 1, 6 PM — Trueno / Milo J / J Noa at Rumsey Playfield
A trio of rising Latin artists, free, gates from 6. Trueno and Milo J are both out of the Argentine hip-hop scene and J Noa is the Dominican teenager everyone has been calling the future of the genre — this is the kind of bill SummerStage books before the room gets too big for it. Rumsey is under festival permit from 8 AM, so the plaza is closed off all day. RSVP doesn't guarantee entry; get there early or take the Member-for-a-Day line. Elsewhere in the park that afternoon: **That Guitar Man at Hernshead, noon to 6**, and **tango at the Shakespeare statue, 6 to 9** — an easy pairing if you want music without a queue.

## Sun Aug 2 — the Meer at 2, K-pop at Rumsey at 7
Two very different afternoons. Up north, the **Harlem Meer Performance Festival brings Kobie Dozier to the Dana Discovery Center at 2 PM** — jazz, soul and hip-hop, free, and the best-sounding small stage in the park. Then at Rumsey, **WayV with THE EITHER and Chopstix at 7 PM**, programmed with China Institute for its centennial: two takes on the future of Chinese pop, with WayV coming out of the NCT ecosystem. Expect the biggest, youngest queue of the weekend at 72nd Street. Mid-park, the **International Folkdancers hold King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM to 8 PM**, live and participatory.

## Mon Aug 3 — nothing on, and rain anyway
81°F, showers and thunderstorms. No music permitted anywhere in the park. The Arsenal's free photography show — forty years of SummerStage through Jack Vartoogian's lens — is open 9 to 5 and is the right rainy-day substitute for a concert.

## Tue Aug 4, 7:30 PM — Nosky's Baroque Band, Naumburg Bandshell
**The 121st season of Naumburg Orchestral Concerts continues with Nosky's Baroque Band, free, 7:30 to 9 at the Bandshell.** Aisslinn Nosky's group plays the period repertoire with more attack than most, which suits an outdoor room — the Bandshell's acoustics reward ensembles that push. This is the oldest free outdoor classical series in the United States, running since 1905, and it stays criminally under-attended for what it is. Arrive by 6:30 for a seat inside the plaza. Rumsey is under an all-day permit for a separate concert production.

## Wed Aug 5 — dark night across the park
Nothing programmed at Rumsey, the Bandshell, the Meer or anywhere else. A rare fully silent Wednesday in the middle of festival season.

## Thu Aug 6, 8 PM — Andrew Bird and the Wordless Music Orchestra at Rumsey
**The show of the week, and free.** Bird plays *The Mysterious Production of Eggs* in full for its 20th anniversary, with the Wordless Music Orchestra behind him — the record that turned a violinist-whistler with a loop pedal into a songwriter people take seriously, orchestrated. Surma opens. Expect the longest line of the week at the 72nd Street entrance from 6. Earlier the same day, the Arsenal hosts **Foundational Voices: The People Who Built SummerStage, 4:30 to 7, free** — an oral history of the festival from the people who made it, and a genuinely good hour if you care how this programming came to exist.

## Quick recap
- **Sat 6 PM, Rumsey:** Trueno / Milo J / J Noa. Free, Latin, gates at 6.
- **Sun 2 PM, Dana Discovery Center:** Kobie Dozier, free, Harlem Meer Performance Festival.
- **Sun 7 PM, Rumsey:** WayV / THE EITHER / Chopstix, free, China Institute centennial.
- **Tue 7:30 PM, Bandshell:** Nosky's Baroque Band — Naumburg, free, 121st season.
- **Thu 8 PM, Rumsey:** Andrew Bird with the Wordless Music Orchestra, *Eggs* at 20. Free, and the one to arrive early for.
- **Thu 4:30 PM, the Arsenal:** Foundational Voices, on how SummerStage got built.
- **Wednesday is dark everywhere.**
