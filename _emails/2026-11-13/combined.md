Hi,

**Saturday morning the loop is not yours.** The Walk to End Alzheimer's holds Bandshell Plaza, the Lower Loop, the Center Loop and Dead Road North from **8 AM to 1 PM** — a permit that was not on file when I wrote to you last Sunday. If you run, ride or walk the drives on a Saturday morning, that is the one thing to know this week.

And then I have to take back last week's forecast. I told you the mild autumn ended hard on Friday. It ended for about a day. **Every day from here to Friday is dry and warmer than the one before it, finishing at 64°.**

## Weather this week

Seven consecutive dry days — rain never gets above 2% — climbing steadily from 46° today to 50°, 51°, 56°, 61°, 62° and **64° on Friday**. Saturday's 8–12 mph northwest wind is the only edge on the whole week; after that it falls away to nothing, so put the long outdoor plans anywhere you like and save Thursday and Friday for whatever you most want to do in shirtsleeves.

## Sat Nov 14, 8 AM–1 PM — Walk to End Alzheimer's closes the lower loop

**Bandshell Plaza, Lower Loop, Center Loop and Dead Road North, 8 AM to 1 PM.** The south end of the park is staging and crowds for five hours. **Run or ride before 8 or after 1**, or take the bridle path and the Reservoir and stay north of the 79th Street transverse.

I want to be straight about this one: **it was not in the city's permit feed when I built last Sunday's email**, so that edition told you Saturday was model yachts and a lantern parade. The permit appeared in this week's pull. Nothing was tagged wrong — the filing simply landed late, and this is the shape of that problem.

The rest of the day, once the walk clears:

- **St. Martin's Parade**, 3:15–5 PM at **Summit Rock**. A lantern procession at the park's highest point, timed for dusk — the prettiest thing on the calendar this week.
- **Santa's Knights and Gladiators NYC**, noon–4 PM on the **Lawn Behind the Met**.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM. The northwest wind will push the fleet.
- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza; **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM.

## Sun Nov 15 — the Great Central Park Hike, on a 50° sunny day

- **[Great Central Park Hike](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/great-central-park-hike-tour)**, 10 AM from the Dana Discovery Center. End to end with Conservancy guides, and a clear 50° Sunday is close to the ideal day for it.
- **World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims**, noon–2 PM, also at Dana Discovery Center Plaza.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM — their standing Sunday.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM. **The drives are clear all day.**

## Mon Nov 16 — a high school jazz band at the Bandshell

**Carmel High School Jazz Band**, 10:30–11 AM at Bandshell Plaza. Half an hour, free, and the kind of thing that only works if someone tells you it is happening.

51° and sunny with a 6 mph wind. **Birding Bob at Strawberry Fields**, 8:30 AM. Lawn maintenance begins at the East 106th Street Lawn and runs through Thursday.

## Tue Nov 17 — nothing booked, 56° and sunny

Not a single one-off permit in the park. The warmest day yet, no wind to speak of, and nothing scheduled anywhere. **If you have one free weekday, this is it.**

## Wed Nov 18, 7 PM — the Conservancy gala takes Rumsey

**The Central Park Conservancy's Annual Gala, "Studio 72," 7 PM at Rumsey Playfield.** A ticketed fundraiser, not a public event, but it is the night the permit has been building toward — the SummerStage footprint has been held since Nov 13 and stays held through Nov 21.

**A correction.** Last Sunday I told you this permit "keeps growing," from one day to two to three. **That was wrong, and the mistake was mine, not the city's.** The permit has read **November 13–21 — nine days — unchanged since at least October 30.** What changed each week was my seven-day window sliding across it: one gala day fell inside the Nov 7–13 window, three inside Nov 9–15, and I reported those slices as if they were the whole permit. I then built a small theory on top of it about permits expanding after filing. The theory may still hold elsewhere; this was not evidence for it. I have changed how I read permit spans.

61° and sunny by day.

## Thu Nov 19 — Love at First Spin, and a second Bethesda permit opens

- **Love at First Spin: Music Discovery in the Park**, noon–4 PM at Bandshell Plaza. Records in the open air on a 62° afternoon.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 8:30 AM.
- A **second closure permit at Bethesda Terrace** starts today, running alongside the one already in force. Both end December 13.

## Fri Nov 20 — 64°, and two more lawns go down

The warmest day of the week and the last of the dry run.

- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 2 PM.
- **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden**, 8:30 AM — his best stop, on the best morning.
- **The Swedish Cottage Lawn and the East 96th Street Playground Lawn both close today**, through December 13.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace is closed every day of this week** and every day through **December 13** — the same permit, extended again. It was December 8 a week ago and December 6 the week before that.
- **East Meadow is closed Saturday and Sunday**, and every weekend after this one through December 13.
- **The gala permit holds Rumsey Playfield and Bandshell Plaza from today through November 21** — nine days for one evening.
- **39 field permits this week**, 27 of them at North Meadow. Frisbee at North Meadow both weekend mornings.
- **Coming next Sunday, November 22: Race to Deliver 4M**, 8–10 AM on the drives. A real loop closure, the first since the marathon.
