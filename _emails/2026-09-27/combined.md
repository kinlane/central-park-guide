*Friday's email ran through the 2nd, so the front half will be familiar — the new material is next weekend.*

One wet Monday, and then the park gets its best run of weather since August: **five consecutive clear days, topping out at 80° on Thursday.** SummerStage closes for the year that same evening, and next Saturday the Conservancy takes families into the North Woods for City of Forest Day.

## Weather this week

Monday is 65° with an 80% chance of light rain — get it over with. Then Tuesday 71°, Wednesday 74°, Thursday **80°** and Friday 78°, all mostly sunny with a 0–11% rain chance. Saturday eases to 71° with a 21% chance. Thursday is very likely the last 80° day of the year.

## Mon Sep 28 — the last wet day

80% light rain at 65°, and one thing booked: **New York Rock'n'roll** at Bandshell Plaza 5–7 PM. Otherwise the park is empty and damp.

## Tue Sep 29 – Wed Sep 30 — it clears, and the Great Lawn comes back

Tuesday: 71°, mostly sunny, no rain at all. **Busking at the Bandshell** 1–3 PM. Bowling Green closes all day for maintenance.

Wednesday: 74° and sunny, with three evening gatherings — a **25th Anniversary** at Hernshead 5–7 PM, **Serenade** at Bandshell Plaza 6–7 PM, and a **Starlight Dinner** at Minton Arcade 6–9 PM. Wednesday is also the last day of the Global Citizen permit: **the Great Lawn is released on October 1** after twelve days held.

## Thu Oct 1 — 80°, and Charley Crockett closes the season

**[Charley Crockett](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/charley-crockett/)** plays Rumsey Playfield 7–10 PM with **Durand Jones and the Indications**. This is the **last SummerStage show of 2026** — the series goes dark until May 2027. An 80° October evening is a good way to end six months of programming.

That morning, a **Central Park Conservancy Birding Event** meets at the Swedish Cottage 9–11 AM. Fall migration is still running.

## Fri Oct 2 — free music, four hours of it

**Free Community Music Live** takes Bandshell Plaza 3–7 PM on a 78° afternoon. **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10, the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2.

## Sat Oct 3 — City of Forest Day in the North Woods

**Wonder in the Woods** is the Conservancy's City of Forest Day: a family morning of discovery in the **North Woods**, meeting at Gottesman Pool at the Davis Center from 10 AM, with a second session at Arsenal Plaza 11 AM–2 PM. The North Woods in early October is the best argument the park makes for itself.

Also on, 71° and mostly dry:

- **Kingman Classic Singles Tournament** on Bowling Green, 10 AM–4 PM — lawn bowls, and the second tournament there in a fortnight.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.

East Meadow is closed, and **38 private ceremony bookings** land Saturday — Cop Cot, Ladies' Pavilion, Wagner Cove and Dene Lawn are all spoken for, and there are ceremonies on Bow Bridge and Gapstow Bridge late in the day.

## Sun Oct 4 — bagels, yoga, and folkdancers

A gentle one. **Bagels in the Park** at the East 72nd Street Playground 9–11 AM. **Yoga at Dalehead Arch** 10–11:30. **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere Castle at 10, the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2, **Vision Boarding** at Cherry Hill 1–3 PM, and the **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11–6.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day, and the permits now run to **October 27**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Strawberry Fields.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday; Columbus Circle Food Market Thursday through Sunday.
- **211 field permits** — 91 at Heckscher, 89 at North Meadow, 21 on the tennis courts. **Still none on the Great Lawn**, and none booked there for the rest of the season.

## Quick recap

- **Five clear days, Tuesday to Saturday**, peaking at **80° Thursday** — probably the year's last.
- **Charley Crockett closes SummerStage Thursday, 7 PM.** Dark until May 2027.
- **City of Forest Day Saturday** — Wonder in the Woods, North Woods, from 10 AM.
- **Free Community Music at the Bandshell Friday**, 3–7 PM, four hours.
- **The Great Lawn is released October 1** after twelve days under the festival permit.
- **Bethesda Terrace now closed through October 27.**

Have a good week in the park,
