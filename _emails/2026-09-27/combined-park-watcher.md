*Friday's digest ran through the 2nd; the front half will be familiar. Three findings are new.*

The Great Lawn question has an answer, and it is bigger than the festival. The lawn is released from the Global Citizen permit on October 1 — but **it carries no field permits in October, November, or anywhere else in the forward calendar.** It is off the league rotation for the rest of the season, not fenced for a fortnight. Bethesda has extended again. And the digest's own guard caught two events that were never in this park at all.

## Weather this week

One wet Monday at 65° and 80% rain, then five clear days: 71°, 74°, **80°**, 78°, 71°. Thursday is very likely the year's last 80°. Dry weather across a festival load-out is better news for the turf than last week's forecast suggested.

## The Great Lawn is out for the season

Field permits by venue, seven weeks:

| Venue | Sep 5–11 | Sep 12–18 | Sep 19–25 | Sep 21–27 | Sep 28–Oct 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heckscher Fields | 24 | ~60 | 103 | 111 | **91** |
| North Meadow | 5 | 24 | 75 | 83 | **89** |
| Tennis Courts | 3 | 4 | 21 | 21 | **21** |
| Great Lawn | ~70 | ~80 | 14 | 0 | **0** |

And looking past this window: **October holds 294 permits at North Meadow, 151 at Heckscher, 70 on the tennis courts — and zero on the Great Lawn.** November too.

So the festival was the occasion, not the whole cause. A twelve-day hold does not explain an empty November. The lawn has been taken off the league calendar for the remainder of the season.

**It is not closed, though, and that distinction matters.** Six non-league bookings sit on the Great Lawn in October — picnics and celebrations, including two on October 3. The public can use it; organised field sport cannot. That is the signature of turf recovery rather than construction, and it is consistent with a lawn that carried seventy softball permits a week through late summer and then an eleven-day festival build.

Still, and I will keep saying this until it changes: **there is no Great Lawn closure or maintenance permit anywhere in the record.** The absence has now persisted six weeks across every reading.

## Tennis: the pattern is real

Court permits went 3, then 4, then 21, and October holds 70. Two weeks ago I called the jump unverified and declined to name it a pattern. Four readings now agree, and the October figure settles it: this is the autumn tennis season, not a block booking.

## Bethesda Terrace extends a second time

The permits now run to **October 27**. They read October 13 two weeks ago and October 25 yesterday. Two extensions inside two weeks.

I have stopped calling the end date settled, which was the right correction to make. What it is worth noting now is the *shape*: the extensions are small and frequent rather than one large revision, which reads like work being re-scoped weekly rather than a single schedule slipping. Six weeks elapsed, a month still on the board.

## Two events that were never in this park

The digest builder flagged a booking whose location reads **"Astoria Park: Great Lawn 01A North Side of Playground."** Chasing it turned up two files — October 3 and October 10 — both Astoria Park in Queens, both stamped `event_borough: Manhattan`, both carrying `place: Great Lawn`, and both with body text asserting the event takes place "in Central Park." They had been published on the site as Central Park events since a **May 2** pull.

They survived every purge for a reason worth knowing: they are future-dated, so the past-event sweep never reached them, and they are absent from the current API, so the merge only ever cleans their text and never deletes them. **A bad record that falls out of the feed becomes permanent.** Both are now removed, and they were inflating the Great Lawn booking count I quote above — the real figure is six, not eight.

## Thu Oct 1 — SummerStage closes

**Charley Crockett with Durand Jones and the Indications**, Rumsey 7–10 PM, is the last show of 2026. Dark until May 2027. The Rumsey permit switches to "October" for that single date and stops.

**The Bandshell has taken over the music calendar** — five bookings there this week against Rumsey's one. Busking Tuesday, Serenade Wednesday, free community music Friday for four hours, rock'n'roll Monday. That is the venue to watch through the autumn.

## Quick recap

- **Bethesda extended a second time**, to October 27. Small frequent extensions, not one slip.
