A weekend with something on nearly every lawn, then a week that empties out. Barefoot Shakespeare plays Summit Rock both weekend afternoons, the folkdancers hold King Jagiello on Sunday, and Labor Day Monday arrives sunny and 78° with almost nothing permitted anywhere — a rare thing in this park. Thursday brings the one unmissable indoor event: New Dendrology opens at the Arsenal Gallery.

## Weather this week

Sunny and 81° Saturday, a bright and cooler 75° Sunday, a sunny 78° Labor Day, then heat rebuilding into the low 80s midweek and 85° Thursday with showers most likely Wednesday night. The outdoor performances all fall in the good half of the week; by Thursday the gallery opening is the smart place to be.

## Sat Sep 5 — the fullest day of the week

**Barefoot Shakespeare** takes Summit Rock 3–8 PM. (Their permit title still reads "August 2026" — the title is stale, the date is right.) **Tango at the Shakespeare Statue** follows 6–9 PM, which is the loveliest thing on the Mall this weekend.

For families and the earlier crowd:

- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM, on Conservatory Water.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 110th and Lenox, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)**, 10 AM from Boys' Gate — the butterfly garden, near the end of its season.
- **[Iconic Views Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.

There is an all-day SummerStage permit at Rumsey Playfield with **no announced lineup** — the same hold we flagged last week. The season's next real show is [Marcus King Band on Sep 12](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/marcus-king-band/). East Meadow is closed all day.

## Sun Sep 6 — folkdancers, birding, and a rally at the Bandshell

The **International Folkdancers** hold King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM — the best free spectacle of the weekend and one you can happen upon rather than plan. **Barefoot Shakespeare** repeats at Summit Rock 3–8 PM.

**[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 AM; fall migration is why you'd take it now. **5 Element Meditation** opens the day at Cherry Hill, 8–9 AM.

Bandshell Plaza carries **End Insurance Apartheid**, formerly Rap Against Rikers, noon–6 PM — live music with a cause behind it, and the day's crowd. East Meadow is closed a second day.

## Mon Sep 7 — Labor Day, and the emptiest calendar in months

Sunny, 78°, and just two permits in the whole park: **Central Park Skate Circle** at Dead Road North 2–7 PM, and the **Public Works Picnic rain date** at the Delacorte 5–6 PM. The skate circle on a perfect holiday afternoon is the reliable free show.

## Tue Sep 8 – Wed Sep 9 — picnic, public art, evening basketball

The **Public Works Picnic** takes the Delacorte Tuesday 5–6 PM. Wednesday brings a **Public Art Fund opening** at Doris C. Freedman Plaza, 6–7 PM, and basketball at James Weldon Johnson Playground 6:30–10:30 PM. Cedar Hill closes all day Wednesday and stays out through Friday.

## Thu Sep 10, 6–8 PM — New Dendrology opens at the Arsenal Gallery

The **[opening reception](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/10/opening-reception-new-dendrology-at-the-arsenal-gallery)** for New Dendrology — a show about trees, at the Arsenal, free — is the week's headline for four of your interests at once. It's indoors on the hottest evening of the week at 85°, which is the other reason to go Thursday. The gallery is open Friday 9 AM–5 PM if the reception doesn't suit.

## Fri Sep 11 — remembrance at East Meadow

The **WTC Mental Health 9/11 National Day of Remembrance Gathering** is at East Meadow, 11 AM–1:30 PM. Bowling Green is closed for maintenance until 2 PM.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** sits under an all-day permit titled only "Closed," every day Saturday through Friday. No reason is given anywhere in the record. Worth knowing before you route a walk through it.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse, 7:30 and 8:30.
- **Columbus Circle Food Market** at Merchants' Gate, 9:30 AM–9 PM.
- **CP Sports Summer Program** at South Dead Road, Tuesday through Friday, morning and afternoon sessions.
- **105 field permits** across the week — about seventy on the Great Lawn, 24 at Heckscher, 5 at North Meadow, 3 on the tennis courts. An ordinary post-Labor-Day load.

## Quick recap

- **Barefoot Shakespeare at Summit Rock**, Saturday and Sunday 3–8 PM.
- **Folkdancers at King Jagiello**, Sunday 11 AM–6 PM; tango at the Shakespeare Statue Saturday 6 PM.
- **Labor Day Monday:** sunny 78°, two permits in the park, skate circle 2–7 PM.
- **New Dendrology opens Thursday 6 PM** at the Arsenal — free, indoors, about trees.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed all seven days** under an unexplained permit.
- **Saturday's SummerStage permit still has no show behind it;** the season resumes Sep 12.

Enjoy the turn toward fall,
