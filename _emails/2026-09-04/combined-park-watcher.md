Last week's read was that the summer permit season had ended and the maintenance calendar had taken over. This week confirms it and adds a wrinkle. The drives stay clear for a second consecutive week — fourteen days with no race, no charity walk and no permit on any drive, the longest open stretch since June — while a permit titled only "Closed" holds Bethesda Terrace and Fountain every day, Saturday through Friday. That is the item to watch.

## Weather this week

Sunny and 81° Saturday, 75° Sunday, a sunny 78° on Labor Day, then heat rebuilding to 83° midweek and 85° Thursday with the best rain chance Wednesday night. Dry, mild weather through the holiday is what lets a restoration schedule run on time — expect this week's lawn closures to hold their stated end dates rather than extend.

## The Bethesda anomaly

Seven separate all-day permits, one per day, Saturday through Friday, each titled nothing but **"Closed"** and each running 12:00 AM to 11:55 PM at Bethesda Fountain Terrace. The permit record gives no organizer, no reason and no event. Last week the same site carried seven closure permits and we read them alongside the weekend's private-booking surge; a second full week makes that reading harder to sustain.

Two possibilities fit the data: sustained work on the Terrace or the arcade, or an administrative block held for something not yet announced. Nothing in the Conservancy or Parks feeds names either. **This is the week's genuine unknown**, and worth checking on the ground if you're near the 72nd Street cross drive.

## Sat Sep 5 — a second unannounced SummerStage permit

The all-day SummerStage permit at Rumsey Playfield is back, and there is **still no lineup in the City Parks Foundation feed** — as flagged in last week's digest for the same date. A second one appears Thursday Sep 10, also empty. The season's next announced show remains [Marcus King Band on Sep 12](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/marcus-king-band/), followed by Young Thug the 13th and Danny Elfman the 15th. Two permits held in a week when the venue announces nothing is the pattern to note: the site is being reserved ahead of a run, not programmed.

Otherwise Saturday is the civilian peak: model yacht racing at Kerbs 9 AM–3 PM, Unity Markets at Farmers' Gate 9 AM–7 PM, the Conservancy's pollinator walk at 10, Barefoot Shakespeare at Summit Rock 3–8 PM, tango at the Shakespeare Statue 6–9 PM. East Meadow closed all day.

## Sun Sep 6 — the Bandshell's political booking

**End Insurance Apartheid**, the event formerly known as Rap Against Rikers, takes Bandshell Plaza noon–6 PM. International Folkdancers hold King Jagiello 11 AM–6 PM. East Meadow closed a second day.

## Mon Sep 7 — Labor Day, and a nearly empty board

Two permits in the entire park: the Skate Circle at Dead Road North 2–7 PM and the Public Works Picnic rain date at the Delacorte. **Two private bookings all day**, against seventeen on Saturday. A sunny 78° holiday with an empty calendar is not a normal Labor Day for this park — the programming has genuinely stopped, not merely thinned.

## Mid-to-late week — the restoration block, smaller than last week's

Four lawn closures, down from six:

- **East Meadow** — Saturday and Sunday, all day.
- **East 106th Street lawn** — Monday through Thursday.
- **Cedar Hill** — Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, all day.
- **Bowling Green** — Friday, until 2 PM.

The East Side concentration from last week persists, and Cedar Hill entering the rotation is new. None of it touches a drive or a major path.

Thursday brings the week's one institutional event: the **[New Dendrology opening reception](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/10/opening-reception-new-dendrology-at-the-arsenal-gallery)** at the Arsenal Gallery, 6–8 PM, open to the public Friday 9 AM–5 PM.

## Fri Sep 11 — remembrance at East Meadow

The **WTC Mental Health 9/11 National Day of Remembrance Gathering** holds East Meadow 11 AM–1:30 PM — the annual gathering, back on the calendar.

## The permit load, in aggregate

**105 field permits** across the week: about seventy on the Great Lawn, 24 at Heckscher, 5 at North Meadow, 3 on the tennis courts. That is down from 133 last week, and the drop is concentrated at North Meadow, which fell from 23 to 5. The leagues are finally winding down, a week behind the event calendar.

**66 private bookings**, against 50 last week — the early-September ceremony season is still climbing. Saturday carries 17, Sunday and Wednesday 12 each, Thursday 11. Ladies' Pavilion leads at 12, the Great Lawn at 10, Cop Cot at 9, Wagner Cove at 7. Midweek bookings running as heavy as weekend ones is the unusual detail; that is not the shape of a normal September.

## Quick recap

- **Fourteen straight days with nothing on the drives** — the longest clear stretch since June.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain closed all seven days** under permits titled only "Closed," reason unrecorded. The week's open question.
- **Two more SummerStage permits with no announced show** (Saturday and Thursday); the season resumes Sep 12.
- **Labor Day is nearly empty:** two permits, two private bookings, sunny and 78°.
- **Field permits down to 105 from 133,** the drop almost entirely at North Meadow.
- **Private bookings up to 66 from 50,** and running heavy midweek, not just on the weekend.
