Two days of closures, then the park hands itself back under some of the best November weather you will see. **Sunday is 61° and dry for the marathon** — a correction to what I told you last week — and from Monday the drives are open into a stretch that reaches **70°**.

## Weather this week — and a correction

Today 58° and sunny. **Sunday 61°, mostly sunny, 0% rain.** Then 66°, 67°, **70°**, 71° with showers Thursday, and storms likely Friday.

Last week I warned that 69° was warm for a marathon and said to drink earlier than felt necessary. **The forecast has come down to 61° and dry.** That is close to ideal racing weather and the warning no longer applies — if you are running Sunday, this is the day you would have ordered.

## Sat Oct 31 — no road miles before 11

**[Abbott Dash to the Finish Line 5K](https://centralpark.guide/events/abbott-dash-to-the-finish-line-5k-2026-10-31/)**, 8:45–11 AM on the Ted Corbitt Loop, finish holding Grand Army Plaza from 8 AM.

Bridle path or the Reservoir if you need to move before 11. After that the loop is clear and it is 58° and sunny.

On foot: the **[North Woods woodland walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** at 10 and the **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 are both well clear of the course.

## Sun Nov 1 — the loop is gone 8 AM to 5 PM

**55,000 runners under a clear 61° sky.**

If you are racing: it is the forecast you wanted. If you are not, the loop is unavailable for nine hours and the Reservoir will be thick with spectators.

**Go north.** The Ravine, the Loch and the North Woods are nowhere near the course, and marathon Sunday is one of the quietest mornings of the year up there — the whole city's attention is three miles south of you.

## Mon Nov 2 – Wed Nov 4 — the drives are back, and it is 70°

**The roads reopen Monday.** The marathon's build-out permit runs on paper to November 10, so expect trailers and fencing coming down across the west side through the week — but that is strike work, not closure. **Nothing blocks a lap from Monday.**

The weather is the story: 66°, 67°, then **70° on Wednesday**, all dry. For November that is extraordinary, and the public calendar is effectively empty — a sports mini-camp Tuesday morning, a kids' soccer session Wednesday afternoon.

**Monday to Wednesday is the best riding and running left this year.** Take all three if you can.

## Thu Nov 5 – Fri Nov 6 — warm and wet

Thursday **71°** with 49% showers; Friday 69° with thunderstorms likely at 58%. The warmest days are the wettest ones.

Friday morning before it breaks: **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM from Belvedere Castle.

**[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** rotating: Loeb Boathouse this weekend and Thursday, Strawberry Fields Monday, **Conservatory Garden** Friday.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is closed all day, every day, and the permits have extended an eleventh time — now **November 30**. That is the entire month, ten weeks in, and still no stated reason anywhere.

**Field permits are down to 66 for the week**, 61 of them at North Meadow. The league season is ending, which means the lawns get quieter from here.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday is 61° and dry** — last week's 69° warning is withdrawn; this is ideal.
- **Today: no road miles before 11.** Dash 5K on the loop.
- **Sunday: loop gone 8 AM–5 PM.** Go north — the Ravine and Loch are empty.
- **Monday the drives reopen** into 66°, 67°, 70°. Best three days left this year.
- **Thursday and Friday: warm and wet**, storms likely Friday.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed through November 30.**

Enjoy the race, then take Monday,
Central Park Guide
