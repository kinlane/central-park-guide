Marathon weekend, and the forecast has turned generous. **Sunday is 61° and mostly sunny with no rain at all** — near-ideal racing weather, and a correction to what I told you last week. Then the park gets an extraordinary stretch for November: **66°, 67°, 70°, 71°**, before rain arrives Thursday.

## Weather this week — and a correction

Today 58° and sunny. **Sunday 61°, mostly sunny, 0% rain.** Monday 66°, Tuesday 67°, Wednesday 70°, Thursday 71° with showers at 49%, Friday 69° with storms likely.

Last week I said 69° would be warm for a marathon and to plan hydration accordingly. **The forecast has come down to 61° and dry, which is close to perfect** — if you are racing Sunday, that advice no longer applies. 61° is what runners hope for.

## Sat Oct 31 — Dash day

**[Abbott Dash to the Finish Line 5K](https://centralpark.guide/events/abbott-dash-to-the-finish-line-5k-2026-10-31/)** runs the Ted Corbitt Loop 8:45–11 AM, with the finish holding Grand Army Plaza from 8 AM. **The loop is out all morning.**

After it clears, 58° and sunny with everything else well away from the course:

- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center.
- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 AM.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 PM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7.

It is also Halloween, and the park has no Halloween event on its calendar at all. East Meadow closed; 20 private bookings.

## Sun Nov 1, 8 AM–5 PM — the TCS New York City Marathon

**55,000 runners, finishing in Central Park, under a clear 61° sky.** The biggest day of the park's year, with the best weather it could have asked for.

**The loop is gone 8 AM to 5 PM.** Two ways to spend it:

- **Watch.** The finish area around Tavern on the Green, or the last mile up East Drive. Late afternoon, when the slower runners come through, is the part worth staying for.
- **Avoid it entirely.** Go north — the Ravine, the Loch and the North Woods are nowhere near the course and are among the quietest places in the city on marathon Sunday.

The **International Folkdancers** hold King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–3 PM, shortened from their usual six hours. East Meadow closed.

## Mon Nov 2 – Wed Nov 4 — the park comes back, and it is 70°

**The race is over and the drives reopen.** The marathon's build-out permit technically runs to November 10, so there will be trailers and fencing coming down across the west side all week — but **the roads are yours again from Monday.**

And the weather is absurd for November: 66°, 67°, then **70° on Wednesday**. Almost nothing is booked. A CP Sports mini-camp on South Dead Road Tuesday morning, Little Warrior Soccer at North Reservoir Wednesday afternoon, and that is the entire public calendar.

**Monday through Wednesday is the best riding and walking of the month**, and it will not repeat.

## Thu Nov 5 – Fri Nov 6 — warm, then wet

Thursday is **71°** with a 49% shower chance; Friday 69° with thunderstorms likely at 58%. The warmest days of the week are also the wettest.

Friday morning, before the weather turns: **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM from Belvedere Castle, and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 30**, an eleventh extension. That is the whole of November.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free; **Wreath Interpretations Open Call** at the Arsenal.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** rotating: Loeb Boathouse this weekend and Thursday, Strawberry Fields Monday, **Conservatory Garden** Friday.
- **Field permits have dropped to 66 for the week**, 61 of them at North Meadow. The league season is winding down.
- **No play on the Great Lawn** for a fourteenth week.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday is 61° and dry for the marathon** — I said 69° last week; the forecast improved and it is now near-perfect.
- **Today: Dash 5K, loop out 8–11 AM.**
- **Sunday: the marathon, 8 AM–5 PM.** Watch at Tavern on the Green, or go north for quiet.
- **Monday the drives reopen**, and Monday–Wednesday hits 70° — the best stretch left this year.
- **Thursday and Friday are warm and wet**, storms likely Friday.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed through November 30.**
