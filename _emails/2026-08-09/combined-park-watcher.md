A quiet operational week followed by a heavy weekend. Monday through Thursday the park is essentially running on maintenance alone; Friday through Sunday it absorbs a training race, a bike race, a car-free street event and two nights of SummerStage.

## Weather this week

Monday peaks at 92° with evening thunderstorms, then upper 80s and humid through Thursday before a clean break Friday into a mid-80s weekend with mid-60s nights. The heat sits squarely on the four maintenance days, which is favorable timing — the turf work lands midweek and the crowds arrive after the weather turns.

## Mon Aug 10 – Thu Aug 13 — the turf rotation

Four consecutive days of lawn work and nothing else on the calendar. Monday and Wednesday carry no one-off permits at all.

- **Tue Aug 11:** Bowling Green closed all day; East Meadow closed to 2 PM; the Pond lawns closed to 2 PM
- **Thu Aug 13:** Billy Johnson lawn closed to 2 PM
- **Mon–Thu:** lawn maintenance at the East 106th Street lawn, all four days

Worth noting the pattern: Bowling Green appears twice this week, closed all day Tuesday and again to 2 PM Friday. That's a lawn getting real attention rather than a routine pass.

## Fri Aug 14 — the weekend load-in starts

Good Morning America occupies Rumsey Playfield until noon, which is a broadcast setup rather than a public event — expect trucks and cable on the Bandshell Plaza side through the morning. [Free Community Music Live](https://centralpark.guide/events/free-community-music-live-2026-08-14/) follows at Bandshell Plaza 3–7 PM. The Urban Park Rangers run family camping overnight at the Great Hill, entering at Strangers' Gate.

The Columbus Circle food market runs Thursday through Saturday at Merchants' Gate, 9:30 AM–9 PM.

## Sat Aug 15 — a training race on the drives and a car-free 110th

Two significant footprints on the same morning:

- **[TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 12M](https://centralpark.guide/events/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-12m-2026-08-15/)**, 7:00–10:30 AM. Two loops of the park drives at marathon pace, with Race-Day Central staged behind Ballfield #8 near East Drive and 102nd from roughly 5:30 AM. The drives stay open to the public, but the loop carries race volume for three and a half hours.
- **[Summer Streets Week 3](https://centralpark.guide/events/summer-streets-central-park-week-3-2026-08-15/)**, 7 AM–3 PM on 110th between Frederick Douglass Circle and Adam Clayton Powell. Third consecutive Saturday for the northern edge.

Add the SummerStage August festival permit at Rumsey running 8 AM to end of day, with [Blues Traveler, Gin Blossoms and Spin Doctors](https://centralpark.guide/events/blues-traveler-gin-blossoms-spin-doctors-2026-08-15/) at 6 PM, and **the East Meadow closed all day** for turf restoration. The Meer end picks up [Unitymarketsny](https://centralpark.guide/events/unitymarketsny-2026-08-15/) at 110th and Lenox, 9 AM–7 PM.

Smaller items in aggregate: model yacht racing at Kerbs, storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue, Tot Shabbat at Levin Playground, the skate circle at Dead Road, and a handful of private lawn bookings at the Pond.

## Sun Aug 16 — CRCA at 4 AM, then a full afternoon

The [Central Park Racing Series](https://centralpark.guide/events/crca-central-park-racing-series-2026-08-16/) has East Drive 4:00–8:30 AM. Back-to-back race mornings on the drives is the thing to watch this weekend — Saturday's training run and Sunday's crit series stack against each other with no recovery day between.

The East Meadow remains closed. SummerStage runs again all day with [Taiwanese Waves](https://centralpark.guide/events/taiwanese-waves-10th-anniversary-9m88-øzi-yellow-chance-emerson-mong-tong-chinat-2026-08-16/) at 6 PM, and the Meer carries [Summer on the Harlem Meer](https://centralpark.guide/events/summer-on-the-harlem-meer-2026-08-16/) 9 AM–6 PM plus the [M.A.R. Quintet](https://centralpark.guide/events/mar-quintet-2026-08-16/) at 2 PM.

## Field permits

349 field permits across the window, concentrated as usual: 121 at North Meadow, 101 at the Great Lawn, 94 at Heckscher, 22 on the tennis courts. A normal summer load.

## Quick recap

- **Four straight maintenance days**, Mon–Thu, with no other programming Monday or Wednesday.
- **Bowling Green closes twice** — all day Tuesday, to 2 PM Friday.
- **Saturday stacks three footprints**: the marathon training run on the drives 7–10:30, Summer Streets on 110th 7–3, and SummerStage at Rumsey all day.
- **Back-to-back race mornings** on the drives, Saturday and Sunday.
- **East Meadow closed both weekend days** for turf restoration.
