The calendar this week is nearly all weekend. Monday through Thursday there is essentially nothing scheduled and the heat peaks at 92°; then Friday cools off and Friday, Saturday and Sunday each carry a full day of programming — plus two consecutive mornings where the drives belong to somebody else.

## Weather this week

Monday tops out at 92° with thunderstorms rolling through the evening, and it stays humid in the mid-to-upper 80s until a clean break arrives Friday at 83° and sunny, with nights finally dropping to the mid-60s. Train early or swim indoors Monday through Thursday, then plan on being outside for all three weekend days — Saturday and Sunday carry only a slight chance of showers.

## Mon Aug 10 – Thu Aug 13 — empty, and hot

Monday and Wednesday have nothing one-off on them whatsoever. The rest is turf work:

- **Tue Aug 11:** Bowling Green closed all day; East Meadow and the Pond lawns closed until 2 PM
- **Thu Aug 13:** Billy Johnson lawn closed until 2 PM
- **Mon–Thu:** lawn maintenance at the East 106th Street lawn

Nothing touches the drives, so the loop is entirely yours for four days — the only obstacle is the weather. Monday is the one to work around. Lap swim at the Gottesman Pool runs 7–8:30 AM weekdays if you want out of the heat, the CP Sports summer program runs all week at South Dead Road, and *40 in Focus* — Jack Vartoogian's SummerStage photography — is up at the Arsenal 9–5.

## Fri Aug 14 — the turn

The first good day in a stretch, 83° and sunny. [Free Community Music Live](https://centralpark.guide/events/free-community-music-live-2026-08-14/) takes Bandshell Plaza 3–7 PM, and Good Morning America has Rumsey tied up until noon. The [Woodland Walk in the Ramble](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-woodland-walk-the-ramble-2026-08-14/) leaves Belvedere Castle at 11 AM.

Best item of the day: the Urban Park Rangers run [overnight family camping](https://centralpark.guide/events/family-camping-with-the-urban-park-rangers-2026-08-14/) at the Great Hill, in through Strangers' Gate from 7 PM.

## Sat Aug 15 — the training run has the drives 7–10:30, and 110th goes car-free

The [TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 12M](https://centralpark.guide/events/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-12m-2026-08-15/) is the first of NYRR's two marathon build-up runs — two full loops of the drives at race pace, with Race-Day Central behind Ballfield #8 near East Drive and 102nd and staging from around 5:30 AM. The drives stay open, but they carry heavy runner volume for three and a half hours. **Go before 7 or after 11**, or use the bridle path and the Reservoir.

The escape route is at the top of the park: [Summer Streets Week 3](https://centralpark.guide/events/summer-streets-central-park-week-3-2026-08-15/) closes 110th to cars between Frederick Douglass Circle and Adam Clayton Powell, 7 AM–3 PM.

The rest of Saturday is the fullest day of the week:

- **[Chess lecture and simul with Michael Rohde](https://centralpark.guide/events/chess-lecture-simul-with-michael-rohde-2026-08-15/)**, 11 AM at the Chess and Checkers House
- **[Storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue](https://centralpark.guide/events/storytelling-in-central-park-2026-08-15/)**, 10 AM–1 PM
- **[Model yacht racing](https://centralpark.guide/events/model-yacht-racing-2026-08-15/)** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM
- **[Blues Traveler, Gin Blossoms and Spin Doctors](https://centralpark.guide/events/blues-traveler-gin-blossoms-spin-doctors-2026-08-15/)** at Rumsey, 6 PM
- **[Tango at the Shakespeare statue](https://centralpark.guide/events/tango-shakespeare-statue-2026-08-15/)**, 6–9 PM
- **[Skate circle](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-skate-circle-2026-08-15/)** at Dead Road, 2–7 PM, and [Unitymarketsny](https://centralpark.guide/events/unitymarketsny-2026-08-15/) at 110th and Lenox, 9 AM–7 PM

**The East Meadow is closed both weekend days.**

## Sun Aug 16 — CRCA at 4 AM, then the north end all afternoon

The [Central Park Racing Series](https://centralpark.guide/events/crca-central-park-racing-series-2026-08-16/) owns East Drive 4:00–8:30 AM. Two race mornings back to back is worth planning around rather than discovering — Saturday's training run and Sunday's crit series with no gap between them. After 8:30 you have the drive back, at a comfortable 82°.

Then:

- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://centralpark.guide/events/seneca-village-tour-2026-08-16/)**, 11 AM from Mariners' Gate
- **[M.A.R. Quintet](https://centralpark.guide/events/mar-quintet-2026-08-16/)**, 2 PM at the Dana Discovery Center, inside [Summer on the Harlem Meer](https://centralpark.guide/events/summer-on-the-harlem-meer-2026-08-16/) 9 AM–6 PM
- **[Taiwanese Waves 10th Anniversary](https://centralpark.guide/events/taiwanese-waves-10th-anniversary-9m88-øzi-yellow-chance-emerson-mong-tong-chinat-2026-08-16/)** at Rumsey, 6 PM — 9m88, ØZI and YELLOW
- **[Sunrise Meditation Gathering](https://centralpark.guide/events/sunrise-meditation-gathering-2026-08-16/)** at the Pool, 9 AM–12, and **[Flowaroundnyc yoga](https://centralpark.guide/events/flowaroundnyc-x-yoga-2026-08-16/)** at the Great Hill, 5–7:30 PM
- **[International Folkdancers](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-international-folkdancers-2026-08-16/)** at King Jagiello Plaza from 11 AM

## Quick recap

- **Four dead days.** Nothing at all Monday or Wednesday; the loop is clear all week.
- **Monday is 92° with evening storms** — the hardest day of the stretch.
- **Friday breaks the heat**, free music at the Bandshell 3–7 PM, family camping overnight at the Great Hill.
- **Saturday 7–10:30 AM the drives carry the marathon training run.** 110th is car-free 7 AM–3 PM as the alternative.
- **Sunday 4–8:30 AM CRCA has East Drive.** Two race mornings back to back.
- **Three nights of music:** Bandshell Friday, Blues Traveler Saturday, Taiwanese Waves Sunday.
- **East Meadow closed all weekend.**

Have a good week,
