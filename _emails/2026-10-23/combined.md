Marathon week. **The course's blue line gets painted on West Drive on Wednesday morning**, the Opening Ceremony takes West Drive on Friday evening, and a kids' race opens Sunday. Then, the day this week ends, the two biggest days of the park's year arrive. And the weather is extraordinary again: **seven sunny days, 58–69°, essentially no rain.**

## Weather this week

Sunny or mostly sunny all seven days — 61°, 65°, 69°, 58°, 60°, 67°, 69° — with rain chances of 0–4% throughout. Nights 43–53°. That is two consecutive flawless weeks, which the park has not had since August.

## Sat Oct 24 — two foliage photo walks, and a fun run at the Bandshell

**Two [Fall Foliage Photo Walks](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/)** — from **The Pool, 9:30–11:30 AM**, and from **Boys' Gate at 10 AM.** North end, peak colour, someone showing you where to point a camera. The best-timed thing on the whole autumn calendar.

- **Ronald McDonald House Fun Run** stages at Bandshell Plaza, 8 AM–2 PM. Six hours; the Mall is busy, the drives stay open.
- **Free Yoga** and **CPC Yoga**, both East Meadow at 11 AM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.
- **Shoresh Prayer Service** at Tarr Playground, 10 AM–1:30 PM; **Awareness Meetup for Namrud Wegahta Tessema** at Ross Pinetum, 3–6 PM.

61° and partly sunny. East Meadow closed, and **32 private ceremony bookings** — the heaviest day of the week.

## Sun Oct 25 — a kids' race on West Drive, then a very good family afternoon

**The NYRR Kids Kickoff holds West Drive, 8 AM–12 PM.** The first of marathon weekend's satellite events, and a genuine morning closure on the west side.

Afternoon is excellent:

- **Geology: Central Park Rocks!** at the Harlem Meer, 1–2 PM — the glacial history written into the park's outcrops.
- **Pinocchio and Geppetto's NYC Adventure** at the **Diana Ross Playground**, 2–3 PM.
- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** and **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, both 2 PM.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11 AM–6 PM; **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7 PM.

65° and sunny. East Meadow closed.

## Mon Oct 26 – Tue Oct 27 — the last quiet days before it all starts

Nothing public except the standing Arsenal exhibits and Birding Bob. **Monday is 69° and sunny** — the warmest day of the week and the last genuinely empty one. If you want a long ride or run before the weekend takes the park, Monday is it.

## Wed Oct 28, 9–10 AM — the blue line gets painted

**The [TCS NYC Marathon Blue Line Painting Ceremony](https://centralpark.guide/events/2026-tcs-nyc-marathon-blue-line-painting-ceremony-2026-10-28/)** takes West Drive for an hour.

This is one of the park's quieter traditions: the blue line that marks the marathon course down the middle of the road gets laid, and the ceremony is open. If you have ever wondered why there is a blue stripe on the asphalt all year, this is the hour it happens.

## Thu Oct 29 – Fri Oct 30 — the Opening Ceremony

**The Marathon Opening Ceremony holds West Drive, Friday 4–7 PM.** Expect the west side to be committed through the evening.

Friday morning offers the last calm thing of the week: **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)** at 10 AM from Belvedere Castle, at 69° and sunny — the last Ramble walk before the park turns over to the race. A **Meetup with Apple** sits at The Pond Thursday and Friday mornings.

## The next two days — the biggest of the park's year

This email's window ends Friday. What follows it immediately:

- **Saturday Oct 31** — **Abbott Dash to the Finish Line 5K**, 8:45–11 AM on the Ted Corbitt Loop, with the finish at Grand Army Plaza from 8 AM.
- **Sunday Nov 1** — **the TCS New York City Marathon**, 8 AM–5 PM, 55,000 runners finishing in the park.

**The loop is gone both days.** If you are running the marathon, good luck. If you are not, Saturday and Sunday are for watching or for being somewhere else entirely — and the Conservatory Garden Tour at 11 on Saturday and the North Woods walk at 10 are both still on, well away from the course.

## Steady all week

- **The marathon build-out permit** runs through November 10 across the finish-line end of the park. Scaffolding, fencing and trailers are thickening on the west side and around Tavern on the Green all week. **The drives are not closed by it** — the real closures are the three events above.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 23**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free. **Wreath Interpretations Open Call** at the Arsenal all week.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** rotating: Loeb Boathouse weekend, Strawberry Fields Monday, **Conservatory Garden** Friday.
- **No league play on the Great Lawn** for a twelfth week.

## Quick recap

- **Seven sunny days, 58–69°**, almost no rain. Monday at 69° is the pick.
- **Wednesday 9 AM: the blue line gets painted** on West Drive.
- **Friday 4–7 PM: Marathon Opening Ceremony**, West Drive.
- **Sunday 8 AM–12: NYRR Kids Kickoff** holds West Drive.
- **Two Fall Foliage Photo Walks Saturday** — The Pool 9:30, Boys' Gate 10.
- **Then: Dash to the Finish Line Oct 31, the Marathon Nov 1.** The loop is gone both days.

Have a good week in the park,
