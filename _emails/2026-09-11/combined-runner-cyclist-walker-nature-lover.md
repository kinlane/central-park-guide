The clear run is over. After three weeks with nothing on the drives, this weekend puts a charity walk on the Bandshell Saturday, the Fifth Avenue Mile and Race for the Cure on top of each other Sunday morning, and an 83% chance of thunderstorms over the whole of Sunday. The good news is on the other side: Monday through Wednesday are sunny, mild and almost entirely unpermitted.

## Weather this week

Saturday is the weekend's only good day at 79° and mostly sunny, storms move in overnight, and Sunday runs 79° with an 83% chance of showers and thunderstorms. Monday through Wednesday come back clear and cool at 74–79°, which is where your long efforts belong this week.

## Sat Sep 12 — Buddy Walk on the Bandshell 10 AM–2 PM

**The [NDSS New York City Buddy Walk](https://centralpark.guide/events/ndss-new-york-city-buddy-walk-2026-09-12/) holds Bandshell Plaza and Dead Road North from 10 to 2**, with staging across the Mall and crowds arriving on the 72nd Street approaches from mid-morning. The Fifth Avenue Mile also carries an all-day permit at Grand Army Plaza; Saturday is its setup, not the race.

**Run or ride before 9 AM or after 2:30.** The south end is committed in between, and the drives carry pedestrian traffic on both approaches.

Four good things on foot, all in the morning: **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere Castle at 10, with fall migration running; the **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11, behind the scenes with the staff; the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 from Mariners' Gate; and a chess master playing thirty people at once at the Chess and Checkers House from 11.

East Meadow is closed and 32 private bookings have the scenic south end spoken for.

## Sun Sep 13 — two races and a thunderstorm. Do not plan a morning.

**[The New Balance 5th Avenue Mile](https://events.nyrr.org/new-balance-5th-avenue-mile) goes off at 7:25 AM** from Grand Army Plaza, running up Fifth. Two hours later **[Susan G. Komen Race for the Cure](https://centralpark.guide/events/susan-g-komen-nyc-race-for-the-cure-2026-09-13/) takes Bandshell Plaza, 9 AM–2 PM**, with a loop course and thousands of participants.

**The loop is unusable from dawn to roughly 2:30 PM.** This is not a "go early" week — early is exactly when the Mile is staging at the southeast corner. If you need the miles, the bridle path and the Reservoir are your options, or wait until mid-afternoon. And check the radar first: an 83% thunderstorm chance over a 9 AM charity race is a schedule that moves.

If it clears, the **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)** leaves Boys' Gate at 10 — the summer flowers are nearly finished, so this is close to the last one — and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** goes at 2. East Meadow closed a second day.

## Mon Sep 14 – Wed Sep 16 — the week's real riding and running

Sunny, 74–79°, and the board is nearly bare: an evening dance program at the Bandshell Monday, a dinner at Naturalists' Gate, a concert at Rumsey Tuesday night. Nothing on any drive, no crowds, no heat.

**Take the long one Monday or Tuesday.** After a weekend like this one, three clear mild days in a row is the gift.

## Thu Sep 17 – Fri Sep 18 — showers return

Warmer and wetter, 82° Thursday and 79° Friday with shower chances both afternoons. Friday morning offers **Birding Basics** at Belvedere at 10 and the **Seneca Village Tour** at 2. The Osborn Cup holds Bowling Green all day Friday — bowls, not your line.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain sits under an all-day "Closed" permit every day again — 21 consecutive days now**, three full weeks, with no reason anywhere in the record. If the Terrace is on your walking route, it still isn't available.

[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/) runs five mornings from Loeb Boathouse at 7:30 and 8:30. East 106th Street lawn is out Monday through Thursday.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday morning is gone:** 5th Avenue Mile 7:25 AM, Race for the Cure 9–2, 83% thunderstorms.
- **Saturday: Buddy Walk on the Bandshell and Dead Road**, 10–2. Ride or run before 9 or after 2:30.
- **Monday–Wednesday: sunny, 74–79°, nothing permitted.** The week's long efforts go here.
- **Four Conservancy walks and tours Saturday morning**, plus the pollinator walk Sunday.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed a 21st straight day.**

Pick your mornings carefully,
