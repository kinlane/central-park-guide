Three weeks of empty drives end this weekend, and they end emphatically: three permitted races and charity walks across Saturday and Sunday, the heaviest private-booking day of the season, and an 83% thunderstorm chance sitting on Sunday morning where two of those events are scheduled. Then the calendar empties again for three days, and SummerStage runs five nights out of seven. Bethesda Terrace, meanwhile, closes for a twenty-first consecutive day.

## Weather this week

Saturday holds at 79° and mostly sunny before storms arrive overnight; Sunday runs 79° with an 83% chance of showers and thunderstorms. Monday through Wednesday are clear and mild at 74–79°. A charity race scheduled 9 AM–2 PM under that Sunday number is the week's most likely cancellation or compression — worth watching what actually happens on the ground.

## The weekend the permit feed under-described

Three race-shaped events, none of which the feed flagged as affecting the loop:

- **NDSS New York City Buddy Walk** — Saturday 10 AM–2 PM, Bandshell Plaza and Dead Road North.
- **New Balance 5th Avenue Mile** — Sunday, gun at 7:25 AM, staging at Grand Army Plaza.
- **Susan G. Komen Race for the Cure** — Sunday 9 AM–2 PM, Bandshell Plaza.

All three arrived tagged only as generic events. Two of them stage at Naumburg, which is precisely where AIDS Walk stages — the event that taught this newsletter to stop trusting the feed's own tagging. They have since been corrected in our data, but the pattern is the story: **the permit feed does not describe loop impact, and a weekend can look empty in the data while three thousand people assemble at the Bandshell.**

## Bethesda Terrace, week three

Seven more all-day permits titled nothing but **"Closed."** That is **21 consecutive days** — a full three weeks — with no organizer, no reason and no event attached to any of them.

I offered two readings a fortnight ago: physical work, or an administrative hold ahead of an unannounced event. Three unbroken weeks effectively settles it. A hold resolves into a named permit — Rumsey's did, exactly on schedule. Twenty-one days of anonymous all-day closure at the park's most photographed structure is restoration work, and the absence of any public notice about it is itself the thing worth asking about.

## Mon Sep 14 – Wed Sep 16 — the calendar empties again

Three sunny days carrying a dance program at the Bandshell, a sponsor dinner at Naturalists' Gate, a Danny Elfman concert, a mobile health unit at Central Park North and a corporate team-building booking. Bowling Green closed all day Tuesday; East 106th Street lawn out Monday through Thursday.

The post-Labor-Day pattern holds: weekends carry everything, weekdays carry almost nothing.

## SummerStage runs five of seven nights

Marcus King Saturday, Young Thug Sunday, Danny Elfman Tuesday, **New York Sings Yiddish Thursday — rescheduled from a June storm** — and a sold-out, twice-moved Yeat on Friday. Two of the five are weather makeups, which is worth noting: the venue is now absorbing its own backlog in the same weeks it runs new programming, and the all-day festival permit at Rumsey covers five days rather than the usual two or three.

## The permit load, in aggregate

**182 field permits**, up from 105 two weeks ago and 144 last week. Roughly eighty on the Great Lawn, sixty at Heckscher, 24 at North Meadow. **Heckscher has more than doubled in a week**, from 24 to about sixty, which is the sharpest single-venue move I have seen in this series — fall league play starting, most likely, and worth confirming next week.

**89 private bookings**, up from 79 and 66 in the two prior weeks — a third consecutive increase. **Saturday alone carries 32**, the heaviest day of the season, followed by Friday the 18th at 17 and Thursday at 13. Cop Cot leads at 12, Wagner Cove at 9, Ladies' Pavilion at 8. The late-week weight is new; ceremony bookings have been a weekend phenomenon until now.

## Quick recap

- **Three races and walks across Saturday and Sunday**, none flagged by the permit feed as touching the loop.
- **83% thunderstorms over Sunday**, where two of the three are scheduled.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed a 21st consecutive day** — three weeks, unexplained. Restoration is now the only reading that fits.
- **Heckscher field permits more than doubled**, 24 to about sixty in one week.
- **89 private bookings, 32 on Saturday** — a third straight weekly increase, now spreading into Thursday and Friday.
- **SummerStage runs five nights**, two of them June and July makeups.
