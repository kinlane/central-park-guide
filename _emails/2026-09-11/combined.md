After three weeks of nothing on the drives, everything lands at once. Two charity events stage at the Bandshell on back-to-back mornings, the Fifth Avenue Mile runs Sunday at 7:25, and the forecast puts an 83% chance of thunderstorms directly on top of all of it. Then the weather turns beautiful for a quiet Monday through Wednesday, and SummerStage plays five nights out of seven.

## Weather this week

Saturday is the good day — 79° and mostly sunny — then storms arrive overnight and Sunday carries an 83% chance of showers and thunderstorms at 79°, which is the single most consequential fact in this email. Monday through Wednesday are clear and mild at 74–79°, with showers creeping back Thursday and Friday.

## Sat Sep 12 — the Buddy Walk takes the Bandshell, and the week's best weather

**The [NDSS New York City Buddy Walk](https://centralpark.guide/events/ndss-new-york-city-buddy-walk-2026-09-12/) holds Bandshell Plaza and Dead Road North, 10 AM–2 PM.** Staging spills across the Mall and the 72nd Street approaches from mid-morning. The Fifth Avenue Mile also holds an all-day permit at Grand Army Plaza — that is Saturday setup; the race itself is Sunday.

If you are running or riding, go before 9 or after 2:30, and expect the south end to be busy either way.

Everything else worth the trip:

- **[Chess lecture and simul with IM Kassa Korley](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/chess-lecture-simul-with-kassa-korley)**, 11 AM at the Chess and Checkers House — he plays up to thirty people at once.
- **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** 10 AM at Belvedere; **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** 11 AM; **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.
- **[Marcus King Band](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/marcus-king-band/)** at Rumsey, 7–10 PM — SummerStage's return.
- Model yachts at Kerbs 9–3, **That Guitar Man** at Hernshead noon–6, **tango at the Shakespeare Statue** 6–9 PM.

East Meadow is closed, and **32 private ceremony bookings** make it the heaviest day of the season so far.

## Sun Sep 13 — the Fifth Avenue Mile at 7:25, Race for the Cure at 9, and storms over both

**[The New Balance 5th Avenue Mile](https://events.nyrr.org/new-balance-5th-avenue-mile) goes off at 7:25 AM**, staging at Grand Army Plaza and running up Fifth. **[Susan G. Komen Race for the Cure](https://centralpark.guide/events/susan-g-komen-nyc-race-for-the-cure-2026-09-13/) takes Bandshell Plaza 9 AM–2 PM** with a full loop course behind it.

**The loop is not usable Sunday morning.** Between the two events and an 83% thunderstorm chance, treat the whole morning as gone: the south end and the Mall are committed from dawn, and the drives carry race traffic through early afternoon. If you need miles, go very early or wait until after 2:30 — and watch the radar, because a 9 AM charity race in a thunderstorm is a schedule that changes.

Still on the board if the weather holds: the **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)** from Boys' Gate at 10, **International Sunday** on the Great Hill 10 AM–2 PM, a **Celtic Harp Circle** at Cop Cot noon–2, the **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–6, and **[Young Thug](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/young-thug/)** at Rumsey 8–10 PM.

## Mon Sep 14 – Wed Sep 16 — the reward: three clear, empty days

Sunny and 74–79°, and almost nothing permitted. **Dance Is Life** at the Bandshell Monday 5–7 PM, the **Starlight Dinner** at Naturalists' Gate 6–9 PM, and **[Danny Elfman](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/danny-elfman/)** at Rumsey Tuesday 8:30 PM. Bowling Green is closed all day Tuesday.

These are the days to take the long ride or the long run. After the weekend, the road is yours.

## Thu Sep 17 – Fri Sep 18 — two rescheduled shows close the week

**[New York Sings Yiddish](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/new-york-sings-yiddish-26/)** finally happens Thursday 7–10 PM at Rumsey, moved from a June storm. Friday brings a sold-out, twice-rescheduled **[Yeat](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/yeat/)** at 8 PM, plus **Birding Basics** at 10, the **Seneca Village Tour** at 2, the **Osborn Cup** on Bowling Green all day, and a Grade 8 picnic at Heckscher. Showers return both afternoons.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — an all-day permit titled only "Closed," every day again. That is **21 consecutive days** now, across three weeks, with no reason on record.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, Monday–Friday 9 AM–5 PM.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse, 7:30 and 8:30.
- East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday; Columbus Circle Food Market at Merchants' Gate.
- **182 field permits** — roughly eighty on the Great Lawn, sixty at Heckscher, 24 at North Meadow. **89 private bookings**, 32 of them Saturday.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday morning is gone:** Fifth Avenue Mile 7:25 AM, Race for the Cure 9 AM–2 PM, and an 83% thunderstorm chance over both.
- **Saturday: the Buddy Walk** holds the Bandshell and Dead Road 10 AM–2 PM.
- **Saturday is the weekend's only good weather** — 79° and mostly sunny.
- **Monday–Wednesday are clear, mild and nearly empty.** Take the long one then.
- **SummerStage plays five nights:** Marcus King, Young Thug, Danny Elfman, Yiddish, Yeat.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed a 21st straight day**, still unexplained.

Have a good week in the park,
