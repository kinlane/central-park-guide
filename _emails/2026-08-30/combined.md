Summer's permits are finally off the road. There is not a single race, charity walk or loop closure between Monday and Sunday — the first clean week since June — and the one unmissable thing on the calendar is free: Samara Joy and Christian McBride at Rumsey Playfield on Wednesday night. What the park *is* doing this week is repairing itself, with six lawns closed for post-summer restoration.

## Weather this week

Warm and sticky all seven days with a thunderstorm chance on most of them, peaking Tuesday at 85° and Thursday at 86°, before a northwest wind dries things out Friday and hands you a mostly sunny 79° Saturday. Nights slide into the 60s by the weekend, so the early mornings are the good hours — and Wednesday's outdoor concert is the one evening worth watching the radar for.

## Mon Aug 31 — a choir at the Bandshell

[Xinsheng Chinese Choir](https://centralpark.guide/events/xinsheng-chinese-choir-summer-concert-2026-08-31/) sings at Bandshell Plaza 6–7:30 PM, which is a pleasant thing to walk into on a humid Monday evening. Earlier, Cherry Hill is closed until 2 PM and there's a [kids' launch event](https://centralpark.guide/events/launch-81st-st-august-mpl-2026-08-31/) at Mariners' Playground 9 AM–12:30 PM. Nothing anywhere near the drives.

## Tue Sep 1 – Thu Sep 3 — the quiet middle, and the lawns come out of service

Three days with almost nothing scheduled and the hottest air of the week. This is where the maintenance story sits: Bowling Green closed all day Tuesday, The Dairy lawn Wednesday and Thursday, Levin Playground's lawns Thursday and Friday, and East 72nd Street Playground Tuesday through Friday. None of it touches a running or riding line — it's the Conservancy putting the summer lawns back together after Labor Day.

The [CP Sports Summer Program](https://centralpark.guide/events/cp-sports-summer-program-2026-09-01/) runs at South Dead Road Tuesday through Friday, morning and afternoon sessions, if you have a kid to occupy. The [Columbus Circle Food Market](https://centralpark.guide/events/columbus-circle-food-market-2026-09-03/) sets up at Merchants' Gate Thursday through Saturday, 9:30 AM–9 PM.

## Wed Sep 2, 7–10 PM — Samara Joy and Christian McBride, free, Rumsey Playfield

**The week's event.** [SummerStage marks Verve's 70th anniversary](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/verve-70th-anniversary-christian-mcbride-mei-semones-friends-play-the-music-of-getz-gilberto/) with Samara Joy — six-time GRAMMY winner, Bronx native — and Christian McBride, an eleven-time GRAMMY bassist who has recorded with McCoy Tyner, Herbie Hancock and Chick Corea on one side and Sting and Paul McCartney on the other. Mei Semones & Friends play *Getz/Gilberto* and Sparklmami DJs.

It's free and it's a Wednesday, which means the line forms early and the field fills. There's a slight storm chance that evening — go anyway, but don't bring anything that can't get wet.

## Fri Sep 4, 10 AM — Birding Basics at Belvedere Castle

The Conservancy's [Birding Basics Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour) meets at Belvedere Castle, and fall migration is exactly why you'd take it now. Friday is also the first genuinely comfortable day of the week — drier air, northwest wind.

[Barefoot Shakespeare](https://centralpark.guide/events/barefoot-shakespeare-presents-our-august-2026-performances-2026-09-04/) plays Summit Rock Friday, Saturday and Sunday, 3–8 PM. The Conservancy's [Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park) runs those same three days at 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.

## Sat Sep 5 — the fullest day of the week

Mostly sunny and 79°, and something on nearly every surface:

- **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)**, 10 AM from Boys' Gate — the secret butterfly garden, worth it while the summer flowers hold.
- **[Model Yacht Racing](https://centralpark.guide/events/model-yacht-racing-2026-09-05/)** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM. Conservatory Water on a clear Saturday is the best free hour in the park with a kid.
- **[Unity Markets](https://centralpark.guide/events/unitymarketsny-2026-09-05/)** at Farmers' Gate, 110th and Lenox, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **[Tango at the Shakespeare Statue](https://centralpark.guide/events/tango-shakespeare-statue-2026-09-05/)**, 6–9 PM — dancing on the Mall as the light goes.

There's a SummerStage permit open at Rumsey all day Saturday, but no lineup is announced in any of our sources — treat it as a hold, not a show.

## Sun Sep 6 — folkdancing at King Jagiello

[Central Park International Folkdancers](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-international-folkdancers-2026-09-06/) take King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, which is the reliable, slightly hypnotic Sunday fixture up by the Turtle Pond. [Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour) runs again at 10 AM.

## Steady all week

**East Meadow is closed** Monday through Friday for restoration and again both weekend days — the longest closure on the board this week. The East 106th Street lawn is out Monday through Thursday.

**[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — new to this email, and the densest guided birding in the park. Monday, Thursday and Friday at 8:30 AM, Saturday and Sunday at 7:30 and 9:30. Meet at Strawberry Fields Monday, Conservatory Garden Friday, the Boathouse on the weekend. $10, paid at the end.

**Ball fields:** 133 permits across the week — 63 on the Great Lawn, 28 at Heckscher, 23 at North Meadow. A completely normal late-summer load.

## Quick recap

- **Wednesday 7 PM is the week.** Samara Joy and Christian McBride, free, Rumsey Playfield.
- **The drives are clear all seven days.** No race, no walk, no permit on the road — first time since June.
- **Six lawns are out for restoration**, East Meadow the whole week including the weekend.
- **Saturday is the family day:** model yachts at 9, pollinator walk at 10, tango at 6.
- **Birding Bob runs five days a week** and is now in this email — Strawberry Fields, Conservatory Garden, the Boathouse.

See you out there,
