The week the park stops performing and starts recovering. There is no race, no charity walk and no permit on either drive for seven straight days — the first fully clear week since June — and in their place the Conservancy has taken six lawns out of service for post-Labor-Day restoration. Read the two facts together: this is the seasonal turn, and the maintenance calendar is the story.

## Weather this week

Humid and unsettled through Thursday with daily thunderstorm chances and highs of 85–86°, then a northwest wind clears it Friday into a mostly sunny 79° Saturday with 60s overnight. Wet, warm ground through midweek is exactly the condition that pushes lawn restoration schedules, so expect these closures to hold rather than lift early.

## Mon Aug 31 – Fri Sep 4 — the restoration block

Six separate lawn closures overlap this week, which is more than any week since spring:

- **East Meadow** — closed Monday through Friday, *and* again Saturday and Sunday. The longest single closure on the board.
- **East 106th Street lawn** — Monday through Thursday.
- **East 72nd Street Playground lawn** — Tuesday through Friday.
- **Cherry Hill** — Monday, until 2 PM.
- **Bowling Green** — Tuesday, all day.
- **The Dairy lawn** — Wednesday and Thursday, all day.
- **Levin Playground lawns** (Sections A and B) — Thursday and Friday.

None of it touches the drives or a major path. What it does tell you is where the summer wore through: the East Side lawns from 72nd up to 106th are nearly all out at once, while the West Side is untouched this week.

## Wed Sep 2 — SummerStage's September run opens

The [Verve 70th anniversary show](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/verve-70th-anniversary-christian-mcbride-mei-semones-friends-play-the-music-of-getz-gilberto/) — Samara Joy and Christian McBride — takes Rumsey Playfield 7–10 PM under an all-day festival permit that runs 8 AM to midnight across Rumsey and Bandshell Plaza. A free show by two GRAMMY-heavy names on a weeknight is a crowd-management event as much as a concert; the pressure lands on the 72nd Street approaches and Bandshell Plaza from late afternoon.

## Sat Sep 5 — a second SummerStage permit, with nothing announced behind it

There's an identical all-day SummerStage permit at Rumsey on Saturday, but **no lineup appears in the City Parks Foundation feed or anywhere else in our sources**. Their next announced show is Marcus King on Sep 12. Read Saturday's permit as a hold or a load-in rather than a public event — and if something does get announced late, that's the week's thing to watch.

Otherwise Saturday is the busiest civilian day: model yacht racing at Kerbs Boathouse 9 AM–3 PM, Unity Markets at Farmers' Gate 9 AM–7 PM, the Conservancy's pollinator walk at 10, and tango at the Shakespeare Statue 6–9 PM.

## Sun Sep 6 — folkdancers, and East Meadow still out

[International Folkdancers](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-international-folkdancers-2026-09-06/) at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–6 PM. East Meadow remains closed for a seventh straight day.

## The permit load, in aggregate

**133 field permits** across the week: 63 on the Great Lawn, 28 at Heckscher, 23 at North Meadow, 9 on the tennis courts, 5 each at South Dead Road and Levin. That's an ordinary late-summer volume — the leagues have not wound down yet even as the event calendar has.

**Private bookings** are running heavy, as they do every early-September weekend: **50 across the week**, with Saturday alone carrying 17 and Sunday 9. Cop Cot leads at 11 and Ladies' Pavilion at 8 — the two small ceremony sites — followed by the Great Lawn at 7 and Ross Pinetum at 4. Bethesda Terrace adds seven separate closure permits of its own. If you're photographing the south end on a weekend morning, assume the picturesque spots are spoken for.

## Quick recap

- **Zero permits on the drives all week** — the first clear seven days since June.
- **Six lawns out for restoration**, East Meadow for all seven days; the closures cluster on the East Side.
- **Wednesday:** SummerStage's September run opens with Samara Joy and Christian McBride, free.
- **Saturday's SummerStage permit has no announced show** — a hold, unless something lands late.
- **133 field permits**, a normal late-summer load, plus a heavy weekend of private ceremony bookings.
