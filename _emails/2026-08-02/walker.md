Hi walkers — this week splits cleanly in two. Monday through Wednesday the park is quiet, cool-ish and largely unbooked, which is the best walking you'll get in August. Then the heat arrives Thursday and the calendar fills, ending in a Saturday with markets at two gates, tours at eleven, tango at six and a skate circle in between.

## Weather this week
Monday is wet at 81°F, Tuesday clears beautifully at 83°F, and then it climbs — 82°F Wednesday, 87°F Thursday, 88°F Friday through Sunday with an afternoon thunderstorm chance each day. Walk the long routes Tuesday and Wednesday; from Thursday on, keep to the shaded north — the Ravine, the Loch, the North Woods — and go before ten.

## Mon Aug 3 — the rain walk
Showers and storms most of the day at 81°F. Almost nothing is booked: lawn maintenance at East 106th Street and the East 72nd Street Playground, and free movies on the Great Lawn at 7 if the sky clears. A wet Monday empties the Ramble and the Hallett Sanctuary completely, and the Loch runs properly after rain. If you own a decent rain shell, this is the most private the park will be all week.

## Tue Aug 4 — the best walking day, ending at the Bandshell
83°F and mostly sunny with almost no wind. Nothing is closed on the paths. The evening is the draw: **Naumburg Orchestral Concerts play the Bandshell at 7:30 — free, no ticket, the 121st season** — and the walk down the Mall into it with the light going is one of the small perfect things this park does. People start settling around 6:30, so come at 6 if you want a bench. National Night Out fills Merchants' Gate 2–6 PM; the Bowling Greens are closed for the day.

## Wed Aug 5 — nothing booked anywhere
82°F with a shower chance. This is the emptiest day on the whole calendar — no closures beyond the East 106th lawn, no permits at the scenic stops, no crowds anywhere. Take the route you've been saving. Bow Bridge, the Ramble, up through the Ravine to the Loch and back down the west side; it'll be yours.

## Thu Aug 6 — the heat starts, and so does the market
87°F, storms possible. **The Columbus Circle food market opens at Merchants' Gate, 9:30 AM to 9 PM, and runs through Saturday** — worth timing into a walk, and worth avoiding if you want quiet at the southwest corner. The **Iconic Views tour steps off from Columbus Circle at 10** if you'd rather be led. At the Arsenal, "Foundational Voices: The People Who Built SummerStage" runs 4:30 to 7, free, and the Jack Vartoogian photography show is up there all week, 9 to 5, also free — both are indoor and air-conditioned, which will matter by Thursday. Rumsey has Andrew Bird at 8; the 72nd Street area is busy from 6.

## Fri Aug 7 — pollinators at eleven, R&B at seven
88°F and mostly sunny. **The Conservancy's Summer Pollinator Walk leaves from Boys' Gate at 11** — a staff pick, and the kind of slow, close-looking walk that suits a hot day better than distance does. Iconic Views again at 10 from Columbus Circle, market again at Merchants' Gate. Rumsey has a free R&B bill at 7. Everything else is field permits.

## Sat Aug 8 — the fullest day of the week, and how to walk around it
88°F. Take this one in order: **model yacht racing at Kerbs Boathouse from 9**, **Unity Markets at Farmers' Gate 9 to 7**, the food market at Merchants' Gate, **storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue 10 to 1**, then two Conservancy tours at 11 — **Seneca Village from Mariners' Gate** and **the Conservatory Garden tour** up at 105th. Afternoon brings the **skate circle at Dead Road North from 2**, **Jack in the Park at Summit Rock from 4**, and **tango at the Shakespeare Statue from 6 to 9**, which is the loveliest of them. East Meadow is closed all day, 31 private ceremonies are booked across the scenic spots, and Arise New York holds Bandshell Plaza 10 to 6 — so if you want a quiet stretch, it's the North Woods and the Ravine, and only those.

## Sun Aug 9 — folkdancers at Jagiello, and a busy Meer
88°F with storms building. **The Central Park International Folkdancers hold King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM to 8 PM** — free to watch, easy to fall into, and reliably the friendliest crowd in the park. Up north, **Summer on the Harlem Meer runs 9 to 6 at the Dana Discovery Center** with **Uptown Royalty performing at 2**, free. The pollinator walk repeats at 11 from Boys' Gate. Early risers should know the cycling club has East Drive until 8:30, which means peloton noise on the east side and nothing else. East Meadow closed again.

## Quick recap
- **Wednesday is the empty day.** Nothing booked park-wide. Walk whatever you like.
- **Tue 7:30 PM: Naumburg at the Bandshell**, free — the walk down the Mall into it is the week's best hour.
- **Saturday is the fullest day of the summer so far** — markets, tours at 11, skate circle at 2, tango at 6.
- **Monday is the rain walk** — Ramble, Loch and Ravine, nobody there.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain stays closed through Sept 2.** Route around it.
- **The North Woods and the Ravine are the only quiet ground Saturday**, and the coolest ground Thursday onward.
- **Fri and Sun 11 AM: Summer Pollinator Walk from Boys' Gate**, Conservancy.

Enjoy the last stretch of high summer,
The Central Park Guide
