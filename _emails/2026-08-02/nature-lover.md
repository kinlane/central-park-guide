Hi nature lovers — the Conservancy has put three good walks on the calendar this week: the Summer Pollinator Walk twice, Friday and Sunday, and the Conservatory Garden tour Saturday morning alongside Seneca Village. Around them, the week gives you one wet Monday and one genuinely empty Wednesday, which are the two best days to be in the Ramble.

## Weather this week
Storms through Monday at 81°F, a clear and mild 83°F Tuesday, then a slow climb — 82°F Wednesday, 87°F Thursday, 88°F Friday through Sunday with afternoon convection most days. The heat matters more than the rain here: from Thursday on, be in the Ravine or the North Woods before nine or don't go, and note that the Loch runs properly for a day or two after Monday's storms.

## Mon Aug 3 — rain in the Ramble, and nobody in it
81°F, showers and thunderstorms most of the day. Nothing is booked anywhere near the woodland. This is the day the Ramble belongs to whoever's willing to get wet, and the Hallett Nature Sanctuary with it — both empty out completely in weather. **The Loch will be running after this**, so if you've only seen the waterfalls as a trickle, Tuesday morning is when to walk the Ravine from the Huddlestone Arch up. Late-summer rain also brings the mushrooms out along the North Woods trail edges within a day or two.

## Tue Aug 4 — clear, mild, and the water still up
83°F and mostly sunny with almost no wind. The best conditions of the week for slow looking. Take the Ravine while the Loch is still full from Monday. Nothing is permitted anywhere in the north end, and the Conservatory Garden's perennial beds are into their late-summer register now — less peak, more depth, with the Italianate lawn hedges holding shape and the south garden's perennials at their most layered. Early morning; the garden opens at eight and is empty until nine.

## Wed Aug 5 — the emptiest day on the calendar
82°F with a shower chance. There is nothing programmed in the entire park today — no tours, no permits at the water bodies, nothing in the north end. Turtle Pond, the Upper Lobe, the Gill, Wagner Cove: pick any of them and you'll be alone. Mid-August is a quiet stretch for birds — breeding is done, the first southbound shorebirds and warblers are only just starting to move — so this is a week for insects, dragonflies over Turtle Pond, and the herons that have been working the Meer edges all summer.

## Thu Aug 6 — heat arrives; stay north and early
87°F, storms possible. The **Iconic Views tour leaves Columbus Circle at 10** if you want the built park rather than the wild one. Otherwise the day belongs to the north end before nine — the Ravine holds shade and moving water and runs several degrees cooler than anywhere south of the Reservoir. PAGNY has a wellness event on the Great Hill 9 to 11, which is the one crowd note up there.

## Fri Aug 7, 11 AM — Summer Pollinator Walk from Boys' Gate
**Conservancy, free with registration, leaving from Boys' Gate at Central Park West and 100th Street.** This is a staff pick and it earns it: the north end's meadow plantings are at their fullest in early August and the walk is built around what's actually working the flowers — native bees, wasps, the late-summer butterflies, and the plant choices behind them. It's also the right kind of walk for an 88°F day, since it's slow and stays in the planted north end rather than covering ground. Register through the Conservancy; it fills.

## Sat Aug 8, 11 AM — Seneca Village and the Conservatory Garden, same hour
88°F. Two Conservancy walks step off simultaneously and you'll have to choose. **The Seneca Village tour leaves Mariners' Gate** at West 85th, covering the archaeology and the landscape evidence of the community that was cleared to build the park — this is history through terrain, and the ground itself is the primary document. **The Conservatory Garden tour** starts at 105th and Fifth, in the formal gardens at their August fullness. Be aware the rest of the park is at capacity this day — markets at two gates, the skate circle, 31 private ceremonies at the scenic spots — so the North Woods and the Ravine are the only genuinely quiet ground, and they are very quiet.

## Sun Aug 9 — the pollinator walk again, and the Meer all day
88°F with storms building. **The Summer Pollinator Walk repeats at 11 from Boys' Gate.** Up at the Harlem Meer, **Summer on the Harlem Meer runs 9 to 6 at the Dana Discovery Center** — catch-and-release fishing off the Meer edge is the draw, and the herons work that shoreline early regardless of who else is there. Get there before ten and the north shore is still yours.

## Quick recap
- **Fri and Sun 11 AM: Summer Pollinator Walk from Boys' Gate**, Conservancy, free with registration. The best thing on the calendar.
- **Sat 11 AM: Seneca Village from Mariners' Gate** or **the Conservatory Garden tour** at 105th — same hour, pick one.
- **Monday's rain empties the Ramble and Hallett completely**, and refills the Loch.
- **Tuesday morning is the Ravine walk** — waterfalls up, 83°F, clear.
- **Wednesday nothing is programmed park-wide.** Turtle Pond, the Gill, Wagner Cove, all yours.
- **Thursday onward: north end before nine, or not at all.** The Ravine runs coolest.
- **Saturday the park is at capacity** — North Woods and Ravine are the only quiet ground.
