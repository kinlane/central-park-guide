Hi — two things on the calendar for you this week and one gift. The two are a wellness morning on the Great Hill Thursday and yoga with art on the Great Lawn Sunday. The gift is Wednesday, when nothing at all is programmed anywhere in the park — no tours, no permits, no crowds. Take it.

## Weather this week
Storms through Monday at 81°F, then a clear and mild 83°F Tuesday before the heat settles in — 82°F Wednesday, 87°F Thursday and three straight days at 88°F Friday through Sunday, with afternoon convection most days. Sunrise moves from 5:53 to 5:59 across the week and sunset from 8:09 back to 8:02, so from Thursday on your practice window is the ninety minutes after first light and nothing after.

## Mon Aug 3 — the rain, and the emptiest Ramble of the summer
81°F with showers and thunderstorms most of the day. Nothing is booked. Rain does something to this park that nothing else does — the Ramble, the Hallett Sanctuary and the North Woods empty out entirely, and the sound changes. If you have any tolerance for getting wet, this is the most solitude available all week. The Loch runs properly after a storm, and standing at Huddlestone Arch listening to it is worth the walk up.

## Tue Aug 4 — clear, still, and quiet until nine
83°F, mostly sunny, wind at 0 to 5 mph — the calmest air of the week. The Conservatory Garden opens at eight and is empty until nine, and the north garden's still water and the wisteria pergola are the two best sitting spots in the park at that hour. Nothing is permitted anywhere in the north end. In the evening, **Naumburg's final concert of the season plays the Bandshell at 7:30** — free, ninety minutes, and if you sit at the back of the Mall it functions as a sound bath more than a concert. Crowds gather from 6:30, so stay north of 79th if you want the quiet version of the evening.

## Wed Aug 5 — nothing is happening, anywhere
82°F with a shower chance. This is a park-wide blank: no tours, no concerts, no closures beyond one lawn in the northeast, no crowd-drawing permits at any gate. Summit Rock, Hallett, the Ramble, the north end of the Conservatory Garden — all of them will be genuinely empty. If you've been meaning to do a long slow morning without planning around anyone, this is the day the calendar hands you.

## Thu Aug 6, 9–11 AM — wellness morning on the Great Hill
87°F. **PAGNY holds a wellness event on the Great Hill, 9 to 11 AM** — the Great Hill is one of the quietest large open spaces in the park and its shade is genuine, which matters at 87°F. If group programming isn't your thing, the same corner of the park is where you want to be anyway that morning. Later, the **Arsenal's photography exhibit is open 9 to 5, free and air-conditioned** — a real option once the heat sets in. Evening note: Rumsey has a large free concert at 8, so the whole 72nd Street area is loud and crowded from 6 onward.

## Fri Aug 7, 11 AM — the pollinator walk, which is slower than it sounds
88°F. **The Conservancy's Summer Pollinator Walk leaves Boys' Gate at 11**, free with registration. It moves slowly and stays in the planted north end rather than covering distance, which makes it the right kind of thing for a hot morning — attention rather than exertion. Otherwise the day is heat management: north end before nine, shade after.

## Sat Aug 8 — the busiest day; here is where nobody is
88°F and the fullest day of the summer so far. Markets at two gates, tours at eleven, a skate circle from two, tango at six, and thirty-one private ceremonies across the scenic spots. The honest advice: **the North Woods and the Ravine are the only quiet ground in the park today**, and they are very quiet — everything else is drawing. Go up past the Loch and stay there.

## Sun Aug 9, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM — Embodied Expression: yoga and art on the Great Lawn
**Great Lawn, 10:30 to 12:30.** A combined movement and art practice — the pairing is unusual and the Great Lawn at mid-morning is more open than it sounds, with the field permits concentrated at the diamonds rather than the center. It's 88°F with storms building later, so bring more water than you think and know the session ends before the worst of it. The **pollinator walk repeats at 11 from Boys' Gate** if you'd rather walk than practice, and up at the Harlem Meer, **Summer on the Harlem Meer runs 9 to 6** — the north shore before ten is one of the calmest places in Manhattan.

## Quick recap
- **Wednesday nothing is programmed park-wide.** The whole day is yours; take the long slow one.
- **Sun 10:30 AM–12:30 PM: Embodied Expression yoga and art on the Great Lawn.**
- **Thu 9–11 AM: wellness morning on the Great Hill** — real shade, quiet corner.
- **Fri and Sun 11 AM: Summer Pollinator Walk from Boys' Gate** — slow, close attention, free with registration.
- **Monday's rain empties the Ramble and Hallett** more completely than any other condition.
- **Tuesday is the still day** — Conservatory Garden at eight, before anyone.
- **Saturday the only quiet ground is the North Woods and the Ravine.** Everywhere else is drawing.
- **Evening noise:** Rumsey Thursday and Friday from 6, the Bandshell Tuesday from 6:30. Stay north of 79th.

Breathe easy this week,
