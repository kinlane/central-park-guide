Hey runners — only one thing takes road all week, and it's the CRCA peloton on East Drive before dawn Sunday. Everything else that could get in your way is weather: a wet Monday, then a steady climb into the upper 80s from Thursday on. Tuesday and Wednesday are the two clean days and you should spend them well.

## Weather this week
Monday is wet at 81°F with storms most of the day, Tuesday clears to 83°F and mostly sunny, then Wednesday holds at 82°F before the heat builds — 87°F Thursday and 88°F Friday through Sunday, each afternoon carrying a thunderstorm chance. Put your quality work Tuesday and Wednesday mornings; from Thursday onward the only comfortable window is before 7 AM, and sunrise slides from 5:56 to 5:59 across those days.

## Mon Aug 3 — the drives are clear and nobody is on them
81°F with showers and thunderstorms most of the day. Nothing is permitted anywhere near the loop — the only closures are lawn maintenance at East 106th Street and the East 72nd Street Playground Lawn, both well off your line. Wet drives, empty drives. If you've been putting off easy miles, this is the day for them, and the Reservoir track drains better than the bridle path does after a hard cell.

## Tue Aug 4 — clean loop, Bandshell fills from 6:30
83°F and mostly sunny, the most pleasant day of the seven. Nothing on the drives. Two crowd notes only: the Bowling Greens and the East 102nd Street Landscape are both closed all day for maintenance, neither of which touches your route, and **Naumburg's final August concert plays the Bandshell at 7:30** — people settle on the Mall from about 6:30, so the 72nd Street crossings thicken in the evening. National Night Out runs 2–6 PM at Merchants' Gate if you enter at Columbus Circle.

## Wed Aug 5 — the emptiest day on the calendar
82°F with a scattered storm chance. This is the quietest permit day of the week park-wide: no closures beyond the East 106th lawn, no staging, nothing at the gates. Pair it with Tuesday as your second quality session. Morning, before the afternoon cells build.

## Thu Aug 6 — heat arrives, and Rumsey gets its biggest night
87°F, storms possible late. The drives stay clear all day. What changes is foot traffic: **Andrew Bird plays Rumsey Playfield at 8 with the Wordless Music Orchestra**, free, and it will be the best-attended show of the week — the 72nd Street entrance is congested from about 6 onward. The Columbus Circle food market also opens for its Thursday-through-Saturday run, 9:30 AM to 9 PM at Merchants' Gate, so both southern entrances are slow in the evening. Thursday is also the busiest field day of the week with 71 softball permits across North Meadow, Great Lawn and Heckscher — relevant only if you cool down on grass.

## Fri Aug 7 — 88°F, and a second free Rumsey night
Mostly sunny and hot, storms possible after dark. Still nothing on the drives. **Funk Flex's Birthday R&B Picnic takes Rumsey at 7**, free again, with the same 72nd Street pinch from about 5:30. Get out early — 5:57 sunrise, and the difference between a 6 AM start and an 8 AM start this week is roughly ten degrees.

## Sat Aug 8 — no road closures, but the park is full
88°F. **East Meadow is closed all day**, so it's out for strides. The drives are open and unpermitted, which makes this the only genuinely free weekend morning you'll get — but from 9 AM the park fills hard: model yacht racing at Kerbs, two Conservancy tours stepping off at 11, markets at Farmers' Gate and Merchants' Gate, the skate circle at Dead Road North from 2, and 31 private bookings scattered through the scenic spots. Run early and you'll have it to yourself; run at 10 and you'll be weaving.

## Sun Aug 9, 4–8:30 AM — CRCA holds East Drive
**The CRCA Central Park Racing Series has East Drive from 4:00 to 8:30 AM** — club crit racing, a fast tight bunch in the dark and the early light, same as last Sunday. This is not a run-through-it morning on the east side. Take the Bridle Path or the Reservoir until 8:30, or go west and accept the transverse crossings. After that the loop is yours, but it's 88°F with storms building, so the honest advice is to run at 6 on the west side and be done. East Meadow closed again all day; the folkdancers hold King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM to 8 PM, which is a crowd note rather than a route note.

## Quick recap
- **Sun 4–8:30 AM: CRCA crit racing on East Drive.** The only road event of the week. Bridle Path, Reservoir, or west side until 8:30.
- **Tuesday and Wednesday are the clean days** — nothing permitted, best air of the week. Use both.
- **Monday is wet and completely open.** Easy miles, nobody out there.
- **Thu–Sun sit at 87–88°F.** Before 7 AM or don't bother.
- **East Meadow closed Saturday and Sunday.** No strides.
- **Bethesda Terrace stays closed through Sept 2.** Second week of that; pick your new cooldown stop.
- **Rumsey evening crowds Thursday and Friday** — 72nd Street entrance is the pinch, from 5:30.

Have a strong week,
The Central Park Guide
