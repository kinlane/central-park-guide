Hi all,

**Two things.** The parade moved to **tomorrow, Saturday, 8:30 AM** — postponed off Thursday by the nor'easter, the first time weather has moved it in a hundred years. And separately: **there is no loop closure anywhere this week.** Not one race, walk or permit on East, West or Center Drive across all seven days.

## Weather this week

Tomorrow is **51° and partly sunny, wind 10–15 mph**; then **Sunday night into Monday is wet, rain near certain, 55° Monday with a 10–16 mph wind**. After that it is clean and climbing — 50° Tuesday, 52° Wednesday, 59° Thursday, **62° Friday.** Monday is the one to write off; Thursday and Friday are the pick of the week for anything long.

## Sat Nov 28 — the drives are yours, the west sidewalk is not

**The parade runs down Central Park West from W 77th to Columbus Circle from 8:30 AM.** Inside the park nothing is permitted — **the drives, the Reservoir and the bridle path are all open and unaffected.**

What it does hit is access:

- **Every west-side entrance from 77th down to Columbus Circle** is behind the route through the late morning. Come in from the east or from above 86th.
- **The grandstands are on the CPW sidewalks from 65th up through the 70s, and the permit runs until Friday December 5** — so the west sidewalk stays narrowed for a week after the parade is over. If you run the outside of the park, that is your problem for seven more days, not one.
- **Merchants' Gate at Columbus Circle is held 8 AM to 11 PM tomorrow through Monday** as the bleacher work area. Avoid that corner for the whole weekend.

An honest conflict in our own listings: **the Seneca Village Tour meets at 2 PM at Mariner's Gate, W 85th and CPW** — on the parade street. The parade will be well past by two; the grandstands and crowds will not be. Worth knowing before you plan around it.

**Birding Bob at the Boathouse, 7:30 AM** — east side, before any of it starts, and the best way to spend tomorrow morning if you want nothing to do with a parade. **East Meadow closed.**

## Sun Nov 29 — 53° and clear, and the last dry morning before the rain

Nothing on the drives. Mostly sunny, 6 mph wind — **the best riding and running day of the weekend**, and the rain does not arrive until after dark.

**Birding Bob at the Boathouse** 7:30 AM. **Colony HS Band and Choir** at the Bandshell 9–11 AM if your route goes past. **Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–3. East Meadow closed.

## Mon Nov 30 – Tue Dec 1 — write off Monday, take Tuesday

**Monday: 55°, rain near certain, 10–16 mph.** Nothing booked, and nothing worth going out for.

**Tuesday: 50° and sunny, wind under 10, nothing booked anywhere.** Clean and quiet.

**Birding Bob at Strawberry Fields** Monday 8:30. The East 106th Street lawn maintenance runs Mon–Thu — north end, off the drives, no impact.

## Wed Dec 2 – Fri Dec 4 — the week's two good days, and the Meer gets busy

52°, 59°, then **62° on Friday**, all dry. **Thursday and Friday are where the long efforts belong.**

- **Thursday 5:30 PM: the 30th Annual Holiday Lighting at the Dana Discovery Center**, on the Harlem Meer. Free, and worth it even if a tree lighting is not usually your thing — the north end rarely gets a night like this. The permit holds the Dana lawn and the Meer picnic area from Wednesday, so expect setup traffic up there mid-week.
- **Birding Bob: Boathouse Thursday 8:30, Conservatory Garden Friday 8:30.** The Garden on a dry 62° Friday morning is the single best hour in the park this week.
- **Iconic Views tour** Friday 2 PM.

## Worth knowing

- **No loop impact all week** — the first fully clear seven days since early November.
- **Bethesda Terrace, the Swedish Cottage lawn, the E 96th playground lawn and the Harlem Meer picnic area** stay closed all week; East Meadow closed weekends. The feed publishes no end date for any of them. None affects a route you use.
- **Field permits: three, all week.** The season is over, and the North Meadow is effectively yours again.

Have a good week out there,
Kin
