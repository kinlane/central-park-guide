Hi,

**The parade is tomorrow.** The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed on Wednesday and now steps off **Saturday, November 28 at 8:30 AM** — the first weather postponement in its hundred-year history. I wrote to you last week saying Thursday; the nor'easter moved it.

Which means the park's west edge is a parade route tomorrow morning, on a Saturday, with the normal weekend load already on top of it.

## Weather this week

Tomorrow is **51° and partly sunny with wind 10–15 mph** — a good day, and comfortably inside the limits that grounded Thursday. Then **Sunday night into Monday turns wet, with rain near certain and 55° on Monday**, before a clean, warming run: 50° Tuesday, 52° Wednesday, 59° Thursday and 62° Friday.

## Sat Nov 28, 8:30 AM — the parade, and what it actually does to the park

The route runs **Central Park West from West 77th Street south to Columbus Circle**, then down Sixth Avenue. So the park's entire western boundary is the parade, and the southwest corner is its turn.

Three things from the permit record that no parade coverage will tell you:

- **The grandstands are on the Central Park West sidewalks, and they do not come down tomorrow.** The permit covers CPW sidewalks from 65th Street north through the 70s and **runs until Friday, December 5, 5 PM.** Expect narrowed sidewalk along the park's west edge for another week after the parade.
- **Merchants' Gate — the Columbus Circle entrance — is held 8 AM to 11 PM**, tomorrow through Monday, as the bleacher back-of-house. That corner is a work site all weekend, not just during the parade.
- **Over 1,000 extra NYPD officers** are on for Saturday, per the department.

Two of our own listings sit awkwardly against it:

- **The Iconic Views tour goes at 10 AM** from Doris C. Freedman Plaza at Fifth and 60th. That is the park's southeast corner at the height of the parade. It is across the park from the route, but the whole south end will be dense.
- **The Seneca Village Tour meets at 2 PM at Mariner's Gate, W 85th and Central Park West** — on the parade street. The parade will be long gone by two; the grandstands will not be.

Otherwise: **Birding Bob at the Boathouse, 7:30 AM**, well clear of all of it. Frisbee at North Meadow 9–5. **East Meadow closed.**

**On the balloons** — they were deflated Wednesday night and trucked back to New Jersey to be re-prepped in about 24 hours, and Macy's confirmed there was no public second inflation. Tomorrow's wind forecast is well under the threshold that stops them, but I have not seen Macy's say outright that they fly, so treat it as likely rather than settled.

## Sun Nov 29, 9–11 AM — a high school band at the Bandshell

**Colony HS Band and Choir hold Bandshell Plaza, 9 to 11 AM.** Two hours, free, on a 53° mostly sunny morning — the fourth visiting band permit in three weeks.

**International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM. **Birding Bob at the Boathouse** 7:30 AM. East Meadow closed again. Rain arrives overnight.

## Mon Nov 30 – Tue Dec 1 — one wet day, then it clears

**Monday is 55° with rain close to certain and a 10–16 mph wind.** Nothing is booked. **Tuesday is 50° and sunny** with nothing booked either — the better of the two by a wide margin.

**Birding Bob at Strawberry Fields**, Monday 8:30 AM. East 106th Street lawn maintenance runs Mon–Thu.

## Wed Dec 2 – Thu Dec 3 — the holiday lighting at the Harlem Meer

**The 30th Annual Central Park Holiday Lighting is Thursday at 5:30 PM**, at the Dana Discovery Center on the Harlem Meer — Santa, carols on the plaza, hot cocoa and a tree. It is free, it is thirty years old, and it is the north end's one big night of the year.

The permit behind it holds the **Dana Discovery Center Lawn and the Harlem Meer picnic area on both Wednesday and Thursday**, so setup is visible from Wednesday morning.

Also Thursday: **the opening reception for the 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations at the Arsenal, 6–8 PM.** Free.

**The Catholic League crèche display** goes up on the Grand Army Plaza sidewalks, Wednesday through Friday.

## Fri Dec 4 — Wreath Interpretations opens properly

**44th Annual Wreath Interpretations at the Arsenal Gallery**, 9 AM–5 PM, on a 62° day. Four decades of artists reinterpreting the wreath, in the Parks Department's own building at Fifth and 64th. Free, indoors, and reliably strange in the best way.

**Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden**, 8:30 AM. **Iconic Views tour** 2 PM.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is closed every day**, under two overlapping permits. **The Swedish Cottage lawn, the East 96th Street playground lawn and the Harlem Meer picnic area** are closed all week; **East Meadow** is closed weekends. The permit feed publishes no end date for any of them, so I cannot tell you when they come back.
- **Field permits are down to three for the whole week**, against a September peak of 223. The league season is finished.
- **The Great Lawn has its first bookings since July** — four Little League softball permits, all in **April 2027**. Nothing on it before then.

## Quick recap

- **The parade is Saturday at 8:30 AM**, not Thursday. CPW from 77th to Columbus Circle.
- **Grandstands stay on the CPW sidewalks until December 5.**
- **Merchants' Gate is a work site 8 AM–11 PM, Saturday through Monday.**
- **Sunday 9 AM: free high school band and choir at the Bandshell.**
- **Monday is the wet day; Tuesday is the clear one.**
- **Thursday 5:30 PM: the 30th Holiday Lighting at the Harlem Meer.**
- **Friday: Wreath Interpretations opens at the Arsenal**, 62° and free.
