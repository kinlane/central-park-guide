Hi there,

**The parade is tomorrow, not Thursday.** The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed by the nor'easter — the first weather postponement in its hundred-year history — and now steps off **Saturday, November 28 at 8:30 AM** down Central Park West.

And then the park's holiday season begins in earnest: a tree lighting at the Harlem Meer on Thursday, and four decades of wreaths at the Arsenal on Friday.

## Weather this week

Tomorrow is **51° and partly sunny** with a 10–15 mph wind — a genuinely decent parade morning. **Sunday night into Monday is wet**, with rain near certain and 55° on Monday, and then it clears and warms steadily: 50°, 52°, 59° and **62° by Friday**. The back half of the week is the good half.

## Sat Nov 28, 8:30 AM — the parade, and the part about the park

The route is **Central Park West from W 77th Street down to Columbus Circle**, then south on Sixth Avenue. If you are watching, the park side of CPW is the classic spot.

Three details from the permit record worth having:

- **The grandstand seating is on the Central Park West sidewalks** from 65th Street up through the 70s — and that permit **runs until Friday, December 5.** The stands do not come down after the parade; the west sidewalk stays narrowed for another week.
- **Merchants' Gate, the Columbus Circle entrance, is held 8 AM to 11 PM** tomorrow, Sunday and Monday as the bleacher work area.
- **Balloons:** they were deflated Wednesday night and taken back to New Jersey to be re-prepped in roughly 24 hours, and Macy's confirmed there was **no public second inflation night**. Tomorrow's wind is well under the limit that stopped them Thursday, but I have not seen Macy's state plainly that they fly, so I would call it likely rather than certain.

**A note on the two bands I told you about last week.** Pearland High School, from Texas, **raised $350,000** to extend the trip for more than 500 students — hotels, meals and transport — so they stayed. UConn's band left the city and planned to return Friday for the rescheduled parade. The University of Alabama's band went home, with the Iron Bowl on Saturday. Neither Pearland nor UConn has been confirmed in writing as marching tomorrow, so I will not claim it, but the fundraising story is the best thing to come out of this postponement.

In the park itself tomorrow: **Iconic Views tour 10 AM** (southeast corner, busy), **Seneca Village Tour 2 PM from Mariner's Gate at W 85th and CPW** — on the parade street, long after it clears but with the stands still up. Frisbee at North Meadow 9–5. East Meadow closed.

## Sun Nov 29, 9–11 AM — Colony HS Band and Choir at the Bandshell

**Two hours of a visiting high school band and choir at Bandshell Plaza, free**, on a 53° mostly sunny morning. That is the fourth visiting band permit in three weeks, and the only one with a choir attached.

**International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM — open circle, no experience needed. Rain arrives after dark.

## Mon Nov 30 – Tue Dec 1 — a wet Monday, a clear Tuesday

Nothing is booked on either day. **Monday is 55° with rain near certain; Tuesday is 50° and sunny.** If you have one errand in the park this week, make it Tuesday.

## Wed Dec 2 – Thu Dec 3 — the 30th Holiday Lighting at the Harlem Meer

**Thursday at 5:30 PM, the 30th Annual Central Park Holiday Lighting** at the Dana Discovery Center on the Harlem Meer. **Santa, carols on the plaza, hot cocoa, and the tree.** Free. Thirty years running, and the north end's biggest evening of the year — if you have children, this is the week's event.

The permit holds the **Dana Discovery Center Lawn and the Harlem Meer picnic area on Wednesday and Thursday**, so you will see it being built from Wednesday morning.

Also Thursday evening: **the opening reception for the 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations at the Arsenal Gallery, 6–8 PM.** Free.

**The Catholic League crèche display** goes up on the Grand Army Plaza sidewalks, Wednesday through Friday.

## Fri Dec 4, 9 AM–5 PM — 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations opens

**At the Arsenal Gallery**, the Parks Department's own building inside the park at Fifth and 64th. Forty-four years of artists being handed a wreath and asked what else it could be. Free, indoors, and consistently more interesting than it sounds — on a 62° day.

**Iconic Views tour** 2 PM.

## What the permits say

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is closed every day this week**, under two separate overlapping permits with different titles at the same spot. The **Swedish Cottage lawn, the E 96th Street playground lawn and the Harlem Meer picnic area** are closed all week; **East Meadow** is closed weekends.
- **The feed publishes no end date for any of those closures**, so I cannot tell you when they reopen.
- **Field permits: three for the entire week**, against 223 at the September peak. The season has ended.
- **The Great Lawn is booked for the first time since July** — four Little League softball permits, all in **April 2027.**

## Quick recap

- **Parade: Saturday 8:30 AM**, CPW from 77th to Columbus Circle. Not Thursday.
- **The grandstands stay up on the CPW sidewalks until December 5.**
- **Sunday 9–11 AM: free band and choir at the Bandshell.**
- **Tuesday is the clear day; Monday is the wet one.**
- **Thursday 5:30 PM: the 30th Holiday Lighting at the Harlem Meer** — Santa, carols, cocoa, free.
- **Friday: Wreath Interpretations opens at the Arsenal**, free, 62°.
