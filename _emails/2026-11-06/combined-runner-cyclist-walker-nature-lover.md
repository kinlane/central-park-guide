Hi all,

**Seven consecutive sunny days, zero rain, highs 55° to 63°.** This is the week to spend outside. The one thing to plan around is tomorrow morning: **a charity run has a permit on the Upper Loop, 9 AM to noon.**

## Weather this week

Sunny every day — 62°, 61°, 59°, 63°, 61° on Veterans Day, then 55° and 51° — with rain chances at or below 10% all the way through. **Windy Saturday through Monday at 16–20 mph out of the northwest**, which on the open west side of the loop you will feel on every lap.

**Tuesday, 63° and sunny with the wind down, is the long run and the long ride of the week.** Take it then if you can take only one.

## Sat Nov 7, 9 AM–noon — Saving the Elephants Run has the Upper Loop

**The permit reads Bandshell Plaza and the Upper Loop, 9 AM to noon.** That is the northern arc of the loop drive. **Do not count on a clean lap tomorrow morning:** expect coning, staging and foot traffic at the north end, with the Bandshell end of the park used for setup.

Your options, in order:

- **Go before 8:30.** 62° and sunny, and the loop is entirely yours.
- **Use the Bridle Path or the Reservoir** between 9 and noon — both are well clear of it.
- **Ride south.** The lower loop and the 72nd Street transverse are unaffected.

Worth saying plainly: **our own tagging missed this one.** It reached me only because I read the raw permit this morning — the loop flag that would normally force it into this email never fired. I am fixing the cause; meanwhile this paragraph is here by hand.

After noon, Saturday is excellent:

- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-north-woods-tour)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center — waterfalls, the Loch, the best remaining color.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM.
- **East Meadow closed** — the lawn only. East Drive beside it is open.

## Sun Nov 8 — sunny, windy, and a good walking day

**61° and sunny with a steady 16 mph.** No loop impact at all.

- **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM from Belvedere Castle.
- **[Iconic Views of Central Park](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.
- **[Leaf Peeping Hike](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/11/08/leaf-peeping-in-central-park-hike)** with the Urban Park Rangers, 1–2:30 PM from Boys' Gate at West 100th — free, and the north end is at its best right now.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM. East Meadow closed again.

## Mon Nov 9 – Thu Nov 12 — four empty days, all of them sunny

**Nothing is booked. Not one scheduled event in four days**, and the forecast is 59°, 63°, 61°, 55° under full sun with the wind easing after Monday.

**The marathon envelope expires Tuesday the 10th.** It has reported a loop-affecting event every day since the race — that was load-out, and the drives have been open throughout. From Wednesday it is gone and the record matches reality again.

- **Birding Bob**: Strawberry Fields Monday 8:30 AM, Loeb Boathouse Thursday 8:30 AM.
- **Lawn maintenance** at the East 106th Street Lawn, Monday to Thursday — only matters if you cool down up there.
- **[New Dendrology](https://www.nycgovparks.org/art-and-antiquities/arsenal-gallery)** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free, for anyone who wants an hour on trees.

## Fri Nov 13 — the Conservancy's gala, and the week's first clouds

**The CPC Annual Fall-Winter Gala holds Rumsey Playfield and Bandshell Plaza 9 AM to midnight.** The Bandshell end is out all day. **No effect on the drives** — but if you normally loop past Rumsey, there will be trucks.

51° with a slight chance of rain. **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM, and **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden** at 8:30 AM — his best venue, one week in the rotation.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace closed every day, now through December 6.** A thirteenth extension, and there is now a second overlapping closure permit on the same spot from November 19. Route around the arcade.
- **The Great Lawn has its first permit in sixteen weeks** — a picnic, tomorrow. The four-month blank was never a closure after all.
- **Coming: Race to Deliver 4M on Sunday November 22, 8–10 AM.** That one is a genuine loop closure. Diary it now.

Go early tomorrow, and take Tuesday.
