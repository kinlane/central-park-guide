Hi,

Three one-off events in the window and eleven relevant records in total — another very thin week. But two of the standing anomalies I have been reporting to you for months both moved this week, and one of them moved in a way I did not expect.

## Weather this week

Seven consecutive sunny days, rain at or near zero, highs easing from 62° to 51°. Windy Saturday through Monday at 16–20 mph. Pattern-wise: the warm anomaly has broken and we are now running close to normal for mid-November, with no precipitation to speak of.

## The Great Lawn has a permit, for the first time in sixteen weeks

**A picnic, tomorrow, November 7.**

That is the entire event, and it matters only because of what preceded it: **sixteen consecutive weeks in which the Great Lawn held zero permits of any kind, with no closure, maintenance or restoration permit anywhere in the record to account for it.** Every other major lawn that goes out of service files something. This one filed nothing and simply sat empty from July.

**A booking means it was never closed.** Whatever kept the Great Lawn empty for four months, it was not an administrative closure — or the closure ended without a permit marking either end of it. I had been treating the silence as evidence of an unfiled closure. One picnic undercuts that reading, and I would rather tell you I was probably wrong than keep the tidier story.

It is one permit, and it is a picnic, not field play. I will keep counting.

## Bethesda now has two overlapping closure permits

The daily `Closed` run has been **extended a thirteenth time, to December 6** — it was December 1 last week, November 30 before that, and October 13 when it first appeared. Thirty-one consecutive days currently on the books.

**New this week: a second permit series at the same place, titled `Central Park Closure`, running November 19 to December 6.** Eighteen days, overlapping the first series entirely.

Two permits, one location, two different titles, same end date. Three readings and I cannot yet separate them: a correction filed under a new title without the old one being withdrawn; a second, broader closure layered on for a different scope of work; or duplicate filing. The shared December 6 end date is the one real clue — both now expire together, which argues they are describing the same piece of work.

## The marathon envelope expires Tuesday

Four days left. It has flagged a loop-affecting event **every day for ten days after the race finished**, covering all three drives at 00:00–23:59, while the drives were in fact open throughout. From Wednesday the record stops claiming a closure that is not there.

This remains the cleanest illustration of the modelling problem I have raised with you before: **a permit that reserves ground for three weeks and a permit that shuts a road for two hours are indistinguishable in this data.** The Holiday Lighting and New Year permits will arrive in the same shape.

## Our own tagging missed a loop event this week

Worth telling you because you are the one subscriber who would want to know. **Saving the Elephants Run, tomorrow 9 AM–noon, has a permit covering Bandshell Plaza and the Upper Loop** — and it carried no loop-impact flag. It reached my desk because I read the raw permit, not because the system surfaced it.

The cause: the place-name list that recognises loop locations contained `Lower Loop`, which I added in September when a different charity run exposed the same gap, but not `Upper Loop`. I patched the instance instead of the category. There are exactly six loop and drive names in the current feed, so the fix closes it properly this time.

## The rest of the window

- **East Meadow closed** Saturday and Sunday, 00:00–23:59 — the lawn and the East 96th Street Playground.
- **Mon Nov 9 – Thu Nov 12: no one-off events at all.** Lawn maintenance at the East 106th Street Lawn is the only operational item.
- **Fri Nov 13: CPC Annual Fall-Winter Gala**, 9 AM–midnight, holding Rumsey Playfield and Bandshell Plaza. The Conservancy's own fundraiser taking the full SummerStage footprint for fifteen hours.
- **Field permits down to 53** from 71 last week, 41 at North Meadow. Given that I called the season over at 66 and was wrong, I am not calling this one either.
- **Swedish Cottage Lawn closed November 20 – December 6**, 17 straight days — a new series this week, and it shares Bethesda's December 6 end date.

## Quick recap

- **The Great Lawn has a permit after sixteen weeks of nothing.** It was probably never closed, and I was probably over-reading the silence.
- **Bethesda now has two overlapping closure permits**, both ending December 6.
- **The marathon envelope expires Tuesday**, after ten days of flagging a closure that was not there.
- **Our tagging missed a real loop event tomorrow** — `Upper Loop` was not in the place list. Fixing the category, not the instance.
- **Three new multi-week closures all end December 6:** Bethesda, Swedish Cottage, East 96th Street Playground lawn.
