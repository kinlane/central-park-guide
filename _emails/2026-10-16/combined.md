A busy weekend and then the park empties out. **Making Strides Against Breast Cancer holds Bandshell Plaza, Mineral Springs and Rumsey Playfield from midnight to 6 PM on both Saturday and Sunday** — thirty-six hours across the Mall — with a second Making Strides walk at Cedar Hill on Sunday morning. After that, the calendar is nearly bare until Halloween.

## Weather this week

Tonight brings storms at 90%, then Saturday clears to a cool 53° after an early shower, **39° overnight**, and Sunday is sunny and 55°. The week warms gently — 57°, 64°, 63°, 61°, 65° — and stays mostly dry. Proper jacket weather now, and the light is doing the thing it does in late October.

## Sat Oct 17 — Making Strides on the Mall, and Open House New York

**[Making Strides Against Breast Cancer of Central Park](https://centralpark.guide/events/making-strides-against-breast-cancer-of-central-park-2026-10-17/)** holds **Bandshell Plaza, Mineral Springs and Rumsey Playfield, midnight to 6 PM.** Eighteen hours. The Mall and the 72nd Street approaches will carry crowds all day; the drives are not closed, but walkers and cyclists should expect to pick their way through the middle of the park.

Also on, 53° and clearing:

- **Open House New York Weekend** at Grand Army Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM — the city's architecture weekend.
- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 AM. Mid-October is that garden's moment.
- **CPC Yoga** on East Meadow, 11 AM–12 PM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs, 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9–7.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.

East Meadow closed; 27 private ceremony bookings.

## Sun Oct 18 — the Ramble, on a sunny 55° morning

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 AM — Conservancy guides through the Ramble in full autumn colour. On a clear, cold, sunny Sunday in late October this is the best thing the park does all year.

Two Making Strides walks run the same day: the Bandshell permit continues midnight to 6 PM, and the **WICT New York Chapter walk** takes Cedar Hill 8 AM–12 PM. Between them the Mall and the east side are both busy in the morning.

Also: **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Mon Oct 19 – Thu Oct 22 — the quietest stretch of the autumn

**There are no public events in the park Monday through Thursday.** What is booked is soccer training, school programmes and lawn maintenance — routine, invisible, and none of it in anybody's way.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Tuesday, 4–5 PM: a bench dedication for Bruce Rosenfeld** at the Central Park Zoo. Small, public, and the kind of thing the park quietly does all the time.
- **Birding Bob is rotating meeting points** this week — Strawberry Fields Monday, Loeb Boathouse Thursday, the **Conservatory Garden** Friday. Worth checking which before you set out.

Tuesday is the warmest day at 64°, Wednesday 63° and mostly sunny. **Nothing on any drive all week** — if you have been waiting for an empty loop, this is it.

## Fri Oct 23 — the North Woods

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** meets at the Dana Discovery Center at 10 AM — the park's wildest and least-visited end, with a guide. The second woodland walk of the week and the one fewer people know about.

**[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM. 65° and mostly dry.

## Looking ahead — two weeks out

Worth putting in the diary now: **Abbott Dash to the Finish Line 5K on Saturday Oct 31**, 8:45–11 AM, and **the TCS New York City Marathon on Sunday Nov 1**, 8 AM–5 PM with 55,000 runners finishing in the park. That weekend the loop is gone, and it is the biggest two days of the park's year.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** at the Arsenal all seven days — submissions for the winter wreath show.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 16**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free.
- **102 field permits** — 73 at North Meadow, 21 on the tennis courts, 8 at Heckscher. North Meadow is carrying the league calendar almost alone now.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday.

## Quick recap

- **Making Strides holds the Bandshell midnight–6 PM Saturday AND Sunday**, plus a second walk at Cedar Hill Sunday morning.
- **Sunday 10 AM: the Ramble woodland walk** — the best walk of the year, on a sunny 55° morning.
- **Monday to Thursday has no public events at all** and nothing on the drives.
- **Friday 10 AM: the North Woods woodland walk** from Dana Discovery Center.
- **Open House New York, Saturday** at Grand Army Plaza.
- **Diary it: Dash to the Finish Line Oct 31, the Marathon Nov 1.**

Have a good week in the park,
