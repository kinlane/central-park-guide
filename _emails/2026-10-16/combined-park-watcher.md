Last week I reported a four-hour Making Strides walk arriving tagged only
`Family & Community` — this pipeline's catch-all for permits that matched no rule
— and said the question was how charity walks get categorised, not one more
name. **It has happened twice more, on a bigger event, starting tomorrow.**

Then the park goes quiet: no public events at all Monday through Thursday, and
nothing on any drive for seven days.

## Weather this week

Storms tonight, Saturday clearing to 53° with **39° overnight** — the coldest
night of the autumn. Then a dry, gently warming week: 55°, 57°, 64°, 63°, 61°,
65°. Dry weather across a quiet calendar is when restoration work gets done, so
watch for new lawn closures appearing next week.

## Three charity-walk permits, none categorised

| Permit | When | Where | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Making Strides Against Breast Cancer of Central Park | Sat, 00:00–18:00 | Bandshell Plaza, Mineral Springs, 102nd St Cross Drive, Rumsey Playfield | `Family & Community` |
| Making Strides Against Breast Cancer of Central Park | Sun, 00:00–18:00 | same four | `Family & Community` |
| WICT New York Chapter — Making Strides | Sun, 08:00–12:00 | Cedar Hill | `Family & Community` |

Thirty-six hours across the Mall and Rumsey Playfield, plus a separate walk on
Cedar Hill, and **all three carry exactly one tag, which in this pipeline means
"we could not classify this."** None of them reaches the walker, runner,
cyclist or park-watcher interest lists. I have carried all three into this
week's editions by hand.

**Two things I am deliberately not doing.** I am not flagging them as loop
events: the Bandshell permit names the 102nd Street Cross Drive and no park
drive, and cross drives are excluded from the loop rule on purpose, as permits
that stage traffic without closing the loop. Inventing a closure the permit does
not state is the opposite failure from the ones fixed this autumn.

And I am not adding another name pattern. Four of those went in during
September and early October, and they only ever catch races we already know
about. **"Making Strides Against Breast Cancer" contains no race word at all** —
no walk, run, race, 5K or mile — so no keyword rule would have caught it either.
The gap is structural: a charity walk whose title describes a cause rather than
a distance falls straight through to the catch-all. That is a decision about
`TAG_RULES`, and it is yours.

## Mon Oct 19 – Thu Oct 22 — the emptiest stretch since August

**No public events in the park for four consecutive days.** The permit board
holds soccer training, school programmes and lawn maintenance, and nothing else.

This is worth recording as a seasonal marker, not a gap. The park ran festivals,
a triathlon, a four-night film series and a marathon training run across six
weeks; it has now stopped, and the next thing on the calendar is Halloween. The
only public item Monday to Thursday is **a bench dedication for Bruce Rosenfeld
at the Zoo on Tuesday afternoon** — which is the kind of entry that only shows
up when nothing else is competing for the page.

**Nothing touches a drive for all seven days**, for the first time since the
third week of September.
