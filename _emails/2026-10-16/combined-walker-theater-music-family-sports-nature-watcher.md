A full weekend, then the quietest week of the autumn. **Making Strides Against Breast Cancer takes the Bandshell both weekend days**, Open House New York runs Saturday, and Sunday brings the **Ramble woodland walk** — which on a sunny 55° October morning is the best thing on the park's whole calendar. Then Monday through Thursday has nothing public at all.

## Weather this week

Storms tonight, then Saturday clears to 53° with **39° overnight** — the coldest night yet. Sunday is sunny and 55°. The week warms gently to 64° Tuesday and stays dry through Friday. Coats and good light.

## Sat Oct 17 — Making Strides, and Open House New York

**[Making Strides Against Breast Cancer of Central Park](https://centralpark.guide/events/making-strides-against-breast-cancer-of-central-park-2026-10-17/)** holds Bandshell Plaza, Mineral Springs and Rumsey Playfield from midnight to 6 PM. Eighteen hours — expect the Mall to be busy all day.

- **Open House New York Weekend** at Grand Army Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM — the city's architecture weekend, with the park as a site.
- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 AM, behind the scenes with the garden staff. **This is the Conservatory Garden's best fortnight of the year** — if you only do one thing on this list, do this.
- **CPC Yoga** on East Meadow, 11 AM–12 PM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.

East Meadow closed; 27 private ceremony bookings.

## Sun Oct 18 — the Ramble woodland walk

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 AM — Conservancy guides through the Ramble, which is a genuinely serene woodland a few steps from the busiest part of the park, in full autumn colour.

The Bandshell Making Strides permit continues to 6 PM, and a **second Making Strides walk** takes Cedar Hill 8 AM–12 PM, so both the Mall and the east side carry crowds in the morning.

Also: **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Mon Oct 19 – Thu Oct 22 — nothing public, and that is the news

**No public events anywhere in the park Monday through Thursday.** What is booked is soccer training, school programmes and lawn maintenance. After a month of festivals, triathlons and film screenings, the park has simply stopped — and a cool, dry, empty weekday in late October is its own argument for going.

Two things:

- **Tuesday, 4–5 PM: a bench dedication for Bruce Rosenfeld** at the Central Park Zoo. The park does these quietly and constantly; this one is on the public calendar.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** is rotating meeting points — Strawberry Fields Monday, Loeb Boathouse Thursday, the **Conservatory Garden** Friday. Migration is at its tail end.

**New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free, is the indoor standby.

## Fri Oct 23 — the North Woods

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** meets at the Dana Discovery Center at 10 AM. The park's wildest and least-visited end, with a guide — the second woodland walk of the week and much the quieter of the two. 65°.

**[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Looking ahead

**Saturday Oct 31: Abbott Dash to the Finish Line 5K.** **Sunday Nov 1: the TCS New York City Marathon**, finishing in the park with 55,000 runners. The biggest two days of the park's year, two weeks out — worth deciding now whether you want to be there or well away from it.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** at the Arsenal all seven days.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** closed all day, every day — permits now run to **November 16**.
- **102 field permits** — 73 at North Meadow, 21 tennis, 8 at Heckscher.
