Hi wellness seekers — the week opens with its centerpiece for you: a daylong meditation gathering at the Bandshell this Friday. Around it, three mild mornings make for gentle dawn practice before the midweek rain turns the park quiet in a different way.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, then thunderstorms Monday evening and a wet Tuesday and Wednesday before Thursday clears. The dry mornings are yours for sunrise practice; the rainy middle is a good excuse for stillness indoors or a solitary damp walk when it eases.

## Fri Jul 24 — Peace in the Park, and a vigil at dusk
The week's centerpiece: a **Meditation Marathon — Peace in the Park** fills Bandshell Plaza from midday, a long, open, communal sit. Later, at 4 PM, a **multi-faith vigil gathers at Summit Rock**, the park's quiet high point on the west side. Before all of it, the Conservatory Garden opens at 8 AM cool and nearly empty — the best early hour of the week.

## Sat Jul 25 — find the edges
82°F and sunny, and the park fills — markets, a festival at Rumsey, weekend crowds. For solitude, keep to the **North Woods, the Ravine, and Hallett Nature Sanctuary**; let the busy south-central core have the crowds.

## Sun Jul 26 — go south and west
84°F and sunny. A race draws people to the north end before mid-morning, so the calm is on the other side today — the **Ramble, Shakespeare Garden, and the Pond at Hallett** stay contemplative. Move slowly and let the north empty out.

## Mon Jul 27 — sunrise before the heat
86°F and building toward evening storms. **Sunrise on Summit Rock or Cedar Hill** is the window — practice early, before the warmth and the afternoon instability arrive.

## Tue Jul 28 — a day for stillness indoors
Showers and thunderstorms all day. A natural rest day. Note that the Bethesda Arcade — a favorite covered, resonant space — is closed all week, so keep your sheltered practice at home or under the Met's colonnade instead.

## Wed Jul 29 — the garden after rain
More showers, 83°F. When it eases, the **Conservatory Garden after rain** is yours alone — wet stone, dripping hedges, no one there. A quiet, restorative window between storms.

## Thu Jul 30 — the clearest dawn of the week
Mostly sunny, 84°F, the air washed clean. The **Reservoir at sunrise** is the best light of the week — a long, open horizon and cool, still air. Close the week there.

## Quick recap
- **Fri — Meditation Marathon: Peace in the Park** at Bandshell Plaza (from midday) + a **multi-faith vigil at Summit Rock** (4 PM).
- **Dry weekend dawns** are for sunrise practice — Summit Rock, Cedar Hill, the Conservatory Garden.
- **Sat & Sun the crowds are south-central and north** — find solitude in the North Woods, Ravine, and Ramble.
- **Bethesda Arcade closed all week** — shelter elsewhere on the wet days.
- **Thursday's clear dawn at the Reservoir** is the week's best light.
