Hi families — the weekend is made for you: dry, sunny, and full of free things to do, from Saturday-morning storytelling to the model sailboats on Conservatory Water. The rain holds off until Monday evening, so front-load the outdoor plans.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, then thunderstorms Monday evening and a wet Tuesday and Wednesday before Thursday clears. Do the big outdoor days over the weekend, keep the umbrellas handy for Monday's evening movie, and treat Tuesday as an indoor day.

## Fri Jul 24 — an easy park day
Sunny and 83°F. Nothing scheduled for kids specifically, but Gottesman Pool up at the Harlem Meer is open for the morning, and it's a fine, mild day for the playgrounds and a picnic.

## Sat Jul 25 — the big morning: storytelling and sailboats
82°F and sunny — the best family day of the week. **Storytelling in Central Park** gathers at the Hans Christian Andersen statue by Conservatory Water at 10 AM, the classic Saturday reading for younger kids. Right beside it, **model yacht racing** runs on Conservatory Water from 9 AM at Kerbs Boathouse — kids love watching the little boats tack across the pond.

## Sun Jul 26 — up to the Harlem Meer
84°F and sunny. **Summer on the Harlem Meer** at the Dana Discovery Center from 9 AM is the day's family programming — shoreline nature and activities at the park's northern lake. Heads-up if you're driving or biking up: a running race crowds the north end near 102nd Street before 10 AM, so give the northwest a wide berth early.

## Mon Jul 27 — Pinocchio by day, a movie after dark
86°F, with storms possible by evening. **Pinocchio in the Park** plays the Great Lawn at 11 AM — outdoor kids' theater — and there's a **free movie on the Great Lawn** after sunset. Watch the forecast for the evening show; the storm risk could push it.

## Tue Jul 28 — an indoor day
Showers and thunderstorms all day. A good day for a museum on the park's edge rather than the playgrounds.

## Wed Jul 29 — showery, ball games up north
More showers, 83°F. Universal Kids Soccer runs on the East 106th Street lawn and there's an annual softball event at Heckscher — outdoor, so weather-dependent.

## Thu Jul 30 — clearing, back-to-school field day
Mostly sunny, 84°F. A CRF back-to-school **field day at Mineral Springs** kicks off at 1 PM — games and activities as the summer starts to turn toward fall.

## Quick recap
- **Sat is the day:** storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue (10 AM) and model-boat racing on Conservatory Water (from 9 AM).
- **Sun:** Summer on the Harlem Meer at the Dana Discovery Center (9 AM).
- **Mon:** Pinocchio in the Park on the Great Lawn (11 AM) + a free evening movie — watch for storms.
- **Thu:** back-to-school field day at Mineral Springs (1 PM).
- **Weekend is dry and sunny; Tuesday is a wet indoor day.**

See you in the park,
