Hi park watchers — the calendar's rhythm this week is a strong dry weekend, a rained-out middle, and one unusual operational note: the park's centerpiece terrace is closed for the full seven days. SummerStage keeps its steady Rumsey cadence underneath it all.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, then thunderstorms Monday evening, a wet Tuesday, and a showery Wednesday before Thursday clears. No heat wave — comfortable midsummer warmth throughout — and this reads as the last fully dry weekend before an unsettled stretch.

## The weekend anchor — Team Championships 5M
Sunday's **NYRR Team Championships 5M** (7:30 AM, 102nd Street Cross Drive, ~5,000 club runners) is the week's marquee athletic event — a double-points Club Points Series race that stages the whole north end from 6 AM. It's the recurring summer touchstone for the club-running calendar.

## The steady beat — SummerStage at Rumsey
SummerStage runs all week at Rumsey Playfield: festival days Saturday and Sunday, the marquee **Julieta Venegas / Lasso / Micro TDH** bill Wednesday at 6 PM, and free concerts continuing on the Great Lawn Thursday. The Bandshell adds its own thread — a community concert Saturday, a high-school choir Monday, and a notable milestone Tuesday: **National Dance Institute's 50th Anniversary "Runs and Leaps."**

## Operational — Bethesda closed, and a maintenance rotation
The week's standout operational item: **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain are closed all seven days** — unusual for a marquee landmark in peak season, worth watching for what it signals (likely stonework or event prep). Alongside it, a rolling maintenance schedule takes lawns offline in sequence — Cherry Hill and the Dairy Friday, Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill Monday, Bowling Green Thursday — and the **East Meadow is closed the full weekend.**

## Pattern note
Private-event volume is high and routine — dozens of picnics, weddings, and celebrations across Cop Cot, Ladies' Pavilion, Wagner Cove, and the Great Lawn, the normal summer-weekend baseline. Youth and adult sport permits run 40–70 a day, peaking Sunday. Nothing anomalous in the mix beyond the Bethesda closure and the dance-institute milestone.
