Hi sports fans — a full slate. The fields are booked heavy all week, the Conservatory Water regatta sails Saturday, and Sunday brings the marquee race: the NYRR Team Championships on the loop. The weekend's dry; the midweek rain will scrub some of Tuesday and Wednesday's games.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, then thunderstorms Monday evening and a wet Tuesday and Wednesday before Thursday clears. The weekend games are safe; expect Tuesday's card and some of Wednesday's to wash out.

## Fri Jul 24 — a normal busy Friday
Sunny, 83°F. About **41 sport permits** across the South Dead Road, the Great Lawn, and the North Meadow — softball, soccer training, and the CP Sports summer program running its usual daytime blocks. Nothing unusual, just full fields.

## Sat Jul 25 — the big field day, plus the regatta
82°F and sunny — the busiest fields of the week at roughly **53 permits.** Two to watch beyond the leagues: **model-yacht racing** on Conservatory Water from 9 AM at Kerbs Boathouse, and the **Central Park Skate Circle** rolling at Dead Road at 2 PM. The East Meadow is closed all weekend, so games shift off it.

## Sun Jul 26 — Team Championships 5M, and the fullest card
The week's headline: the **NYRR Team Championships 5M** at 7:30 AM, ~5,000 club runners on the full loop from the 102nd Street Cross Drive, double points in the Club Points Series. Underneath it, the day carries the week's **heaviest permit load — around 70** — plus the Skate Circle again at 2 PM. East Meadow still closed.

## Mon Jul 27 — full slate, storm risk late
86°F with thunderstorms possible by evening; about **52 permits** on the books, though the late games are the ones at risk. Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill go offline for maintenance.

## Tue Jul 28 — rain scrubs the card
Showers and thunderstorms all day. Some **59 permits** are scheduled, but expect the fields to sit — a washout more than a game day.

## Wed Jul 29 — an annual softball event at Heckscher
More showers, 83°F. The **2026 Annual Softball Event takes Heckscher Fields** from 11 AM — a marked tournament rather than a routine league block — and Universal Kids Soccer runs up on the East 106th Street lawn. Weather-dependent.

## Thu Jul 30 — drying out, Bowling Green offline
Mostly sunny, 84°F, and back to a full slate around **51 permits.** One note for the lawn-bowling crowd: **Bowling Green Lawn is closed for maintenance**, so that's out today.

## Quick recap
- **Sun — Team Championships 5M**, ~5,000 runners on the full loop from 102nd; the day also carries ~70 field permits.
- **Sat regatta:** model-yacht racing on Conservatory Water (9 AM); Skate Circle at Dead Road (Sat & Sun, 2 PM).
- **Wed — 2026 Annual Softball Event at Heckscher** (11 AM), a tournament block.
- **Tuesday washes out**; Monday's and Wednesday's late games are storm-risky.
- **East Meadow closed the weekend; Bowling Green closed Thursday.**
