Hi music fans — a rich, varied week: a park-institution guitarist on the Lake, tango on Literary Walk, a Latin-alt-pop marquee at SummerStage, and free concerts bookending it. The weekend and Thursday are dry; the midweek shows carry a rain asterisk.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, then thunderstorms Monday evening and a wet Tuesday and Wednesday before Thursday clears. The outdoor weekend programming is safe; keep an eye on the sky for Tuesday's Bandshell dance and Wednesday's Rumsey headliner.

## Fri Jul 24 — a quiet, meditative note
No concert today, but Bandshell Plaza hosts a midday Peace in the Park meditation gathering — ambient rather than a performance, but the Bandshell in a contemplative mood is its own kind of listening.

## Sat Jul 25 — guitar on the Lake, tango on Literary Walk
Three to catch. **That Guitar Man From Central Park** plays Hernshead on the Lake at noon — a beloved fixture. A community **concert fills Bandshell Plaza at 2 PM.** And at 6 PM, **tango under the Shakespeare statue** on Literary Walk, live music and dancers on the mall.

## Sun Jul 26 — steppers and folk dance
**The Central Park International Folkdancers** gather at King Jagiello Plaza by Turtle Pond at 11 AM, and there's a **steppers performance and dance demo at Summit Rock** at 1:30 PM — percussion and movement on the park's high west-side outcrop.

## Mon Jul 27 — young voices at the Bandshell
A **high school choir concert** takes Bandshell Plaza at 5 PM. Storms are possible by evening, so it may run early or move — worth checking before you head over.

## Tue Jul 28 — a 50-year dance milestone
**National Dance Institute's 50th Anniversary "Runs and Leaps"** fills Bandshell Plaza at noon — a landmark for the NYC arts-education institution. It's a wet day, so confirm before you go.

## Wed Jul 29 — the week's marquee: Julieta Venegas at Rumsey
**Julieta Venegas, Lasso, Micro TDH, and DJ Anamaria Sayre** headline SummerStage at Rumsey Playfield at 6 PM — the Latin alt-pop centerpiece of the week. Doors and the queue build from about 5:30; showers are in the forecast, so bring a layer.

## Thu Jul 30 — a free double bill
Two free shows to close the week: a **salsa community event at the Dana Discovery Center** on the Harlem Meer at 4 PM, and **free concerts on the Great Lawn** at 7 PM as the weather clears.

## Quick recap
- **Wed Jul 29, 6 PM — Julieta Venegas / Lasso / Micro TDH at Rumsey (SummerStage)** — the marquee.
- **Sat:** That Guitar Man at Hernshead (noon), Bandshell concert (2 PM), tango at the Shakespeare statue (6 PM).
- **Tue:** National Dance Institute's 50th at Bandshell Plaza (noon) — rain caveat.
- **Thu free double bill:** salsa at Dana Discovery Center (4 PM), Great Lawn concerts (7 PM).
- **Weekend and Thursday are dry; Tuesday and Wednesday shows carry rain risk.**
