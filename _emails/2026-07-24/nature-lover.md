Hi nature lovers — a two-part week. Three calm, dry mornings to start, when the Ramble and North Woods are quiet before the crowds; then a wet middle that will send the Loch and the Ravine streams running hard by Thursday. The Harlem Meer is the place to be up north this weekend.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, then thunderstorms Monday evening and a soaking Tuesday and Wednesday before Thursday clears. Take the dry weekend dawns for the woodland quiet, and let the midweek rain do its work — the water features are at their best in the day or two after.

## Fri Jul 24 — a quiet, comfortable dawn
Sunny, 83°F. Before 8 AM the Ramble and North Woods are still and cool, the dawn chorus thinning now into midsummer but the herons active at the Lake and Turtle Pond. A gentle morning to move slowly.

## Sat Jul 25 — sailboats on Conservatory Water
82°F and sunny. **Model yacht racing** runs on Conservatory Water from 9 AM at Kerbs Boathouse — a slow, absorbing spectacle if you like the pond and its fringe of red-eared sliders. The Ramble stays quiet if you go early, before the weekend fills.

## Sun Jul 26 — Summer on the Harlem Meer
84°F and sunny. **Summer on the Harlem Meer** at the Dana Discovery Center from 9 AM is the north end's shoreline morning — turtles, egrets, and the Meer's quiet water. It's the calmest corner of the park on a busy Sunday; the mid-park core will be far busier.

## Mon Jul 27 — early, before the heat and storms
86°F and building toward evening thunderstorms. The morning is the window — the Hallett Nature Sanctuary and the Pool's north-end shade before it turns warm and unsettled.

## Tue Jul 28 — rain does the work
Showers and thunderstorms all day. Not a walking day, but this is the rain the streams have been waiting for — expect the **Loch and the Ravine to run strong** once it passes.

## Wed Jul 29 — showery, and a school casts at the Meer
More showers, 83°F. A middle-school group fishes the Harlem Meer mid-morning in the catch-and-release program — a reminder that the Meer's a good, gentle fishing stop all week. Turtle Pond and the Meer are lively after the warm rain.

## Thu Jul 30 — clear air, running water
Mostly sunny, 84°F, the air washed clean and the light sharp. The **Loch waterfalls in the North Woods** are worth the walk now, at their fullest after two days of rain. The best woodland morning of the week.

## Quick recap
- **Sat:** model-yacht racing on Conservatory Water (from 9 AM).
- **Sun:** Summer on the Harlem Meer at the Dana Discovery Center (9 AM) — the quietest corner on a busy day.
- **Dry weekend dawns** are the woodland windows; the mid-park core is busy midday.
- **Tuesday's rain feeds the streams** — Thursday's Loch and Ravine waterfalls are the payoff.
- **Thursday clears** to the best woodland morning of the week.
