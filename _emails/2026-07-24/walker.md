Hi walkers — this is a lovely weekend to be out, dry and mild three days running. The one thing to know before you set off: **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain are closed all week**, so the park's centerpiece stop is off the table through next Thursday. Plan your routes around the Lake path and the Mall instead.

## Weather this week
Sunny and low-to-mid 80s Friday through Sunday, turning unsettled midweek — thunderstorms Monday evening, a wet Tuesday, and a showery Wednesday before Thursday clears. Save your longer wanders for the weekend and Thursday; Tuesday is an umbrella-or-skip day.

## Fri Jul 24 — sunny, and the best walking of the week
83°F and sunny. With Bethesda closed, loop the Lake from Bow Bridge to Cherry Hill and up the west side instead — the light on the water is lovely by late afternoon. Cherry Hill itself is closed for maintenance, and there's a gentle Peace in the Park meditation gathering filling Bandshell Plaza at midday if you want to pause.

## Sat Jul 25 — music worth stopping for
82°F and sunny. Two on-route moments: **That Guitar Man From Central Park** plays Hernshead on the Lake at noon — a park institution — and there's **tango under the Shakespeare statue** on Literary Walk at 6 PM, worth lingering over. Food markets sit at Merchants' Gate and Farmers' Gate. A heads-up for your favorite quiet corners: Ladies' Pavilion, Cop Cot, and Wagner Cove are all booked for ceremonies through the day, so wander the Ramble and North Woods instead.

## Sun Jul 26 — south and west stay calm
84°F and sunny. One crowd note: a running race takes the north loop 7:30–10 AM with crowds near the 102nd Street Cross Drive, so keep to the south half or head out after 10:30. The Central Park International Folkdancers gather at King Jagiello Plaza by the Turtle Pond at 11 AM — a nice thing to pass.

## Mon Jul 27 — morning walk, then storms
86°F with thunderstorms possible by evening — walk in the morning. Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill close for maintenance, so keep to the west side or the mid-park paths on the east.

## Tue Jul 28 — wet all day
Showers and thunderstorms. An umbrella-and-empty-paths day if you love those, or a day to stay in. Bethesda is still closed, so it's no loss to the route.

## Wed Jul 29 — showery and quiet
More showers, 83°F. The rain keeps the park to yourself if you don't mind getting damp; the Ramble under a canopy is at its most private.

## Thu Jul 30 — clearing, lovely again
Mostly sunny, 84°F, the air washed clean. A fine day to walk long. Bowling Green closes for maintenance, so skip that corner of the southwest.

## Quick recap
- **Bethesda Terrace & Fountain closed all week** — route via the Lake path and the Mall instead.
- **Fri, Sat, Sun are the walking days** — dry, sunny, mild.
- **Sat on-route music:** That Guitar Man at Hernshead (noon), tango at the Shakespeare statue (6 PM).
- **Sun:** a race crowds the north loop before 10:30 — keep south.
- **Tuesday is wet**, Wednesday showery; **Thursday clears.**

Enjoy the week,
The Central Park Guide
