*Friday's email ran through the 18th, so the front half here will be familiar — the new news is next weekend.*

The thing you could not have known on Friday: NYRR's marathon training series puts an **18-mile run on the Ted Corbitt Loop next Sunday, 7 AM to noon**. Five hours of runners on the road is the longest single occupation since spring. Before that, Tuesday through Thursday deliver the best weather of the month with nothing permitted anywhere.

## Weather this week

Monday shakes off a damp start to reach 74°, then Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday run sunny at 72°, 78° and 83° with no rain in them at all. Shower chances return Friday at 20% and build across the weekend to 37% Sunday. Put your long effort midweek — that is a clearer road and better air than next Sunday will offer.

## Mon Sep 14 – Wed Sep 16 — the week, if you only get three days

Nothing on any drive, nothing on a path you use. A dance program at the Bandshell Monday evening, a concert at Rumsey Tuesday night, a mobile health unit up at Central Park North Wednesday. Bowling Green closed all day Tuesday, East 106th Street lawn out Monday through Thursday — neither on your line.

**Tuesday and Wednesday are the pick.** 72° and 78°, sunny, dry, empty. This is the combination the summer never gave you.

## Thu Sep 17 – Fri Sep 18 — warmer, then damp

Thursday hits 83° and stays mostly sunny; Friday holds 80° with a 20% shower chance. Friday morning brings **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** to Belvedere Castle at 10 — fall migration is at its peak now — and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 from Mariners' Gate.

## Sat Sep 19 — clear road, ordinary Saturday crowds

76°, slight shower chance, and nothing on the drives. The **Osborn Cup** takes Bowling Green 7 AM–6 PM both weekend days — lawn bowls, a genuinely odd and lovely thing to walk past. The **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** goes at 2 from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.

East Meadow is closed, and the usual weekend load fills Heckscher and North Meadow. Your morning is free.

## Sun Sep 20, 7 AM–noon — the loop belongs to the 18-milers

**[The TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 18M](https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-18m)** holds the Ted Corbitt Loop from 7 AM until noon. This is the second and longer leg of NYRR's marathon build-up: a large field going around repeatedly for five hours.

**Cyclists: do not plan a Sunday morning ride.** Go before 7, after noon, or take the day. Runners — you can join the flow or work around it, but the drives will be dense the entire morning and there is a 37% shower chance over it.

Afternoon is yours again: the **International Folkdancers** hold King Jagiello 11–6, and jazz plays the Bandshell 1–4 PM if you want somewhere to end a walk.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is still under an all-day "Closed" permit — and we can finally see how long. The permits run unbroken from today through **October 13**, 31 more days, with no reason given anywhere. Route your walks around the Terrace for another month.

[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/) runs five mornings from Strawberry Fields at 7:30 and 8:30, $10 at the end of the walk.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday 7 AM–noon: 18-mile training run owns the loop.** Ride before 7 or after 12.
- **Tuesday and Wednesday are the week:** 72–78°, sunny, dry, nothing booked.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed through October 13** — now with an end date, still no reason.
- **Birding Basics Friday 10 AM** at Belvedere, peak fall migration.
- **Saturday is clear on the drives**; Osborn Cup on Bowling Green both days.

Take the midweek,
Central Park Guide
