*Friday's email ran through the 18th, so the front half will look familiar — next weekend is the new material.*

Next Sunday afternoon is the one to circle: a marionette **Pinocchio** at the Swedish Cottage, **jazz at the Bandshell**, and open chess for anyone who wants to sit down and play — all within a few hundred yards of each other. Before that, Tuesday through Thursday bring the best weather of the month, and Danny Elfman plays Rumsey on Tuesday night.

## Weather this week

Monday clears to 74° after a damp start, then Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are sunny and dry at 72°, 78° and 83°. Shower chances return Friday and build through next weekend to 37% on Sunday — so the outdoor afternoon programming on the 20th is worth checking before you set out.

## Mon Sep 14 – Wed Sep 16 — quiet, clear, and one big concert

**Dance Is Life** takes Bandshell Plaza Monday 5–7 PM, with a **Starlight Dinner** at Naturalists' Gate 6–9 PM. Tuesday brings **[Danny Elfman](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/danny-elfman/)** to Rumsey Playfield at 8:30 PM — the composer of Batman, Beetlejuice and the Simpsons theme, outdoors in the park, which is the most distinctive booking of the month. Bowling Green is closed all day Tuesday.

Wednesday is sunny, 78°, and nearly empty: a mobile health unit at Central Park North, a corporate team-building session at Cleopatra's Needle.

## Thu Sep 17 — New York Sings Yiddish, finally

**[New York Sings Yiddish](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/new-york-sings-yiddish-26/)** plays Rumsey 7–10 PM — YidLife Crisis, Yair Keydar, Riki Rose and Daniella Rabbani, rescheduled all the way from a June storm. A one-off, and free.

## Fri Sep 18 — a sold-out closer and a school picnic

**[Yeat](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/yeat/)** at Rumsey 8–10 PM, sold out and twice moved. Earlier: **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10, the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2, the **Osborn Cup** starting its two-day run on Bowling Green, and a Grade 8 picnic filling the Heckscher lawns 10–2.

## Sat Sep 19 — model yachts, a guitar man, and tango

- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM, on Conservatory Water.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.
- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM; **Central Park Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7 PM.
- **Tango at the Shakespeare Statue**, 6–9 PM.
- **Osborn Cup** on Bowling Green, 7 AM–6 PM — lawn bowls, and a real curiosity.

76°, slight shower chance. East Meadow closed.

## Sun Sep 20 — the best afternoon of the month

The morning belongs to runners: NYRR's **[18-mile marathon training run](https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-18m)** holds the loop 7 AM–noon, so the drives are dense and the paths are busy. Come after lunch instead, when four good things happen at once:

- **Pinocchio in the Park** at the **Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre**, 2–3 PM — the park's own puppet theatre, and the rare indoor option if the showers arrive.
- **Justin Lees Jazz Group** at Bandshell Plaza, 1–4 PM.
- **[Chess & Checkers House Community Play](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/chess-checkers-house-community-play)** from 1 PM — casual games, first-timers explicitly welcome.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11–6, **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7, and a **Society Strong workout social** on the Adventure Playground Lawn 3:30–6.

37% shower chance, so have the Marionette Theatre as your fallback.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — the unexplained all-day closures now run unbroken to **October 13**. A month more, and still no stated reason.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Strawberry Fields.
- **CP Sports Summer Program** at South Dead Road, Monday–Friday.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday afternoon is the week:** Pinocchio at the Marionette Theatre 2 PM, jazz at the Bandshell 1–4, open chess from 1.
- **Danny Elfman at Rumsey Tuesday, 8:30 PM.**
- **Tuesday–Thursday are the month's best weather** — sunny, 72–83°.
- **New York Sings Yiddish Thursday**, rescheduled from June.
- **Sunday morning the loop belongs to an 18-mile training run** — come after noon.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed through October 13.**

Enjoy the turn into fall,
