Hi all,

**The marathon is done and the loop is clear — every day this week.** No race, no permit, no closure on any drive from Monday through Sunday, which has not been true since the end of September. It lands alongside 65°, 67° and 68° under full sun. Midweek is where the whole week is.

## Weather this week

Mostly sunny and far too warm for November through Wednesday — **65°, 67°, 68°** — then light rain Thursday through Saturday before Sunday clears to a breezy 63°. **Front-load everything into Monday through Wednesday**; by Friday it is 69% rain and you will be choosing between wet and indoors.

## Mon Nov 2 – Thu Nov 5 — nothing on the calendar at all

Four days, **zero scheduled events.** Not a race, not a ride, not a tour.

One thing worth knowing so it does not alarm you: **the marathon's permit runs on paper through November 10**, covering East Drive, West Drive, Center Drive and the 102nd Street Cross Drive. That is the load-out — fencing, trailers and the finish-line structure coming down along the west side and around Tavern on the Green. **The roads themselves are open.** Expect a crew and a truck, not a barrier.

- **Wednesday, 68° and sunny, is the ride of the month.** If you have been waiting for a long one, take it then.
- **Runners:** the loop is yours and so is the Reservoir. No course traffic, no cones, nothing closed.
- **Lawn maintenance** at the East 106th Street Lawn, Monday through Thursday — worth knowing only if you cool down up there.
- **[Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** at Strawberry Fields Monday 8:30 AM, Loeb Boathouse Thursday 8:30 AM.
- **[New Dendrology](https://www.nycgovparks.org/art-and-antiquities/arsenal-gallery)** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free — a tree show, and a decent use of the first wet hour Thursday.

## Fri Nov 6 — the rain arrives, the walks go anyway

69° and **light rain likely.** Two Conservancy walks run regardless:

- **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM from Belvedere Castle. Wet woodland is better woodland — the birdsong carries and nobody else turns up.
- **[Iconic Views of Central Park](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.
- **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden**, 8:30 AM. The best stop in his rotation and the one week he is there.

## Sat Nov 7 — the North Woods, and a lawn closure that is not a road closure

65° with showers near 47%.

- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-north-woods-tour)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center. Waterfalls, the Loch, peak color.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.
- **East Meadow is closed** for maintenance and restoration. **This is the lawn only** — East Drive alongside it is open and running and riding are unaffected. I flag it because the permit reads "closure" and it is easy to misread.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM.
- Eighteen private ceremonies booked, two of them in Shakespeare Garden. Wander elsewhere if you want the garden to yourself.

## Sun Nov 8 — the best day of the weekend

**63°, mostly sunny, breezy out of the northwest.** Clearly the day to be out.

- **[Leaf Peeping Hike](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/11/08/leaf-peeping-in-central-park-hike)** with the Urban Park Rangers, 1–2:30 PM from Boys' Gate at West 100th Street. Free, and well-timed for the north end's color.
- **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)** 10 AM, **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** 2 PM.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM — back to their full four hours now the marathon is out of the way.
- **East Meadow closed** again; the lawn, not the drive.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is closed every day, now through December 1.** The permits have been extended twelve times running. Route around the arcade.
- **No play on the Great Lawn for a fifteenth week.**
- **North Meadow has 56 field bookings over the weekend** — relevant only if you cool down up there on Saturday or Sunday morning.

A clear loop, 68° and an empty calendar is about as good as this park gets. Take Wednesday.

Have a strong week,
Kin
