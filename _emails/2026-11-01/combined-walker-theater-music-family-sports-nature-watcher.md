Hi there,

The marathon is behind us, and the park has gone remarkably quiet. **Monday through Thursday have no scheduled events at all** — four consecutive empty days, which I do not think I have sent you before — and then **Sunday arrives with six things at once.** The whole week is shaped like that: nothing, then everything.

## Weather this week

Sunny and unseasonably mild through Wednesday — **65°, 67°, 68°** — then rain Thursday through Saturday, clearing to a bright 63° Sunday. **Save the walking for midweek and the Sunday cluster**, and plan Friday and Saturday around a roof.

## Mon Nov 2 – Thu Nov 5 — the quietest stretch of the year

Nothing is booked. Two or three private ceremonies a day and otherwise an empty calendar.

What is still worth the trip:

- **[New Dendrology](https://www.nycgovparks.org/art-and-antiquities/arsenal-gallery)** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free. A show about trees, indoors, which makes it the right call when Thursday turns wet.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — Strawberry Fields Monday 8:30 AM, Loeb Boathouse Thursday 8:30 AM.
- **Walk the drives.** They reopened Monday after the race and nothing is closed on them all week. 68° and sunny on Wednesday with the park near-empty is a rare combination.

## Fri Nov 6 — two Conservancy walks, in the rain

69° with light rain likely at 69%. Both walks go:

- **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM from Belvedere Castle.
- **[Iconic Views of Central Park](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.
- **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden**, 8:30 AM — the formal garden, once a rotation, and the best of his stops.

## Sat Nov 7 — model yachts, the North Woods, and eighteen weddings

65° with showers around 47%.

- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM. Radio-controlled sailboats on Conservatory Water, and children watch it for far longer than you would expect.
- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-north-woods-tour)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center — waterfalls, the Loch, the best color in the park.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, 2 PM from Mariners' Gate. The African-American community that lived here before the park did.
- **Eighteen private bookings**, the busiest ceremony day in over a month — two in Shakespeare Garden, several around Heckscher. Lovely to walk past, impossible to have to yourself.
- **Fifteen soccer bookings at North Meadow** plus frisbee and kickball at Heckscher. The leagues have come back up after two thin weeks.
- **East Meadow is closed** for maintenance.

## Sun Nov 8 — everything at once

**63° and mostly sunny — the clear pick of the weekend.**

- **[Leaf Peeping in Central Park](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/11/08/leaf-peeping-in-central-park-hike)**, 1–2:30 PM from Boys' Gate at West 100th Street. Urban Park Rangers, free, no registration, and timed for the north end at its best.
- **Animal Rescue Red Carpet** at Literary Walk, 10 AM–noon. Adoptable dogs on the Mall under the elms — a good hour with children.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM. Open circle, no experience wanted, back to full length now the marathon has cleared out.
- **NYMS Mushroom Research Meeting**, East 102nd Street Landscape, 10 AM–3 PM. The New York Mycological Society doing actual fieldwork, and they welcome onlookers.
- **[The Ramble walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)** 10 AM and **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** 2 PM again.
- **Eleven more soccer and frisbee bookings** at North Meadow; East Meadow closed.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain — closed all day, every day, now through December 1.** That is the twelfth extension since mid-October. It has now swallowed all of November.
- **No play on the Great Lawn for a fifteenth straight week**, with no closure permit anywhere in the record to account for it.
- **Field permits: 71 this week**, 56 at North Meadow.
- **Nothing on a stage.** No theater, no concerts, nothing at the Delacorte. The next music of any kind is the Carmel High School Jazz Band on **November 16** — I will have detail nearer the time.

## Quick recap

- **Monday to Thursday is empty** — four days, no events, and 65°, 67°, 68° of sun. Walk midweek.
- **Sunday is the day:** leaf peeping, folkdancers, rescue dogs and mushrooms, all between 10 and 3.
- **Saturday: model yachts at Kerbs, the North Woods walk, and eighteen weddings.**
- **Rain Thursday through Saturday**, clearing Sunday.
- **Bethesda is closed into December.** No stage events anywhere until November 16.

Enjoy the quiet,
Kin
