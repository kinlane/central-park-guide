The marathon is behind us and **the drives are open again.** What follows is the quietest week Central Park has on its books all year — Monday through Thursday have **not a single one-off event between them** — and it arrives at 65°, 67° and 68° under full sun. If you have been waiting for the park to yourself, this is it.

## Weather this week

Mostly sunny and absurdly mild through Wednesday — **65°, 67°, 68°** — then rain settles in Thursday and stays through Saturday, with Sunday clearing to 63°. Take Monday through Wednesday; whatever you were saving for the weekend, do it midweek instead.

## Mon Nov 2 – Thu Nov 5 — the drives are back, and nothing is booked

**The roads are yours.** The marathon's permit envelope runs on paper through November 10, so expect fencing and trailers still coming down along the west side and around Tavern on the Green — but that is cleanup crews, not a closure. **No drive is shut any day this week.**

And the public calendar is genuinely empty. Four days, zero scheduled events. Two or three private bookings a day and that is all.

- **Mon–Wed is the best riding and running of the month.** 65°, 67°, 68°, dry, no race traffic, no permits on the loop. Go long on Wednesday if you can only pick one.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — Strawberry Fields Monday 8:30, Loeb Boathouse Thursday 8:30.
- **[New Dendrology](https://www.nycgovparks.org/art-and-antiquities/arsenal-gallery)** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free. A good rainy-Thursday hour.
- **Lawn maintenance** at the East 106th Street Lawn, Monday through Thursday.

Thursday brings 70° with a 50% chance of rain — the warmest day of the week and the first wet one.

## Fri Nov 6 — two Conservancy walks before the rain sets in

Friday is 69° and **light rain likely at 69%.** The walks run anyway:

- **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)**, 10 AM from Belvedere Castle.
- **[Iconic Views of Central Park](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 2 PM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.
- **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden**, 8:30 AM — the best venue in his rotation and the one worth the trip uptown.

## Sat Nov 7 — eighteen private bookings and the North Woods

65° with showers around 47%. **Eighteen private bookings**, the busiest ceremony day in weeks — two at Shakespeare Garden, the rest scattered through Heckscher. If you are wandering, the romantic spots are taken.

- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-north-woods-tour)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, 2 PM from Mariners' Gate.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM.
- **East Meadow is closed** for maintenance — the lawn, not the drive beside it. Running and riding are unaffected.
- **Fifteen soccer bookings at North Meadow** plus frisbee and kickball at Heckscher. The leagues are back up after two thin weeks.

## Sun Nov 8 — the week's one real cluster

**63° and mostly sunny — the best day of the weekend by a wide margin.** Everything happens at once:

- **[Leaf Peeping Hike](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/11/08/leaf-peeping-in-central-park-hike)** with the Urban Park Rangers, 1–2:30 PM from Boys' Gate at West 100th. Free, and the timing is right for peak color up north.
- **Animal Rescue Red Carpet** at Literary Walk, 10 AM–noon.
- **[International Folkdancers](https://www.centralpark.com/events/central-park-dance-skaters-association/)** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM, back to full length after the marathon cut them short.
- **NYMS Mushroom Research Meeting** at the East 102nd Street Landscape, 10 AM–3 PM — genuinely a mycological survey, and they do not mind company.
- **[Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour)** 10 AM, **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** 2 PM, **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse** 7:30 AM.
- **East Meadow closed** again; eleven more soccer and frisbee bookings at North Meadow.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain — closed all day, every day, now through December 1.** That is a twelfth extension. The permits have been rolling forward since mid-October and have now crossed out of November entirely.
- **Field permits are back to 71 for the week**, 56 of them at North Meadow. Two weeks ago I called the leagues finished; they were not.
- **No play on the Great Lawn for a fifteenth week** — and still no closure or maintenance permit anywhere in the record to explain it.
- **Nothing at the Delacorte, and no concerts.** The next music on the books is the Carmel High School Jazz Band on November 16.

## Quick recap

- **The drives are open.** Nothing on the loop any day this week; the marathon paperwork running to Nov 10 is load-out, not closure.
- **Mon–Wed: 65°, 67°, 68°, sunny, and literally nothing scheduled.** The best midweek stretch of the year.
- **Rain Thursday through Saturday**, clearing to a sunny 63° Sunday.
- **Sunday is the day to be in the park** — leaf peeping, folkdancers, mushrooms and the Ramble walk all in one morning.
- **Bethesda is closed into December.** Twelfth extension.
