Hi,

Thirteen events in the whole window, two of them one-offs. **This is the thinnest week the permit record has produced since I started sending you this digest** — and the most interesting thing in it is a data artifact rather than an event.

## Weather this week

Sunny and 65°, 67°, 68° through Wednesday — well above normal for early November — then rain Thursday through Saturday and a clear 63° Sunday. Pattern-wise: the warm anomaly that carried through marathon weekend is holding for three more days before the first properly wet stretch of the month.

## The marathon envelope is still flagging, and will until November 10

The marathon ran yesterday. **Its permit does not expire until November 10**, and it covers the full drive network — East, West and Center Drives, the 102nd Street Cross Drive, Bandshell Plaza, Dead Road, Tavern on the Green, twenty-one named places in total — at 00:00–23:59, every single day.

So for the next nine days, the record reports a loop-affecting event in Central Park. **Operationally there is nothing there but load-out:** fencing, trailers and the finish structure coming down along the west side. The drives are open and no day this week has a real closure.

It is the clearest example I have of the problem with how the data models a permit. **A permit that reserves ground for weeks and a permit that shuts a road for four hours look identical in this record** — same flag, same all-day window — and only the context tells you which one you are looking at. The Thanksgiving, tree-lighting and New Year permits are all about to land in the same shape. I read them by hand; a reader looking at the raw calendar would conclude the loop is shut through November 10, and would be wrong.

## Mon Nov 2 – Fri Nov 6 — no one-off events

Five consecutive days with nothing scheduled. Private bookings run two to three a day. **Lawn maintenance** at the East 106th Street Lawn Monday through Thursday is the only operational item.

## Sat Nov 7 – Sun Nov 8 — East Meadow closed, and the leagues come back

- **East Meadow Closure**, both days, 00:00–23:59 — maintenance and restoration, covering the East Meadow and the East 96th Street Playground. A **lawn** closure; East Drive beside it is unaffected.
- **Field permits are back to 71 for the week, 56 of them at North Meadow** — fifteen soccer bookings Saturday, eleven Saturday-to-Sunday across soccer and frisbee, plus kickball at Heckscher. Two weeks ago the weekly count fell to 66 and I told you the league season looked finished. It did not finish; it dipped and recovered. I was early calling it, same as I was early on Heckscher in September.
- **Eighteen private bookings Saturday** — the heaviest ceremony day since late September, two of them in Shakespeare Garden.

## The two standing anomalies

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain has been extended a twelfth time.** The run of daily "Closed" permits now reaches **December 1** — it was November 30 at last week's pull, November 25 the week before, and October 13 when it first appeared. Thirty-one consecutive days currently on the books. Nothing in the record states a cause, and the permit title remains the single uninformative word "Closed."

**The Great Lawn has had zero field permits for fifteen consecutive weeks** — and there is still no closure, maintenance or restoration permit anywhere in the record that would explain it. Every other major lawn that goes out of service files something. This one did not. Fifteen weeks is long past a seasonal explanation.

## Quick recap

- **The envelope flags a closed loop through November 10.** It is load-out. The drives are open every day this week.
- **No one-off events Monday through Friday.** The thinnest window on record here.
- **East Meadow closed Saturday and Sunday** — the lawn, not the drive.
- **Field permits recovered to 71.** My "leagues are winding down" call two weeks ago was premature.
- **Bethesda extended a twelfth time, now to December 1.** Still no stated cause.
- **Great Lawn: fifteen weeks at zero, still no permit explaining it.**
