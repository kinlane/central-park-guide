*Friday's digest ran through the 30th — this one carries the weekend itself.*

Three things worth recording this week. Marathon weekend is locked and
unchanged across two pulls. **Halloween falls on Saturday and the park's permit
calendar has not one Halloween event on it.** And the autumn's venue relocation
has finished moving: North Meadow now carries **94%** of November's field
calendar.

## Weather this week

Sunny every day with **0% rain through Saturday** — 69°, 60°, 61°, 69°, 75°, 70°
— and 69° with a 17% chance on marathon Sunday. A third consecutive flawless
week, which is remarkable for late October, and the dry run-up matters for a
build-out carrying scaffolding across the west side.

Worth noting for the record: **69° is warm for a marathon.** Race-day
temperatures are a thing this event's own history turns on.

## Halloween, and an absence worth naming

Saturday is October 31. **There is no Halloween event anywhere in the park's
permit record** — no parade, no trick-or-treat, no pumpkin event, nothing. What
the park has instead is a road race.

For a park that permits a bench dedication, a harp circle and a greyhound meetup,
an entirely empty Halloween is the kind of absence that says something. The
Village parade takes that crowd and Central Park does not compete. **I have been
treating absences as findings all autumn** — the Great Lawn's missing closure
permit above all — and this is a cleaner one: nothing is broken in the data, the
occasion simply is not the park's.

## Marathon weekend, locked

Unchanged across two consecutive pulls, which for an event this size is itself
the news:

| When | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Wed Oct 28, 9–10 AM | Blue Line Painting Ceremony | West Drive |
| Fri Oct 30, 4–7 PM | Opening Ceremony | West Drive |
| Sat Oct 31, 8–12 | Dash to the Finish Line 5K Finish | Grand Army Plaza |
| Sat Oct 31, 8:45–11 | Dash to the Finish Line 5K | Ted Corbitt Loop |
| Sun Nov 1, 8 AM–5 PM | **TCS New York City Marathon** | Ted Corbitt Loop |

Plus the 24-day envelope underneath. **West Drive closes three times in six days**
— an hour, three hours, and then the whole loop for nine. The loop going in
pieces before going entirely is a sequence I have not seen in this series.

One small marker of how far the reorganisation reaches: the **International
Folkdancers**, who hold King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM every Sunday of the year,
are permitted **11 AM–3 PM** on November 1. Three hours shorter, for a group
nowhere near the course.

## The relocation has settled

November's field permits: **North Meadow 131, Heckscher 4, tennis 4.** North
Meadow carries **94%** of the month.

That closes the question this digest has carried since mid-September. The full
arc, start to finish:

| | Sep 5–11 | Sep 21–27 | Oct 19–25 | November |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Great Lawn | ~70 | 0 | 0 | **0** |
| Heckscher | 24 | 111 | 9 | **4** |
| North Meadow | 5 | 83 | 75 | **131** |

Three venues, two moves, one autumn — and **no closure or maintenance permit for
either move, at any point.** The Great Lawn's absence is now thirteen weeks old
and remains the longest unexplained thing in this data.

## Bethesda Terrace, tenth extension

Now **November 25.** October 13, 25, 27, November 2, 4, 9, 11, 16, 18, 23, 25.
Eleven readings, ten extensions. Nine weeks closed, never once a stated reason.
