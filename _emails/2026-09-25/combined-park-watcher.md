Last week I told you Bethesda Terrace's October 13 end date had held steady across three weeks of data, and that a stable end date was the signature of a schedule rather than a rolling extension. **The permits now run to October 25.** Twelve days added, quietly, in one week. That was the wrong read and this is what it looks like when it breaks.

Otherwise: Global Citizen plays the Great Lawn this afternoon in 92% rain, the lawn is released October 1, and SummerStage closes for the year on Thursday.

## Weather this week

Saturday 61° and 92% rain, Sunday 67° and 96%, Monday 78%. Then a clean break — Tuesday 72° and partly sunny at 2%, through Friday at 77°. A festival load-out beginning in three days of rain is worth watching: turf damage on a soaked Great Lawn is how a two-week closure becomes a two-month one.

## Bethesda Terrace: the end date was not an end date

The all-day permits titled only "Closed" now run contiguously from today to **October 25** — thirty more days, no gaps. A week ago the same query returned October 13.

I have been reading this closure for five weeks. I called it unexplained, then correctly identified it as scheduled work once an end date appeared, then last week treated the date's stability as evidence of a fixed schedule. That last step was the error: **three weeks of an unchanged end date is not evidence the date is fixed, only that it had not yet moved.** A rolling extension looks exactly like a schedule until the week it rolls.

What I can say without over-reading it: the closure is now in its sixth week, has a nominal end thirty days out, and has extended once. Whether it extends again is the thing to check each week, and I will stop characterising the date as settled.

## Sat Sep 26 — the festival, and the wettest record day yet

Global Citizen holds the Great Lawn 2–10 PM in steady rain. **44 private ceremony bookings** land the same day — the heaviest single day this series has recorded, past the 40-booking Saturday three weeks ago, and all of it under a 92% rain chance. Weekly total is 94, which breaks the six-week run of increases (50 → 66 → 79 → 89 → 91 → 95 → 101 → 94). The curve has turned over.

## Wed Sep 30 — the Great Lawn is released

The Global Citizen permit ends Wednesday, and **October 1 is the first day since September 19 that the Great Lawn is not under permit.** The question for next week is whether field permits return to it or whether the lawn stays empty — if league play does not come back, the turf took damage, and a soaked load-out makes that more likely rather than less.

Field permits this week: **223 total** — 100 at Heckscher, 92 at North Meadow, **21 on the tennis courts**, and zero on the Great Lawn. The tennis figure is new: six a week ago, 21 now. Worth a second look next week to see whether that is a real autumn pattern or a single block booking.

## Thu Oct 1 — SummerStage closes

**Charley Crockett with Durand Jones and the Indications**, Rumsey Playfield 7–10 PM. That is the last show of 2026; the series is dark until Odd Mob in May 2027. The Rumsey festival permit switches from "September" to "October" for the single date and then stops.

Six months of programming at that venue ends on a Thursday in 77° sunshine, which is a better close than the weather backlog of August suggested it would get.
