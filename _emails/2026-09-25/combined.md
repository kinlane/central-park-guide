A week of two halves, and the hinge is Tuesday. The weekend is wet — genuinely wet, 92% today and 96% tomorrow — and the **Global Citizen Festival plays the Great Lawn this afternoon straight into it**. Then Tuesday the rain stops dead and the park gets four consecutive days of 72–77° sunshine, closing with **Charley Crockett and SummerStage's final night of the season** on Thursday.

## Weather this week

Saturday is 61° with a 92% chance of rain and 98% overnight; Sunday is 67° and 96%; Monday still 78%. Then it breaks completely — Tuesday 72° and partly sunny at 2%, Wednesday 76°, Thursday and Friday both 77° and mostly sunny. Three soaking days, then the best four days in a month.

## Sat Sep 26, 2–10 PM — the Global Citizen Festival, in the rain

**[The Global Citizen Festival](https://www.centralpark.com/events/global-citizen-festival/)** takes the Great Lawn — artists and world leaders on the global food crisis and debt relief, free by ticket lottery. It is going ahead in 61° and steady rain, so if you have tickets, dress for eight hours of it.

**Mid-park is gone from noon.** Both transverses and every Great Lawn approach carry the crowd. Runners and cyclists: the loop is open but people will be crossing it all afternoon and evening.

The morning, wet as it is, has a lot in it:

- **[Free Yoga in Central Park](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/free-yoga-in-central-park)** on East Meadow at 11, with **CPC Yoga** alongside it.
- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 — behind the scenes with the staff, and the garden is turning.
- **Novice Singles Tournament** on Bowling Green 8 AM–5 PM, both weekend days.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9–3, **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7, the **Farmstand** at Dana Discovery Center 9 AM–8 PM.
- **Summer Muse** on the East 102nd Street landscape 4–10 PM, and **tango at the Shakespeare Statue** 6–9 PM.

Saturday also carries **44 private ceremony bookings** — the heaviest day this series has recorded, and all of it under rain.

## Sun Sep 27 — 96% rain, so plan for a roof

**Pinocchio and Geppetto's NYC Adventure** at the **Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre**, 2–3 PM, is the day's best call — indoors, and a new production.

Outdoors if you are undeterred: **Greyhounds of NYC Meetup** on the Dairy Lawn 10 AM–1 PM, **International Sunday** at Bandshell Plaza 10–2, **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–6, **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10 and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2.

## Mon Sep 28 – Wed Sep 30 — the turn

Monday still carries showers at 78% and one event: **New York Rock'n'roll** at Bandshell Plaza 5–7 PM.

**Tuesday is the pivot** — 72°, partly sunny, a 2% rain chance, and almost nothing booked but **Busking at the Bandshell** 1–3 PM. Bowling Green closes all day for maintenance. If you have been waiting out the weather, Tuesday is your day.

Wednesday warms to 76° and sunny: a **25th Anniversary** gathering at Hernshead 5–7 PM, **Serenade** at the Bandshell 6–7 PM, and a **Starlight Dinner** at Minton Arcade 6–9 PM. Wednesday is also when the Global Citizen permit ends — **the Great Lawn comes back on October 1**.

## Thu Oct 1, 7–10 PM — Charley Crockett closes SummerStage

**[Charley Crockett](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/charley-crockett/)** plays Rumsey Playfield with **Durand Jones and the Indications** — the last SummerStage show of 2026. After Thursday the series is dark until May. 77° and mostly sunny, which is the send-off it deserves.

Earlier, a **Central Park Conservancy Birding Event** meets at the Swedish Cottage 9–11 AM.

## Fri Oct 2 — free music at the Bandshell

**Free Community Music Live** takes Bandshell Plaza 3–7 PM. **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10, the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2. 77° and partly sunny.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — still closed all day, every day, and the permits now run to **October 25**, twelve days later than the record showed last week.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free — the right call for a wet weekend.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse. Fall migration is still running.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday.
- **223 field permits** — 100 at Heckscher, 92 at North Meadow, 21 on the tennis courts, and still **zero on the Great Lawn**.

## Quick recap

- **Global Citizen Festival today, 2–10 PM**, Great Lawn, in 92% rain. Mid-park is gone from noon.
- **Tuesday is the hinge:** 72°, sunny, 2% rain. Four good days follow.
- **Charley Crockett closes SummerStage Thursday**, 7 PM — the season's last show.
- **Sunday is a 96% washout** — Pinocchio at the Marionette Theatre is the indoor play.
- **The Great Lawn is released October 1** when the festival permit ends.
- **Bethesda Terrace now closed through October 25.**
