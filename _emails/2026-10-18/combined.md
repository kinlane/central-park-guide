*Friday's email ran through the 23rd, so the midweek will be familiar — the weekend is new.*

**Seven consecutive sunny days**, 59–66°, with almost no rain in any of them. It is the best full week of weather since the first week of October and the foliage has arrived to meet it — there are **two Fall Foliage Photo Walks on Saturday**. Meanwhile the marathon's three-week build-out has started across the finish-line end of the park.

## Weather this week

Sunny or mostly sunny every single day: 65°, 66°, 62°, 66°, 61°, 59°. Rain chances run 0–12%. Nights drop to 44–51°, so mornings are properly cold and the light through the middle of the day is as good as it gets all year.

## The marathon build has started — but the drives are open

The city's permit for the **TCS New York City Marathon** went live this week, and it runs **October 18 through November 10** across twenty-one locations: West, East and Center Drive, Tavern on the Green, Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge, Belvedere Castle and the rest of the finish-line apparatus.

**That is a build-and-strike envelope, not three weeks of closure.** Expect scaffolding, fencing, trailers and staging to appear and thicken across the west side and the Tavern on the Green area over the fortnight. **The drives are not closed this week.** The actual closures are:

- **Saturday Oct 31** — Abbott Dash to the Finish Line 5K, 8:45–11 AM
- **Sunday Nov 1** — the marathon itself, 8 AM–5 PM, 55,000 runners

## Mon Oct 19 – Thu Oct 22 — quiet, bright, and two small civic moments

Weekdays carry soccer training, school programmes and the standing Arsenal exhibits. Nothing is in anyone's way, and the drives are clear.

- **Tuesday, 4–5 PM: bench dedication for Bruce Rosenfeld** at the Central Park Zoo.
- **Wednesday, 6:30–9:30 PM: the City Parks Foundation dinner** at the Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre — the organisation behind SummerStage, holding its dinner in a puppet theatre, which is a pleasing thing to know happens.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** rotates again: Strawberry Fields Monday, Loeb Boathouse Thursday, the **Conservatory Garden** Friday.

Tuesday and Thursday are both 66° and sunny — the pick of the week.

## Fri Oct 23 — the North Woods

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** meets at the Dana Discovery Center at 10 AM. 61° and sunny. The park's wildest end, with a guide, in peak colour.

**[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Sat Oct 24 — two foliage photo walks, and a fun run at the Bandshell

**Two [Fall Foliage Photo Walks](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/)**: one from **The Pool, 9:30–11:30 AM**, and one from **Boys' Gate at 10 AM.** The north end in late October, with someone showing you where to point a camera — this is the single best-timed thing on the autumn calendar.

- **Ronald McDonald House Fun Run** stages at Bandshell Plaza and Dead Road North, 8 AM–2 PM. Six hours; the Mall will be busy, though the drives stay open.
- **Free Yoga** and **CPC Yoga** both on East Meadow at 11 AM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs, 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9–7.
- **Awareness Meetup for Namrud Wegahta Tessema** at Ross Pinetum, 3–6 PM.

59° and sunny. East Meadow closed.

## Sun Oct 25 — rocks, marionettes, and the North Woods again

A genuinely good family Sunday:

- **Geology: Central Park Rocks!** at the Harlem Meer, 1–2 PM — the glacial history of the park, which is more interesting than it sounds and visible once someone points it out.
- **Pinocchio and Geppetto's NYC Adventure** at the **Diana Ross Playground**, 2–3 PM — note the venue, not the Swedish Cottage this time.
- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** repeats at 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 PM; **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM; **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7 PM.

East Meadow closed.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** at the Arsenal all seven days.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 18**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free.
- **No league play on the Great Lawn**, for the eleventh week running. North Meadow is carrying the whole field calendar now.
