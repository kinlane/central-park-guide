*Friday's email ran through the 23rd, so the midweek will be familiar — the weekend is new.*

**Seven sunny days, 59–66°, and the foliage has arrived to meet them.** Saturday brings **two Fall Foliage Photo Walks** in the north end, and Sunday is the best family day in a month: glacial geology at the Harlem Meer, marionettes at the Diana Ross Playground, and the North Woods walk.

## Weather this week

Sunny or mostly sunny every day — 65°, 66°, 62°, 66°, 61°, 59° — with rain chances of 0–12%. Nights fall to 44–51°. Cold mornings, and the kind of midday light that makes late October the best photography fortnight of the year.

## Mon Oct 19 – Thu Oct 22 — quiet, with two small civic moments

Weekdays are soccer training, school programmes and the standing Arsenal exhibits. Two things on the public calendar:

- **Tuesday, 4–5 PM: bench dedication for Bruce Rosenfeld** at the Central Park Zoo.
- **Wednesday, 6:30–9:30 PM: the City Parks Foundation dinner** at the **Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre.** The organisation behind SummerStage, holding its annual dinner inside a puppet theatre — a lovely detail, and not a public event, but worth knowing the building gets used that way.

**New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free, is the indoor standby. **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** rotates: Strawberry Fields Monday, Loeb Boathouse Thursday, the **Conservatory Garden** Friday.

## Fri Oct 23 — the North Woods

**[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)**, 10 AM from the Dana Discovery Center. The park's wildest and least-visited end, with a Conservancy guide, at 61° and sunny. **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Sat Oct 24 — two foliage photo walks

**[Fall Foliage Photo Walks](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/)**, two of them:

- from **The Pool, 9:30–11:30 AM**
- from **Boys' Gate, 10 AM**

Both in the north end, both timed for peak colour, both with someone showing you where to point a camera. If you own a camera and have been meaning to use it properly, this is the Saturday.

Also on, 59° and sunny:

- **Ronald McDonald House Fun Run** at Bandshell Plaza and Dead Road North, 8 AM–2 PM.
- **Free Yoga** and **CPC Yoga**, both East Meadow at 11 AM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **Awareness Meetup for Namrud Wegahta Tessema** at Ross Pinetum, 3–6 PM.

East Meadow closed.

## Sun Oct 25 — the best family day in a month

- **Geology: Central Park Rocks!** at the Harlem Meer, 1–2 PM. The glacial history written into the park's outcrops — far more interesting than it sounds, and once somebody shows you the scratches on the schist you cannot stop seeing them.
- **Pinocchio and Geppetto's NYC Adventure** at the **Diana Ross Playground**, 2–3 PM. Note the venue — it has moved from the Swedish Cottage this week.
- **[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** repeats at 10 AM.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** at 2 PM; **[Iconic Views](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–6 PM.
- **Central Park Skate Circle** at Dead Road North, 2–7 PM.

## Looking ahead

**The marathon build has started.** The city permit runs Oct 18 to Nov 10 across the finish-line end of the park, so expect scaffolding and fencing around Tavern on the Green and the west side to thicken over the fortnight. Race day is **Sunday Nov 1**, with the **Dash to the Finish Line 5K** the day before.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** at the Arsenal all week.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** closed all day, every day — permits now run to **November 18**.
- **No league play on the Great Lawn** for an eleventh week; North Meadow carries the whole field calendar.
