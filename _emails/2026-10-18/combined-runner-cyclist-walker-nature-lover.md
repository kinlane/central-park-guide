*Friday's email ran through the 23rd, so the midweek will be familiar — the weekend is new.*

**Seven sunny days, 59–66°, open drives every one of them.** This is the last fully clear week before the marathon, and it is a good one. Use it: in a fortnight the loop is gone for two days and the week between now and then fills with build-out.

## Weather this week

Sunny or mostly sunny all seven days: 65°, 66°, 62°, 66°, 61°, 59°. Rain 0–12%. Nights 44–51° — cold starts, brilliant light, no wind to speak of. Properly good riding and running weather.

## The marathon build has started — read this before you panic

The city's marathon permit went live this week and covers **Oct 18 to Nov 10** across twenty-one locations, West, East and Center Drive included. If you look at the raw permit you would think the loop is shut for three weeks.

**It is not.** That is a build-and-strike envelope: scaffolding, fencing and trailers appearing across the west side and Tavern on the Green and thickening as race day approaches. **The drives are open all of this week.** What you will meet is equipment, not closures.

The real closures are **Sat Oct 31, 8:45–11 AM** (Dash to the Finish Line 5K) and **Sun Nov 1, 8 AM–5 PM** (the marathon, 55,000 runners finishing in the park).

## Mon Oct 19 – Thu Oct 22 — the week, and nothing in your way

Nothing on a drive. Weekdays carry soccer training and school programmes on the lawns, and that is all.

**Tuesday and Thursday are both 66° and sunny.** Those are the two to take. Wednesday is 62° and sunny, which is nearly as good.

**[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** is rotating meeting points — Strawberry Fields Monday, Loeb Boathouse Thursday, the **Conservatory Garden** Friday. Check which before you set out.

## Fri Oct 23 — the North Woods, in peak colour

**[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)**, 10 AM from the Dana Discovery Center. 61° and sunny, the park's wildest end, with a guide who knows it. Late October is exactly when to do this one.

**[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Sat Oct 24 — two foliage photo walks, and the Mall is busy

**Two [Fall Foliage Photo Walks](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/)** — from **The Pool, 9:30–11:30 AM**, and from **Boys' Gate at 10 AM.** Both in the north end, both timed for peak colour. If you walk the Ravine and the Loch, this is the weekend.

The **Ronald McDonald House Fun Run** stages at Bandshell Plaza and Dead Road North 8 AM–2 PM. Six hours of it, so the Mall and the Dead Road approaches are busy — but **the drives stay open**. Ride the outer loop and you will barely notice.

59° and sunny. East Meadow closed; **Free Yoga** and **CPC Yoga** both on East Meadow at 11 AM if you want something slower.

## Sun Oct 25 — the North Woods again, and clear roads

**[Woodland Walk: The North Woods](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** repeats at 10 AM. The **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** both go at 2 PM.

Nothing on the drives. Folkdancers at King Jagiello 11–6, Skate Circle at Dead Road 2–7 — both worth walking past, neither in your way. East Meadow closed.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is closed all day, every day, and the permits have extended an eighth time — now **November 18**. Eleven weeks elapsed.

**No league play on the Great Lawn** for an eleventh week; North Meadow is carrying the entire field calendar. Nothing there affects a route, but it is the longest-running oddity in the park's data right now.

## Quick recap

- **Seven sunny days, open drives on all of them.** The last clear week before the marathon.
- **Tuesday and Thursday at 66° are the pick.**
- **The marathon permit is a build envelope, not a closure** — drives open all week.
- **Two foliage photo walks Saturday**, north end, peak colour.
- **Fri and Sun 10 AM: the North Woods walk.**
- **Oct 31 and Nov 1 are the real closures.** Long effort before then.
