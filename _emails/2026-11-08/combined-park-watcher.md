Twelve relevant records and five one-offs — thin again. But the closure pattern I flagged to you last week as a guess firmed up considerably in this week's pull, and I think it is now the most interesting thing in the record.

## Weather this week

Mild and sunny Monday to Wednesday at 59°, 61°, 61°, then a sharp break: Thursday 57° with evening rain, **Friday 47° with light rain and 20–25 mph sustained wind**, Saturday 46°. Pattern-wise this is the end of the warm anomaly that has run since the marathon — Friday is the first properly cold, wet day of the season.

## Five closures, five locations, one date — and they moved together

Last week I told you that three multi-week closures shared a December 6 end date, and that the shared date was the only thing pointing at a common cause. This week there are **five**, and they did something a coincidence cannot do.

| Series | Days | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Bethesda Terrace — "Closed" | 31 | Dec 8 |
| Bethesda Terrace — "Central Park Closure" | 20 | Dec 8 |
| Swedish Cottage lawn | 19 | Dec 8 |
| E 96th St playground lawn | 19 | Dec 8 |
| E 106th St lawn maintenance | 18 | Dec 8 |

**All five moved from December 6 to December 8 in the same filing.** Separate permits, five different locations across the length of the park, extended by exactly two days in lockstep. That is a scheduling decision made once and applied to all of them, not five independent extensions that happen to agree.

Two of those five are new to the group since last week. The East 106th Street lawn maintenance had been appearing as a Monday-to-Thursday item; it is now a month-long run with the same end date, which means I had been reporting a fragment of it as a weekly recurrence.

I do not know what is scheduled. The permits say nothing beyond their titles, and one of the five is still the single uninformative word "Closed." But five locations moving together is a much stronger signal than five locations agreeing, and it is worth watching December 8.

## The Great Lawn is back to zero

Last week's picnic — the first permit on the Great Lawn since July — was Saturday. It has now passed, and **nothing is booked behind it.** The lawn is empty again.

So the revision I gave you last week stands, but it should not be over-read: one booking proves the lawn was never administratively closed, and it proves nothing else. Whatever has kept it empty for four months is still doing so.

## The marathon envelope expires Tuesday

Two days left. It reports a loop-affecting event covering all three drives Monday and Tuesday, 00:00–23:59, nine and ten days after the race. From Wednesday it is gone and the loop record is accurate again until **Race to Deliver on November 22**, which is a genuine two-hour closure.

## The gala permit has grown twice

The CPC Annual Fall-Winter Gala at Rumsey Playfield and Bandshell Plaza was **a one-day permit** when it first appeared on October 30, **two days** in Friday's pull, and is now **three days — November 13, 14 and 15**, 9 AM to midnight throughout.

Worth noting as a pattern rather than an event: this is the second permit this month I have watched expand after filing, the marathon envelope being the other. The feed gives us the current state, not the history, so an event's real footprint is only knowable by pulling repeatedly and diffing. We do; most readers of this data would not.
