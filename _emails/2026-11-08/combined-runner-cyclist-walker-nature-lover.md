Hi all,

**Three good days, then it ends.** Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday are sunny at 59°, 61° and 61° with nothing booked anywhere in the park — and then **Friday arrives at 47° with steady rain and 20–25 mph wind.** The mild autumn is over on Thursday evening.

**Wednesday — Veterans Day — is the one to take.** 61°, sunny, and the wind finally drops to 6–9 mph after three breezy days. It is the best riding and running day left on the forecast.

## Weather this week

Mild and sunny through Wednesday, breezy Monday at 16–20 mph and calm by midweek. **Thursday 57° with rain by evening, Friday 47° with light rain all day and a 25 mph wind, Saturday 46° and raw.** Front-load the week — everything you were saving for the weekend should move to Tuesday or Wednesday.

## Mon Nov 9 – Wed Nov 11 — the loop is clear and nothing is booked

**Three consecutive days with no scheduled events at all.**

One thing to ignore: the record still shows a loop-affecting permit Monday and Tuesday covering East, West and Center Drive. **That is the marathon envelope, and it expires Tuesday.** It has been load-out since the day after the race — fencing and trailers — and the drives have been open throughout. **Nothing blocks a lap any day this week.**

- **Wednesday is the day.** 61°, sun, almost no wind, empty roads.
- **Monday is windy** at 16–20 mph, which on the west side you will feel the whole way round.
- **Lawn maintenance** at the East 106th Street Lawn, Monday to Thursday — relevant only if you cool down up there.
- **[Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)**: Strawberry Fields Monday 8:30 AM, Loeb Boathouse Thursday 8:30 AM.

## Thu Nov 12 – Fri Nov 13 — the weather breaks

Thursday holds at 57° until the evening, when rain moves in and the wind gets up to 25 mph overnight. **Friday is the worst day: 47°, light rain most of the day, 20–25 mph sustained.** If you run or ride Friday, it is a hard-shell day and the Bridle Path will be mud.

**The CPC Annual Fall-Winter Gala takes Rumsey Playfield and Bandshell Plaza from Friday morning — and it now runs three days, through Sunday.** No effect on the drives at all, but the Bandshell end of the park is out, and there will be trucks where you normally loop past Rumsey.

Friday's one bright spot: **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden**, 8:30 AM. Best venue in his rotation, one week in four — and under rain the formal garden is worth it anyway. **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Sat Nov 14 — cold, raw, and better for walking than riding

46° with a 20 mph wind and showers around 31%.

- **St. Martin's Parade**, 3:15–5 PM at **Summit Rock** — a lantern procession at the highest point in the park, timed for dusk. Worth the cold.
- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM; **Birding Bob at Loeb** 7:30 AM.
- **East Meadow closed** for maintenance — the lawn only, East Drive beside it is open.

## Sun Nov 15 — the Great Central Park Hike

- **[Great Central Park Hike](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/great-central-park-hike-tour)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center. The Conservancy's full-length walk, top to bottom, with guides. On a cold clear Sunday with the leaves down and the sightlines open, this is the best walking of the month.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse**, 7:30 AM. East Meadow closed again; the gala's last day at Rumsey.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace closed every day through December 8** — a fourteenth extension. And it is not alone: **five separate closures now all end on exactly December 8**, and all five moved there from December 6 together in this week's filing. Both Bethesda permits, the Swedish Cottage lawn, the East 96th Street playground lawn and the East 106th lawn maintenance. Something is scheduled that week.
- **Race to Deliver 4M, Sunday November 22, 8–10 AM** — the first genuine loop closure since the marathon. Two weeks out; diary it.

Take Wednesday.

Have a strong week,
Kin
