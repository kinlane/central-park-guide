**Take Monday through Wednesday.** Three sunny days at 59°, 61° and 61°, and then the mild autumn the park has been running on since the marathon ends abruptly: **Friday is 47° with steady rain and 25 mph wind.** The weekend stays cold and grey.

The other news is a permit that keeps growing — **the Conservancy's gala now holds Rumsey Playfield for three days**, Friday through Sunday, where a week ago it was one.

## Weather this week

Sunny and mild Monday to Wednesday — 59°, 61°, 61° — with Veterans Day the calmest of the three. **Then it breaks: Thursday 57° with a 50% chance of rain, Friday 47° with light rain and 20–25 mph wind, Saturday 46°.** Do the outdoor things in the first half of the week; there is no second chance in this one.

## Mon Nov 9 – Wed Nov 11 — three empty, sunny days

Nothing is booked across all three. Mild, dry, and the park is as quiet as it gets.

**The marathon envelope expires Tuesday.** It still reports a loop-affecting event Monday and Tuesday covering all three drives — that is the last of the load-out paperwork, not a closure, and the drives have been open since the day after the race. From Wednesday it is gone.

- **Veterans Day, Wednesday**, is 61° and sunny with the wind finally down to 6–9 mph. **That is the pick of the week** for a ride, a long walk or a long run.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — Strawberry Fields Monday 8:30 AM, Loeb Boathouse Thursday 8:30 AM.
- **Lawn maintenance** at the East 106th Street Lawn, Monday through Thursday.

## Thu Nov 12 – Fri Nov 13 — the turn, and the gala moves in

Thursday 57° with rain arriving by evening. **Friday is the hard stop: 47°, light rain most of the day, 20–25 mph wind.** The coldest, wettest day since the spring.

**The CPC Annual Fall-Winter Gala opens Friday**, 9 AM to midnight, holding **Rumsey Playfield and Bandshell Plaza — and it now runs three days, through Sunday.** It was a one-day permit when I first reported it ten days ago, then two, now three. The Conservancy's own fundraiser, not a public event, but the whole SummerStage footprint is out all weekend and there will be production trucks from Friday morning.

Friday's only public event is the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM, and **Birding Bob at the Conservatory Garden** at 8:30 AM — his best stop, on the worst morning of the week.

## Sat Nov 14 — a parade at dusk, and knights behind the Met

46° with showers around 31% and a 20 mph wind. Cold, but the day is well stocked:

- **St. Martin's Parade**, 3:15–5 PM at **Summit Rock**. A lantern procession at the park's highest point, timed for dusk. If you go to one thing this week, this is the one with atmosphere.
- **Santa's Knights and Gladiators NYC**, noon–4 PM on the **Lawn Behind the Met**. New to the calendar, and about as self-explanatory as the name suggests.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM — with a 20 mph wind the boats will be flying.
- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM; **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse** 7:30 AM.
- **East Meadow closed** — the lawn, not the drive.

## Sun Nov 15 — the Great Central Park Hike

Cold and clearing.

- **[Great Central Park Hike](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/great-central-park-hike-tour)**, 10 AM from Dana Discovery Center. The Conservancy's long one, end to end — and a cold bright Sunday is the right day for it.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM.
- **Birding Bob at Loeb Boathouse** 7:30 AM. East Meadow closed; the gala's final day.

## Steady all week

- **Five separate closures now all end on exactly December 8** — both Bethesda permits, the Swedish Cottage lawn, the East 96th Street playground lawn, and the East 106th Street lawn maintenance. **All five moved from December 6 to December 8 together in this week's filing.** Five permits at five locations do not drift two days by coincidence; something is scheduled for that week. I do not know what.
- **Bethesda Terrace is closed every day** through December 8 — a fourteenth extension.
- **The Great Lawn is back to zero.** Last Saturday's picnic was the first permit on it since July; it has now passed, and nothing is booked behind it.
- **Field permits: 56 this week**, 45 at North Meadow.
- **Coming: Race to Deliver 4M, Sunday November 22, 8–10 AM** — a real loop closure, the first since the marathon.

## Quick recap

- **Mon–Wed: 59°, 61°, 61° and sunny, nothing booked.** Veterans Day is the best day of the week.
- **Friday it breaks** — 47°, rain, 25 mph wind, and cold through the weekend.
- **The Conservancy gala holds Rumsey Friday through Sunday**, now a three-day permit.
- **Saturday: St. Martin's Parade at Summit Rock, 3:15 PM** — lanterns at dusk.
- **Sunday: the Great Central Park Hike**, 10 AM from Dana.
- **Five park closures all now end December 8**, having moved together.
