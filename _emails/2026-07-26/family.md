Hi families — two easy wins bookend this week. Monday night there's a free movie on the Great Lawn, and Saturday morning stacks storytelling, chess with a beginners' workshop, and model yacht racing within an easy walk of each other. The middle of the week is rainy, which is honestly a gift for the Chess and Checkers House and the pool.

## Weather this week
Showers start Monday evening and run through Wednesday — Tuesday storms at 78°F, Wednesday showers at 75°F — before Thursday clears at 81°F and Friday and Saturday turn dry and sunny at 83–85°F. Plan the outdoor days for the end of the week and keep Tuesday and Wednesday for indoor-adjacent stops like the Chess and Checkers House or the Arsenal gallery.

## Mon Jul 27 — Pinocchio at 11, free movie on the Great Lawn at 7
82°F, showers late. **Pinocchio in the Park runs at 11 AM on the Great Lawn** — outdoor kids' theater, easy to drop in on. Then a **free movie night on the Great Lawn at 7 PM**; bring a blanket and get there early enough to sit somewhere flat. A high school choir sings at the Bandshell at 5 if you want something in between. Note that Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill are closed for maintenance — both are popular picnic-and-roll-down-the-hill spots, so pick another lawn.

## Tue Jul 28 — a rainy day with a plan
Storms most of the day, 78°F. The Arsenal's free photography show is open from 9 — a real indoor option in the middle of the park. There's also a dance performance at the Bandshell at noon if you get a dry hour. A school group has the Harlem Meer for fishing mid-morning, so the dock will be busy with kids who got there first.

## Wed Jul 29 — showery, and a big softball day at Heckscher
75°F with showers. The **annual softball event fills Heckscher Fields from 11 AM** — worth walking past even if you're not playing, it's the busiest the fields get all summer. A free film screens at the Great Hill at 7 PM, weather permitting. Lap swim runs at the Gottesman Pool at the Davis Center from 7 AM, free, as it does every weekday.

## Thu Jul 30 — market, back-to-school field day, concert on the Great Lawn
Clearing to 81°F. The Columbus Circle food market opens at 9:30 at Merchants' Gate. There's a back-to-school field day at Mineral Springs from 1 PM, and a **free concert on the Great Lawn at 7 PM**. Salsa at the Dana Discovery Center from 4 is the north-end pick if you'd rather have space than crowds.

## Fri Jul 31 — the quiet, easy day
Mostly sunny, 83°F, and the calendar is thin — which after a wet week is the point. Food market at Columbus Circle from 9:30. Playgrounds are all open. This is the day to just go.

## Sat Aug 1 — the big one: storytelling, chess, and model yachts
85°F and sunny. Three good things in one morning: **storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue at 10**, **chess lectures with a beginners' workshop at the Chess and Checkers House from 9** (bring a kid who's curious, they teach from scratch), and **model yacht racing at Kerbs Boathouse from 9** on Conservatory Water. Add That Guitar Man at Hernshead at noon and you have a full day without leaving the mid-park. **East Meadow is closed all weekend** — one fewer big lawn, so Sheep Meadow and the Great Lawn will be fuller.

## Sun Aug 2 — Harlem Meer morning, folkdancing at 11
Past the current forecast, so check before you go. **Summer on the Harlem Meer opens at 9 at the Dana Discovery Center** — the north end's family day, and there's free live music there at 2. The International Folkdancers gather at King Jagiello Plaza at 11, which kids reliably find fascinating for about twenty minutes. East Meadow still closed.

## Quick recap
- **Mon 7 PM — free movie on the Great Lawn.** Pinocchio in the Park at 11 AM the same day.
- **Sat morning is the stack:** storytelling at 10, chess with beginners' workshop from 9, model yachts from 9.
- **Sun — Summer on the Harlem Meer** from 9, with music at 2.
- **Thu 7 PM — free concert on the Great Lawn.**
- **Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill closed for maintenance; East Meadow closed Sat and Sun.** Pick another lawn.
- **Tue and Wed are wet** — the Arsenal gallery and the Chess and Checkers House are the fallbacks.

See you in the park,
