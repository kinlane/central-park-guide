Hi sports fans — the headline is Wednesday, when the annual softball event fills Heckscher Fields from 11 AM. Otherwise this is a heavy but ordinary permit week: North Meadow and the Great Lawn carry twenty-odd games a day, the clay oval runs its July program, and there's no race on the loop for the first time in a while.

## Weather this week
Storms Tuesday at 78°F and showers Wednesday at 75°F, then clearing Thursday at 81°F and dry and sunny Friday and Saturday at 83–85°F. Tuesday is the day to expect cancellations and Wednesday the day to expect a soft, slow outfield — check with your league before you travel for either.

## Wed Jul 29, from 11 AM — Annual Softball Event, Heckscher Fields
**The week's one real event.** Heckscher — the park's oldest ballfields, and still the ones with the best sightlines — carries the annual tournament from late morning, alongside fourteen other permitted games on the same complex. Showers are in the forecast at 75°F, so bring the layer and expect a heavy infield. If you just want to watch good ball in a great setting, this is the day.

## Field load, day by day
The permit footprint this week, so you know where to go and where to avoid:

- **Mon Jul 27** — North Meadow 21, Great Lawn 16, Heckscher 15. A full Monday.
- **Tue Jul 28** — Great Lawn 25, North Meadow 24, Heckscher 7. The busiest day on paper, and the wettest in practice.
- **Wed Jul 29** — North Meadow 23, Heckscher 14 (plus the tournament), Great Lawn 4.
- **Thu Jul 30** — Heckscher 20, Great Lawn 18, North Meadow 10.
- **Fri Jul 31** — North Meadow 13, Heckscher 12, Great Lawn 9. The lightest weekday.
- **Sat Aug 1** — North Meadow 20, Great Lawn 14, Heckscher 10.
- **Sun Aug 2** — North Meadow 20, Great Lawn 17, Heckscher 16.

North Meadow is the constant all week — if you're looking for a pickup game, that's where the density is.

## Also on the fields
- **Youth soccer runs daily.** James Christie training at Levin Playground at 9:30 and South Dead Road at 1, Monday through Friday. Universal Kids Soccer takes the East 106th Street Lawn Wednesday morning.
- **The East 102nd Street Clay Oval** carries its July program Monday through Thursday from 9 AM.
- **Model yacht racing at Kerbs Boathouse, Saturday from 9 AM** on Conservatory Water — the Central Park Model Yacht Club's weekly races, and one of the few competitive things in the park you can watch from a bench with coffee.
- **Chess lecture and simul series, Saturday from 9 AM** at the Chess and Checkers House, with beginners' workshops running alongside.

## Closures that shift schedules
- **Bowling Green closes Thursday for maintenance** — the lawn-bowling green is out for the day.
- **East Meadow is closed all weekend**, Saturday and Sunday both. Any informal play that normally lands there moves to North Meadow, which is already carrying twenty games a day.
- Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill are under maintenance Monday; neither carries permitted play.

## On the loop
Nothing. No race, no charity run, no crit — the first clean week on the drives in a while. **The next race is the marathon training series 12M on Aug 15**, followed by Grete's Great Gallop 10K on Aug 22. The full fall calendar is now on the site: the 18-miler Sep 20, the marathon itself Nov 1, Ted Corbitt 15K Dec 5, and the Midnight Run on New Year's Eve.

## Quick recap

- **North Meadow is the busiest ground all week**, 20+ games most days.
- **Tue is the wettest day** and Tue/Wed the likeliest for cancellations.
- **Sat 9 AM — model yachts at Kerbs; chess simul at the Chess and Checkers House.**
