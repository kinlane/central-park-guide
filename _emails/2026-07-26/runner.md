Hey runners — the rare week with no race, no charity walk, and nothing staged on the loop. Not one event touches the drives between Monday and Sunday, which means the only thing shaping your week is the sky: three wet days up front, then a dry, warm weekend. Plan around that and you'll get everything in.

## Weather this week
Showers arrive Monday evening and stay through Wednesday — Tuesday is the wettest at 78°F with thunderstorms most of the day, Wednesday cooler at 75°F with a north wind behind it — then Thursday clears to 81°F and Friday and Saturday come in dry and sunny at 83–85°F. Take Monday morning and Thursday through Saturday as your quality days, and treat Tuesday and Wednesday as easy rain miles rather than lost ones; the drives never close for weather.

## Mon Jul 27 — get out early, storms come in behind you
82°F with a slight chance of showers building through the day and thunderstorms after dark. The loop is clean — the only permits are Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill maintenance, both off-route. Morning is the whole window for anything hard this week's first half. The Great Lawn has a free movie night at 7 PM, which fills the mid-park transverses from around 6:30, but the drives don't feel it.

## Tue Jul 28 — the washout, and the drives stay open
Showers and thunderstorms most of the day, 78°F. Nothing on the loop. This is the day to accept wet shoes and run easy, or move the session to Wednesday if you have lightning to worry about. Bandshell Plaza has a dance performance at noon — no route impact, just people on the Mall.

## Wed Jul 29 — cool, showery, and a big Rumsey crowd from 5:30
75°F with rain showers and a north wind — genuinely pleasant running temperature once you commit. The one thing to time: SummerStage opens Rumsey Playfield at 6 PM for Julieta Venegas, and the queue backs toward the 72nd Street entrance from about 5:30. If you're running the lower loop in the early evening, expect pedestrians crossing at 72nd; go before 5 or take the Reservoir.

## Thu Jul 30 — the week turns, and the best morning of the first half
Clearing to 81°F with a north wind at 14 mph — the first genuinely good day since Monday. Put your longest or fastest work here. Two evening crowds to know about: SummerStage again at Rumsey (7 PM, the UBB 40th anniversary show) and a free concert permitted on the Great Lawn at the same hour. Both pull foot traffic across mid-park after 6. Bowling Green closes for maintenance, off-route.

## Fri Jul 31 — clean and dry
Mostly sunny, 83°F, nothing on the drives. No qualifiers on this one. Columbus Circle has a food market from 9:30 that thickens the Merchants' Gate entrance, so if you enter at the southwest corner, budget a few seconds of weaving.

## Sat Aug 1 — the loop is yours; East Meadow is not
85°F and mostly sunny — the warmest day of the week, so go early for temperature rather than traffic. The drives are clear all day. **East Meadow is closed all weekend**, so if you use it for strides or a cool-down, it's out Saturday and Sunday both. SummerStage fills Rumsey again at 6 PM.

## Sun Aug 2 — same story, one more Rumsey night
Beyond the current forecast horizon, so check before you go. Event-wise it's a repeat of Saturday: nothing on the drives, East Meadow still closed, and a 7 PM SummerStage show putting a crowd at 72nd in the evening. Morning is wide open in the way it genuinely hasn't been on a Sunday in weeks.

## Quick recap
- **No race, no walk, nothing on the drives — all seven days.** First clean week in a while.
- **Tuesday and Wednesday are wet.** Drives stay open; run them easy rather than skipping.
- **Thursday through Saturday is the running weather** — clearing Thursday, dry Friday, warm Saturday.
- **Rumsey crowds Wed, Thu, Sat, and Sun evenings.** Pedestrians at the 72nd Street entrance from about 5:30; run before 5 or use the Reservoir.
- **East Meadow closed Saturday and Sunday.**
- **Next race on the calendar:** the marathon training series 12M on Aug 15. Two clean weeks to build first.

Have a strong week,
The Central Park Guide
