Hi park watchers — two things worth noting in the data this week. First, the Central Park Conservancy's guided-tour program is back on the calendar after being absent from it entirely: Iconic Views, Activism in Central Park, Seneca Village, the Conservatory Garden and the Ramble woodland walks are all scheduled again. Second, this is a genuinely empty week on the drives — no race, no charity walk, no staged event on the loop from Monday through Sunday, which is rare for a July.

## Weather this week
A wet middle — thunderstorms Tuesday at 78°F, showers Wednesday at 75°F with a north wind — bracketed by a warm Monday at 82°F and a dry, sunny Friday and Saturday at 83–85°F. The pattern matters mostly for the outdoor programming: Wednesday's SummerStage show and the Great Lawn film screening are the two events with real weather exposure.

## The shape of the week
**SummerStage is at its seasonal peak.** Four Rumsey Playfield nights in five days — Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday — all free. That's the densest run of the summer so far, and it puts a recurring evening crowd at the 72nd Street entrance from about 5:30 on each of those days.

**The north end is programming independently again.** Salsa at the Dana Discovery Center Thursday afternoon, Kobie Dozier there Sunday, and Summer on the Harlem Meer opening Sunday morning. The Meer's summer series has been running its own calendar in parallel with Rumsey all season, and this is the week the two are most clearly distinct in audience.

**Maintenance is rotating through the east-side lawns.** Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill Monday, Bowling Green Thursday, with East Meadow closed across the whole weekend. Bethesda Terrace remains fenced. Nothing unusual in the pattern — this is the mid-summer turf-recovery cycle — but four named landscapes offline at once is on the high side.

## First appearances and changes in the data
- **Conservancy tours are on the calendar for the first time.** Iconic Views runs most mornings out of Columbus Circle — seventeen scheduled dates through the end of August alone. The tour meet points brought two named gates onto our map that weren't there before: Women's Gate at West 72nd and Mariners' Gate at West 85th.
- **The SummerStage roster nearly tripled.** Twenty-two Central Park shows are now on the calendar through October, against seven a week ago — the earlier count was an artifact of how the shows were being identified, not of the season.
- **Six NYRR races are now scheduled through year-end**, none of them this week: marathon training 12M on Aug 15 and 18M on Sep 20, Grete's Great Gallop Aug 22, the marathon itself Nov 1, Ted Corbitt 15K Dec 5, and the Midnight Run on New Year's Eve.
- **The Charlie Parker Jazz Festival begins Aug 23** but plays Marcus Garvey Park and Tompkins Square, not Central Park — worth knowing so it doesn't read as a park event when it appears in the city listings.

## Quick recap
- **Nothing on the drives all week.** No race, no walk, no staging — unusual for late July.
- **SummerStage at peak:** four free Rumsey nights, Wed/Thu/Sat/Sun.
- **Conservancy tours return to the calendar** after being missing entirely.
- **Four landscapes offline:** Cedar Hill, Pilgrim Hill, Bowling Green, and East Meadow over the weekend; Bethesda still fenced.
- **The north end runs its own week** — Dana Discovery Center programming Thursday and Sunday.
