Hi nature lovers — the Conservancy's guided walks are back on our calendar this week after a long absence, including the Seneca Village walk on Saturday morning. And Tuesday and Wednesday bring the first real soaking in a while, which means the Loch runs, the Ravine gets loud, and the woodland paths empty of everyone but you.

## Weather this week
Thunderstorms Tuesday at 78°F and showers Wednesday at 75°F with a north wind behind them, then clearing Thursday at 81°F and dry and sunny Friday and Saturday at 83–85°F. Take the two wet days as the gift they are — the North Woods and the Ravine are at their best a few hours after rain, and the heat later in the week will push most people onto the open lawns and away from the woodland.

## Mon Jul 27 — a dry morning before the front
82°F with showers building late. Quiet across the board; the Ramble and Hallett are as empty as a Monday gets. Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill are closed for maintenance — worth knowing if you watch the hawks work the east-side slopes, since the mowing crews change what's hunting there.

## Tue Jul 28 — the rain day, and the best one
Showers and thunderstorms most of the day, 78°F. The canopy over the Ramble handles a steady rain better than you'd expect, and the paths clear out entirely. **Go to the North Woods in the afternoon if the rain lets up** — the Loch is the single best thing in the park after a real soak, and the Ravine's water sounds like something much further from a city than it is.

## Wed Jul 29 — cool, wet, and the north wind arrives
75°F with showers and a shifting north wind — the coolest day since June and the closest thing to autumn light you'll get in July. Turtle Pond and the Belvedere terrace are worth the walk in this weather; the pond surface in rain is a different animal. Nothing scheduled anywhere near the woodland.

## Thu Jul 30 — clearing, and the light comes back
81°F, clearing, north wind at 14 mph. The **Iconic Views tour leaves Columbus Circle at 10 AM** — landscape-and-landmark rather than ecology, but the Conservancy guides know the ground and it's 90 minutes well spent. The Conservatory Garden is the place to be in post-frontal light; the north wind keeps the humidity off.

## Fri Jul 31 — the quiet day
Mostly sunny, 83°F, nothing on the calendar that touches the north end or the woodland. If you want the Hallett Nature Sanctuary or Summit Rock to yourself, this is the morning.

## Sat Aug 1, 11 AM — Seneca Village Tour, Mariners' Gate
85°F and sunny. **The Conservancy's Seneca Village walk meets at Mariners' Gate** (West 85th and Central Park West) — the landscape of the community that lived here before the park was built, read through the ground itself. Not a nature walk in the strict sense, but the best way to understand why the west-side terrain looks the way it does.

Elsewhere the park is at peak summer-Saturday capacity, so the woodland is the refuge: the Ramble and North Woods stay quiet even on days like this. **East Meadow is closed all weekend.**

## Sun Aug 2 — the Meer
Past the current forecast horizon; check before you set out. **Summer on the Harlem Meer opens at 9 at the Dana Discovery Center** — catch-and-release fishing, and the easiest place in the park to spend a morning at the water's edge. Early is best: herons and egrets work the Meer's north shore before the crowds arrive, and the Loch is a five-minute walk uphill from there.

## Coming up
- **Fri Aug 7, 11 AM — Summer Pollinator Walk**, meeting at West 100th Street and Central Park West. The Conservancy's butterfly-garden walk; it runs several dates through the season.
- **Fri Aug 14 — Central Park Woodland Walk: The Ramble**, meeting at Belvedere Castle. Three dates through August.

## Quick recap
- **Tue and Wed are the woodland days.** Rain, empty paths, and the Loch and Ravine running.
- **Sat 11 AM — Seneca Village walk at Mariners' Gate.** Conservancy-guided.
- **Sun 9 AM — Summer on the Harlem Meer.** Go early for the herons.
- **Thu is the light** — post-frontal, north wind, Conservatory Garden.
- **Cedar Hill and Pilgrim Hill under maintenance; East Meadow closed Sat and Sun.**
- **Pollinator walk Aug 7, Ramble woodland walk Aug 14** — both back on the calendar.
