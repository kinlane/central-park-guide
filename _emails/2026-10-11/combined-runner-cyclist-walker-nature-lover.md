*Friday's email ran through the 16th, so the midweek will be familiar — the weekend is new.*

Two clear days, then two bad ones, then winter clothing. **Monday and Tuesday are open and 71°.** **Wednesday and Thursday both have East Drive held from midnight to 8 PM**, and Thursday adds a charity walk on the Lower Loop as well. Then Friday rains and the temperature falls twenty degrees — **40° on Saturday night.**

## Weather this week

Columbus Day 71°, Tuesday 71° and sunny, **Wednesday 76°** — the last warm day of the year. Thursday 67°, Friday 68° with **showers likely at 61%**, then **56° Saturday and 54° Sunday**, with 40° overnight Saturday. Sunday is sunny and cold, which is good running weather if you dress for it.

## Mon Oct 12 – Tue Oct 13 — your whole clear window

Columbus Day has one event in the park (skate circle, Dead Road, afternoon) and Tuesday has none at all. Drives clear both days, 71°, barely any rain risk.

**Put the long effort here.** After Tuesday there is not another clean day on the road until Sunday, and Sunday is 54°.

## Wed Oct 14 — 76°, and East Drive is gone until 8 PM

The warmest day left this year, and an all-day permit holds **Cedar Hill, the Hans Christian Andersen Statue and East Drive from midnight to 8 PM.** The feed calls it only "Party."

**Go clockwise on West Drive**, or take the bridle path. The east side is unusable for twenty hours.

## Thu Oct 15 — skip it

Two loop events on one day:

- **The [Ralph Lauren Pink Pony Walk](https://centralpark.guide/events/ralph-lauren-pink-pony-walk-2026-10-15/) stages at Bandshell Plaza and runs the Lower Loop, 8:30–11 AM.**
- **The East Drive permit runs a second day**, again midnight to 8 PM.

So the southern loop goes in the morning and the east side goes all day. There is no good lap available. **Bridle path, Reservoir, or rest day** — and it is 67° and mostly sunny, which is the annoying part.

## Fri Oct 16 — rain

68° with showers likely at 61%. Drives clear, but Open House New York fills Grand Army Plaza 10 AM–6 PM, so the southeast corner is busy. A short wet day or a day off.

## Sat Oct 17 — cold, and the Conservatory Garden is at its peak

56°, a 25% shower chance, drives clear. The temperature has dropped twenty degrees from Wednesday — first properly cold ride of the season, and 40° follows overnight.

For a walk instead: the **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** goes at 11 AM, behind the scenes with the staff who keep it. Mid-October is when that garden is worth the trip.

East Meadow closed; 25 private bookings on the scenic spots.

## Sun Oct 18 — the Ramble, on the best morning of the year

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 AM. Sunny, 54°, late October — if you take one guided walk this year, this is the one.

A **Making Strides Against Breast Cancer walk** holds Cedar Hill 8 AM–12 PM. The permit covers the hill, not the drives, so the east side paths will be busy but the road is open. Drives are otherwise clear all day.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is closed all day, every day — and the permits have extended a sixth time, now to **November 11**. Nine weeks elapsed.

[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/) runs five mornings from Strawberry Fields at 7:30 and 8:30. Migration is nearly over — these really are the last of them.

East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday; East Meadow closed both weekend days.

## Quick recap

- **Monday and Tuesday are your window** — 71°, clear drives, nothing booked.
- **Wednesday: East Drive held midnight–8 PM.** Go clockwise.
- **Thursday: skip it.** Pink Pony Walk on the Lower Loop 8:30–11, East Drive held all day.
- **Twenty-degree drop by Saturday**, 40° overnight. Winter kit from here.
- **Sunday 10 AM: the Ramble woodland walk** — the best walk of the year.
- **Bethesda Terrace extended again, now through November 11.**

Front-load the week,
