*Friday's email ran through the 16th, so the midweek will be familiar — the weekend is new.*

Autumn arrives mid-week. **Wednesday hits 76°, the last warm day of the year**, and by Saturday night it is 40°. Use the warm half for the gardens, and the cold half for **Open House New York** on Friday and Saturday and **the Ramble woodland walk on Sunday**, which is the best guided walk the park offers all year.

## Weather this week

Columbus Day 71°, Tuesday 71° and sunny, **Wednesday 76°**, Thursday 67°, Friday 68° with showers likely at 61%. Then the drop: **56° Saturday, 54° and sunny Sunday, 40° Saturday night.** A coat by the weekend.

## Mon Oct 12 – Tue Oct 13 — a very quiet, very pleasant start

Columbus Day brings only the **Skate Circle** at Dead Road North, 2–7 PM, reliably good to watch. Tuesday has nothing scheduled at all, at 71° and sunny.

The indoor standby all week: **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free.

## Wed Oct 14 – Thu Oct 15 — warm, and busy on the east side

Wednesday is **76°** — take the gardens while it lasts. A large private event holds Cedar Hill and the east side until 8 PM both days, so the paths over there are crowded; nothing public in it.

Thursday morning the **Ralph Lauren Pink Pony Walk** stages at Bandshell Plaza 8:30–11 AM, so the Mall carries a crowd early.

## Fri Oct 16 – Sat Oct 17 — Open House New York

**Open House New York Weekend** runs at Grand Army Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM both days — the city's annual architecture weekend, with the park as one of its sites. Friday has showers likely at 61%, so Saturday is the better bet at 56° and drier.

Saturday also brings:

- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 AM — behind the scenes with the garden staff. Mid-October is the Conservatory Garden's moment; this is the one to book.
- **CPC Yoga** on East Meadow, 11 AM–12 PM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.

## Sun Oct 18 — the Ramble, and a breast cancer walk at Cedar Hill

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 AM. Sunny, 54°, mid-October — the Ramble in autumn colour with a guide who knows it is the best thing on this week's calendar by a distance.

**The WICT New York Chapter's Making Strides Against Breast Cancer walk** takes Cedar Hill, 8 AM–12 PM. Four hours, and worth knowing about if you are on the east side in the morning.

Also: **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** is at the Arsenal all seven days — the submission window for the winter wreath show, and a genuinely lovely thing to know exists.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** closed all day, every day — permits now run to **November 11**.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Strawberry Fields. Migration is nearly done.
- **Columbus Circle Food Market** at Merchants' Gate, Thursday through Sunday.
- **114 field permits** — 83 at North Meadow, 18 on the tennis courts, 8 at Heckscher. North Meadow is carrying almost the whole league calendar now.

## Quick recap

- **Wednesday is 76°, the last warm day of the year.** 40° by Saturday night.
- **Sunday 10 AM: the Ramble woodland walk** — the best guided walk of the year.
- **Open House New York, Friday and Saturday**, Grand Army Plaza. Saturday is drier.
- **Conservatory Garden Tour Saturday 11 AM** — the garden's peak fortnight.
- **Tuesday has nothing booked** at 71°.
- **Making Strides walk at Cedar Hill Sunday**, 8 AM–12 PM.
