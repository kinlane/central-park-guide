*Friday's email ran through the 16th, so the midweek will be familiar — the weekend is new.*

This is the week summer lets go. **Wednesday hits 76°**, which will be the last warm day of the year, and by **Saturday night it is 40°** — a twenty-degree drop in three days. In between, Thursday stacks two separate loop events, and the weekend brings Open House New York and a breast cancer walk at Cedar Hill.

## Weather this week

Columbus Day is 71°, Tuesday 71° and sunny, **Wednesday 76° and the warmest day left this year.** Thursday eases to 67°, Friday brings rain at 61%, and then the floor drops: **56° Saturday, 54° Sunday, and 40° Saturday night.** Pack the week accordingly — shirtsleeves Wednesday, a coat by Saturday.

## Mon Oct 12 — Columbus Day, almost nothing booked

71° with a 22% chance of a shower. **Central Park Skate Circle** at Dead Road North, 2–7 PM, is the only thing on the board. Drives clear.

## Tue Oct 13 — 71°, nothing at all

Not one scheduled event in the park, mostly sunny. The quietest day of the week.

## Wed Oct 14 — 76°, and East Drive is held

**The warmest day remaining this year** — and an all-day permit holds Cedar Hill, the Hans Christian Andersen Statue and **East Drive from midnight to 8 PM.**

The feed titles it only "Party," which undersells twenty hours of East Drive. **Plan the east side out of the day**, or go clockwise on West Drive.

## Thu Oct 15 — two loop events, one morning

**The [Ralph Lauren Pink Pony Walk](https://centralpark.guide/events/ralph-lauren-pink-pony-walk-2026-10-15/) stages at Bandshell Plaza and runs the Lower Loop, 8:30–11 AM.** On top of that, the **East Drive permit runs a second day**, again midnight to 8 PM.

So Thursday morning the southern loop and the east side are both committed. **If you need road miles, Thursday is the day to skip** — or use the bridle path and the Reservoir. 67° and mostly sunny, which makes it a frustrating one to lose.

## Fri Oct 16 — Open House New York opens, in the rain

**Open House New York Weekend** opens at Grand Army Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM — the city's annual architecture weekend, and the park is one of its sites. 68° with **showers likely at 61%**, so check before you plan a day of it.

## Sat Oct 17 — the cold arrives, and the Conservatory Garden is turning

56° and much cooler. **Open House New York** continues at Grand Army Plaza 10 AM–6 PM.

- **[Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)** at 11 AM — behind the scenes with the staff, and mid-October is when that garden is at its best.
- **CPC Yoga** on East Meadow, 11 AM–12 PM.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs, 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9–7.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.

East Meadow closed, 25 private bookings, and 40° overnight after.

## Sun Oct 18 — a breast cancer walk at Cedar Hill, and the Ramble

**The WICT New York Chapter's Making Strides Against Breast Cancer walk takes Cedar Hill, 8 AM–12 PM.** Four hours, and the permit covers the hill rather than the drives — so expect crowds on the east side paths but no road closure.

**[Central Park Woodland Walk: The Ramble](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 AM — the Ramble in late October, on a sunny 54° morning, is the single best walk the park offers all year.

Also: **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** runs at the Arsenal all seven days — the submission window for the winter wreath show.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 11**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Strawberry Fields. Migration is nearly done.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday.
- **114 field permits** — 83 at North Meadow, 18 on the tennis courts, 8 at Heckscher. Still none on the Great Lawn.

## Quick recap

- **Wednesday is 76°** — the last warm day of the year. **Saturday night is 40°.**
- **East Drive held midnight–8 PM Wednesday AND Thursday** under a permit titled only "Party."
- **Thursday also has the Pink Pony Walk on the Lower Loop**, 8:30–11 AM. Two loop events, one morning.
- **Tuesday has nothing booked** at 71° — the pick of the week.
- **Open House New York, Friday and Saturday** at Grand Army Plaza.
- **Sunday: the Ramble woodland walk at 10 AM** — the best walk of the year, and a Making Strides walk at Cedar Hill.

Have a good week in the park,
