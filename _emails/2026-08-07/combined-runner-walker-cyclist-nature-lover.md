Two things worth planning around this week, and both are early mornings. Saturday the park's northern edge closes to cars for the morning. Sunday before dawn the bike racers have East Drive. Everything in between is heat.

## Weather this week

Upper 80s every single day, peaking at 90° Tuesday, with scattered afternoon thunderstorms threading through Friday, Saturday, Monday night and Wednesday — no clean break anywhere in the seven days. Sunday is the only fully sunny day on the board, so the honest advice is that this is a dawn week: anything hard belongs before 9 AM, and anything after 2 PM should have a storm plan.

## Sat Aug 8 — Central Park North goes car-free, 7 AM–3 PM

Summer Streets takes 110th between Frederick Douglass Circle and Adam Clayton Powell for the morning. It's a city street rather than a park drive, so it doesn't change anything about the loop — but it does hand you a wide, flat, car-free stretch along the park's northern boundary at exactly the hours worth being out. If you've been doing the same six-mile loop all summer, this is the weekend to run or ride the north end instead and come back down through the Harlem Meer.

**The East Meadow is closed all weekend** — Saturday and Sunday both — for turf restoration.

Two Conservancy walks run head to head at 11 AM: the [Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden) with the garden staff, and the [Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour) from Mariners' Gate, on the community that stood between West 82nd and West 89th before the park was built. Later on, Tango at the Shakespeare statue runs 6–9 PM — a good reason to end a hot Saturday walking the south end.

## Sun Aug 9 — CRCA has East Drive, 4:00–8:30 AM

The Central Park Racing Series is on East Drive from 4 until 8:30 in the morning. That's the whole prime window on the east side, so plan around it: go before 4 if you're genuinely that person, wait until after 8:30, or take the bridle path and the Reservoir and leave the drive to the peloton. Don't plan a quiet Sunday sunrise on East Drive this week. [Details](https://centralpark.guide/events/crca-central-park-racing-series-2026-08-09/)

Once they clear, Sunday is the only mostly-sunny day of the week at 89°, and the good stuff is all uptown:

- **[Uptown Royalty](https://centralpark.guide/events/uptown-royalty-2026-08-09/)** at the Dana Discovery Center, 2 PM — Latin, jazz and dance at the Harlem Meer
- **[Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk)** from Boys' Gate, 11 AM — the butterfly garden and what's feeding in it right now
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza from 11 AM, which has been happening on Sundays for decades and is worth slowing down for

The East Meadow is still closed.

## Fri Aug 7 — pollinator walk at 11

Today's one thing is the [Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk) from Boys' Gate at 11 AM, through the summer flowers in the north end. Storms are possible this evening.

## Mon–Thu Aug 10–13 — lawns roped off, and the week's hottest day

Quiet on programming, busy on maintenance. Rolling lawn closures run Monday through Thursday, and four are worth knowing about:

- **Tuesday** — Bowling Greens closed all day; East Meadow and the Pond Lawn closed until 2 PM
- **Thursday** — Billy Johnson Lawn closed until 2 PM

Tuesday is also the hottest day of the week at 90° with afternoon storms behind it. Wednesday has nothing scheduled at all, which at 87° and showery is about right.

## Steady all week

The [Iconic Views walking tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour) leaves Doris C. Freedman Plaza at 10 AM most days. Lap swim at the Gottesman Pool runs 7–8:30 AM at the Davis Center. And the Arsenal has *40 in Focus*, four decades of SummerStage photography, free and open 9–5.

## Quick recap

- **Saturday 7 AM–3 PM** — 110th goes car-free; take the north end while you can
- **Sunday 4–8:30 AM** — bike racing owns East Drive; bridle path or Reservoir instead
- **All weekend** — East Meadow closed
- **Tuesday** — Bowling Greens closed all day, hottest day at 90°
- **Sunday 2 PM** — Uptown Royalty at the Harlem Meer
- **Every day** — upper 80s to 90°, storms on and off; go early

Have a good week out there,
