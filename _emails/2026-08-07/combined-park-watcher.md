A quiet week on the calendar and a busy one on the ground. The operational story is turf: rolling lawn closures run Monday straight through Friday, with four separate lawns taken out of service. The programming story is that SummerStage now effectively holds Rumsey Playfield continuously — the permit covers the weekend and returns Friday for a television broadcast.

## Weather this week

Upper 80s every day through Thursday, peaking at 90° Tuesday, with scattered thunderstorms Saturday, Monday night and Wednesday. Sustained heat like this is what drives the August lawn-closure schedule below — turf on the heavily-programmed lawns doesn't recover between bookings without it.

## Sat Aug 8 – Sun Aug 9 — the weekend footprint

The **East Meadow is closed both days** for turf restoration, the first of five separate lawn closures this week.

**SummerStage holds Rumsey Playfield both days** under an all-day August festival permit rather than per-show bookings — worth noting as a pattern, since it means the venue is effectively unavailable for anything else through the season rather than show by show.

Sunday 4:00–8:30 AM, **CRCA's Central Park Racing Series has East Drive**. This is the recurring club series rather than a one-off, and it holds the east side through the entire pre-8:30 window.

Also Saturday: **Summer Streets takes Central Park North 7 AM–3 PM**, week two of three. NYC DOT's open-streets program now touches the park's northern boundary on three consecutive August Saturdays — a comparatively new fixture on the park's perimeter calendar, and one that appears in the city permit feed rather than any Parks listing.

## Mon Aug 10 – Fri Aug 14 — five lawn closures in five days

The maintenance block is the week:

- **Tuesday** — Bowling Greens closed all day; East Meadow and the Pond Lawn closed until 2 PM
- **Thursday** — Billy Johnson Lawn closed until 2 PM
- **Friday** — Bowling Green closed until 2 PM
- **Monday through Thursday** — rolling lawn maintenance at the East 106th Street Lawn

Two things stand out. The Bowling Greens come out of service twice in four days, which is heavier than the usual August cadence. And the East Meadow is closed for the weekend *and* again Tuesday morning — a sign the restoration there is ongoing rather than a single booking gap.

Wednesday has nothing at all on the calendar, programmed or operational.

## Fri Aug 14 — a broadcast, and the Rangers overnight

**Good Morning America has Rumsey Playfield 12 AM–noon**, which is a media booking rather than a concert and takes the venue for the morning.

The **Urban Park Rangers run overnight family camping** at two sites the same night — Strangers' Gate at 106th and Central Park West, and the Great Hill. Two simultaneous overnight programs in the north end is unusual; typically these run one site at a time.

## Next Saturday

Aug 15 brings NYRR's Marathon Training Series 12M to East Drive 7–10:30 AM — the first of the two-race series that runs up to the November marathon — alongside the third and final Summer Streets on Central Park North.

## Quick recap

- **Five separate lawn closures** Monday to Friday; Bowling Greens twice in four days
- **East Meadow** closed the full weekend and again Tuesday morning — ongoing restoration
- **SummerStage holds Rumsey** all weekend on a festival-wide permit, and again Friday for GMA
- **Summer Streets** takes Central Park North Saturday, week two of three
- **Wednesday** is empty — nothing programmed or scheduled
- **Aug 15** — NYRR 12M on East Drive, and the last Summer Streets
