One email this week instead of ten — everything below is drawn from all the interests you picked, sorted by day rather than by topic. Tonight is a free SummerStage show, Saturday morning the park's northern edge goes car-free, and Sunday before dawn the bike racers have East Drive. The rest of the week is heat.

## Weather this week

Upper 80s every single day, peaking at 90° Tuesday, with scattered afternoon thunderstorms threading through Friday, Saturday, Monday night and Wednesday — no clean break anywhere in the seven days. Sunday is the only fully sunny day on the board, which makes early mornings the whole game this week: anything strenuous belongs before 9 AM, and anything outdoors after 2 PM should have a plan for a storm.

## Tonight, Fri Aug 7 — Funk Flex birthday show at Rumsey, 7–10 PM

A free SummerStage bill at Rumsey Playfield: Kurtis Blow, Jon B, Fonda Rae, Taana Gardner, Strafe, Dres from Black Sheep, with Funk Flex on the decks. Gates well before 7 and this one will fill — it's a birthday R&B and classic hip-hop lineup with real draw. Storms are possible into the evening, so bring a layer you don't mind getting wet.

Earlier in the day, the Summer Pollinator Walk leaves from Boys' Gate at 11 AM for the butterfly garden, and the Arsenal has *40 in Focus*, four decades of SummerStage photography by Jack Vartoogian, free and open 9–5 all week.

## Sat Aug 8 — the busiest day of the week

**Summer Streets comes to Central Park North, 7 AM–3 PM.** The stretch of 110th between Frederick Douglass Circle and Adam Clayton Powell goes car-free for the morning. This is the park's northern edge, so it pairs naturally with the Harlem Meer and the North Woods — and it's the reason to point yourself uptown this weekend rather than toward the usual south-end crowds. [Details](https://centralpark.guide/events/summer-streets-central-park-week-2-2026-08-08/)

The rest of Saturday, roughly in the order you'd hit them walking north:

- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM — Conservatory Water, always worth ten minutes
- **Storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue**, 10 AM–1 PM — the long-running Saturday session
- **Conservatory Garden Tour**, 11 AM, and the **Seneca Village Tour** from Mariners' Gate, 11 AM — two Conservancy walks running head to head
- **Arise New York** at Bandshell Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM
- **Skate Circle** on Dead Road, 2–7 PM
- **Jack in the Park**, amphitheater edition at Summit Rock, 4–7 PM
- **Tango at the Shakespeare statue**, 6–9 PM — the nicest way to end a hot Saturday

**Heads up:** the East Meadow is closed all weekend for turf restoration, Saturday and Sunday both.

## Sun Aug 9 — racers on East Drive before dawn, then the best weather of the week

**CRCA's Central Park Racing Series has East Drive from 4:00 to 8:30 AM.** If you run, ride or walk the loop on Sunday mornings, this is the one to plan around — the peloton owns the road through the whole early window. Go before 4, after 8:30, or take the bridle path and the Reservoir. Don't count on a quiet Sunday sunrise on the east side. [Details](https://centralpark.guide/events/crca-central-park-racing-series-2026-08-09/)

Once they clear out, Sunday is the only mostly-sunny day of the week at 89°:

- **Uptown Royalty** at the Dana Discovery Center, 2 PM — Latin, jazz and dance up at the Harlem Meer, and the pick of the week if you only do one thing. [Details](https://centralpark.guide/events/uptown-royalty-2026-08-09/)
- **Summer on the Harlem Meer**, 9 AM–6 PM, same end of the park — pair it with the above
- **Embodied Expression Yoga and Art** on the Great Lawn, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM onward — a Central Park Sunday institution
- **Summer Street Theater** at Bandshell Plaza, 2 PM
- **Skate Circle** again on Dead Road, 2–7 PM
- Another **Summer Pollinator Walk** from Boys' Gate at 11 AM

## Mon–Thu Aug 10–13 — quiet, hot, and a lot of lawns roped off

The weekday half of the week is thin on programming and heavy on maintenance. Rolling lawn closures run Monday through Thursday, and three specific ones are worth knowing: the **Bowling Greens are shut all day Tuesday**, and both the **East Meadow** and **the Pond Lawn** are closed until 2 PM Tuesday. **Billy Johnson Lawn** closes until 2 PM Thursday.

Tuesday is the hottest day of the week at 90° with afternoon storms. Wednesday has nothing scheduled at all — which, at 87° and showery, is probably the right amount.

## Steady all week

Things that run most days and don't need repeating each time: the **Gottesman Pool lap swim** at the Davis Center, 7–8:30 AM; the **Columbus Circle food market** at Merchants' Gate, 9:30 AM–9 PM; the **Iconic Views walking tour** from Doris C. Freedman Plaza at 10 AM; **CP Sports summer program** on South Dead Road, mornings and afternoons; and the free **Vartoogian photography exhibit** at the Arsenal, 9–5.

The ballfields are booked more or less solid — around 360 permits across North Meadow, the Great Lawn and Heckscher this week. Normal August.

## Quick recap

- **Tonight** — Funk Flex and a free classic-R&B bill at Rumsey, 7 PM
- **Saturday morning** — Central Park North goes car-free 7 AM–3 PM; go uptown
- **Sunday 4–8:30 AM** — bike racing owns East Drive; the loop is not yours
- **Sunday 2 PM** — Uptown Royalty at the Harlem Meer, the week's best hour
- **All weekend** — East Meadow closed; Tuesday, Bowling Greens closed all day
- **Every day** — upper 80s to 90°, storms on and off; move early
