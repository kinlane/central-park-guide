NYRR has claimed the whole week for its third Member Week, and it ends Saturday with a sold-out members-only 10K that holds both drives until noon. Around it: two of SummerStage's biggest nights of the season, the emptiest Thursday in a month, and a Saturday with something on nearly every lawn.

## Weather this week

Monday is wet with thunderstorms, then Tuesday and Wednesday go clear and hot — 85° and 87°, the week's peak — before storms return Thursday and the weekend turns showery in the low 80s. Front-load anything demanding onto Tuesday and Wednesday at dawn, and let Thursday's empty calendar and unsettled sky be the rest day.

## Mon Aug 17 — wet, and just the Bandshell

Thunderstorms at 83°. [Bill Popp and the Tapes](https://centralpark.guide/events/bill-popp-and-the-tapes-2026-08-17/) plays Bandshell Plaza 5–7 PM. Lawn work closes the East 106th Street lawn all day and the Wild West Playground lawn to 2 PM. Nothing on the drives.

## Tue Aug 18 — the best morning, then Chance the Rapper

Clear and 85°. **No permits on the drives** — this and Wednesday are the week's two clean loop mornings, so go early.

In the evening, [Chance the Rapper](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/chance-the-rapper/) brings the *Coloring Book* 10-year anniversary tour to Rumsey Playfield at 7 PM with La Reezy. The Fifth Avenue side around 72nd gets busy from late afternoon.

Bowling Green closes all day, its third closure in two weeks — that lawn is on a restoration program now, not a rotation.

## Wed Aug 19 — hottest day, pop-punk at Rumsey

87° and sunny, the week's peak. Drives are clear again. [Simple Plan, 3OH!3 and Bowling for Soup](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/simple-3-soup/) play Rumsey at 6 PM — a warm night to be standing on that field.

## Thu Aug 20 — nothing scheduled anywhere

No one-off permits in the entire park. Storms and 81°. If the rain breaks, this is as empty as Central Park gets.

## Fri Aug 21 — pollinators, a festival, a picnic

- **11 AM** — [Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk) from Boys' Gate, through the park's secret butterfly garden
- **3–8 PM** — Rubyboo Entertainment Music Festival at Bandshell Plaza
- **5:30–8 PM** — the Public Theater's picnic at the Delacorte, on the Great Lawn's southwest edge

## Sat Aug 22, 7:30 AM–12 PM — Grete's Great Gallop holds the loop

**The loop is unusable all morning.** The permit covers West Drive, East Drive, the Center Loop, the 72nd Street Cross Drive and Bandshell Plaza from 7:30 AM to **noon** — the hours past the finish are the post-race festival, so the road doesn't come back at 10. Run or ride before 7 AM or after noon, or take the Bridle Path or the Reservoir.

It's members-only, sold out, and the standby list is full — a spectating morning unless you already hold a bib. The course is Grete Waitz's old Mini 10K loop, where she won five titles and set world records in 1979 and 1980.

Then the rest of the day opens up:

- **9 AM–3 PM** — Model Yacht Racing at Kerbs Boathouse
- **9 AM–5 PM** — Chess Lecture Simul Series at the Chess and Checkers House
- **10 AM–1 PM** — Storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue
- **11 AM** — [Ramble Woodland Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour) and the [Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden)
- **12–6 PM** — That Guitar Man From Central Park at Hernshead
- **2–7 PM** — Skate Circle at Dead Road
- **5–8 PM** — Carlos Konig Salsa at the Bandshell; **6–9 PM** — Tango at the Shakespeare Statue
- **6–7:30 PM** — Breathwork on the Great Lawn, if you want the quiet end of a loud day

East Meadow is closed all day for restoration, and again Sunday.

## Sun Aug 23 — the loop returns, Kidjo closes the week

Drives are clear. [Angélique Kidjo](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/angelique-kidjo/) headlines Rumsey at 7 PM with Cimafunk and DJ Freshy K, preceded at 4:30 by the free [Voices of Power](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/voices-of-power/) panel on African women in media and diplomacy (RSVP). Elsewhere: [Folkdancers](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-international-folkdancers-2026-08-23/) at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–8 PM, the [Ray Delgado Project](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/ray-delgado-project) at the Dana Discovery Center at 2 PM, the Skate Circle again, and the Pollinator Walk repeating at 11 AM.

## Quick recap

- **Sat 7:30 AM–12 PM: the loop is closed.** Grete's Gallop plus its Bandshell festival — before 7, after noon, or Bridle Path and Reservoir.
- **Tue and Wed are the clean loop mornings** — clear, hot, nothing permitted on the drives.
- **Chance the Rapper Tue 7 PM; Angélique Kidjo Sun 7 PM**, both at Rumsey.
- **Saturday afternoon has everything** — model yachts, chess, storytelling, two Conservancy tours, salsa, tango, breathwork.
- **Thursday is empty** — no permits anywhere in the park.

Have a good week out there,
Central Park Guide
