The operational story this week is small — two lawn days and a weekend meadow closure. The institutional story is bigger: New York Road Runners has claimed the entire week for its third annual Member Week, and it ends Saturday with a race on the park drives that no non-member could register for.

## Weather this week

A wet Monday gives way to two clear, hot days — 85° Tuesday, 87° Wednesday, the week's peak — before thunderstorms return Thursday and a showery pattern settles over the weekend. The heat lands on the two emptiest days on the calendar, which is the same favorable timing the turf work got last week.

## Mon Aug 17 – Thu Aug 20 — the lawn rotation, and a familiar name

Four days carrying almost nothing but maintenance.

- **Mon Aug 17:** East 106th Street lawn (south mount) closed all day; Wild West Playground lawn closed to 2 PM
- **Tue Aug 18:** East 106th Street lawn closed all day; **Bowling Green closed all day**
- **Wed Aug 19 – Thu Aug 20:** no one-off permits at all; East 106th Street lawn work continues through Thursday

Bowling Green is worth flagging for the second week running. It closed all day Tuesday and again Friday afternoon last week; it closes all day Tuesday again this week. Three all-day or half-day closures across two weeks is a restoration program, not a rotation.

## Fri Aug 21 — the Bandshell picks back up

[Rubyboo Entertainment Music Festival](https://centralpark.guide/events/rubyboo-entertainment-music-festival-2026-08-21/) holds Bandshell Plaza 3–8 PM. Notable mostly as a marker: with Naumburg's season closed on August 4, the Bandshell has reverted from its formal concert programming to permitted community use for the rest of the summer.

## Sat Aug 22 — Member Week ends on the drives

[NYRR Grete's Great Gallop 10K](https://centralpark.guide/events/nyrr-gretes-great-gallop-10k-2026-08-22/) runs 7:30 AM, with the permit holding West Drive, East Drive, the Center Loop, the 72nd Street Cross Drive and Bandshell Plaza until **noon** — the extra hours past the finish are the post-race festival.

Two things make this one worth naming rather than logging:

- **It is closed to the public.** Registration is members-only, it is sold out, and the member standby list is full. A permitted event holding five parcels of public parkland for four and a half hours, which no member of the public could buy into, is a different category from the open races that normally use the loop.
- **It anchors a week, not a morning.** This is NYRR's third Member Week (Aug 17–22) — a full week of member programming culminating in the race and festival. The pattern to watch is a running organization moving from race-day permits toward week-long institutional presence in the park.

Also Saturday: East Meadow closed all day for maintenance and restoration, running through Sunday.

## Sun Aug 23 — the meadow stays down

East Meadow closed a second full day. Otherwise the day belongs to SummerStage at Rumsey, which winds toward the end of its season.

## Quick recap

- **NYRR Member Week, Aug 17–22** — third annual; a week-long institutional claim, not a race-day permit.
- **Sat Aug 22, 7:30 AM–12 PM** — Grete's Great Gallop 10K holds five parcels including both drives and the Center Loop. Members-only, sold out, standby full.
- **Bowling Green, closed all day Tuesday** — third closure in two weeks. This is restoration.
- **East Meadow, closed Sat and Sun** — full weekend down.
- **Naumburg is done for the year** — season closed Aug 4; the Bandshell is back to permitted community use.
