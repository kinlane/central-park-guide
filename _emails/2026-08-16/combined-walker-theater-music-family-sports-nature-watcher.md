Two of SummerStage's biggest nights of the season land this week — Chance the Rapper Tuesday and Angélique Kidjo Sunday — with a Saturday in between that has salsa, tango, chess, model yachts, storytelling and a race all running at once. It's the fullest week the park has had in a while.

## Weather this week

Monday is wet, Tuesday and Wednesday go clear and hot at 85° and 87°, then thunderstorms Thursday and a showery, mid-80s weekend. The two big outdoor nights sit on the right side of the weather — Tuesday is dry and clear, and Sunday's showers should be scattered rather than settled.

## Mon Aug 17 — Bill Popp at the Bandshell

[Bill Popp and the Tapes](https://centralpark.guide/events/bill-popp-and-the-tapes-2026-08-17/) plays Bandshell Plaza 5–7 PM. A wet day otherwise, and the only thing on the calendar.

## Tue Aug 18 — Chance the Rapper at Rumsey

[Chance the Rapper](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/chance-the-rapper/) brings the *Coloring Book* 10-year anniversary tour to Rumsey Playfield at 7 PM, with La Reezy opening. Clear and 85° — the best evening weather of the week for it. Expect the Fifth Avenue side around 72nd to be busy from late afternoon.

## Wed Aug 19 — Simple Plan, 3OH!3 and Bowling for Soup

Rumsey again at 6 PM for a [three-band pop-punk bill](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/simple-3-soup/). The hottest day of the week at 87°, so it will be a warm one standing on the field.

## Thu Aug 20 — nothing scheduled

No one-off permits anywhere in the park. Storms and 81°. The quietest day of the week if you want the place to yourself.

## Fri Aug 21 — pollinators, a festival and a picnic at the Delacorte

- **11 AM** — [Summer Pollinator Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/summer-pollinator-walk), meeting at Boys' Gate. The Conservancy's walk through the park's secret butterfly garden.
- **3–8 PM** — Rubyboo Entertainment Music Festival takes Bandshell Plaza for the afternoon and evening.
- **5:30–8 PM** — the Public Theater's picnic at the Delacorte, on the Great Lawn's southwest edge.

## Sat Aug 22 — the full day

Saturday has something on nearly every lawn. The morning belongs to the [NYRR Grete's Great Gallop 10K](https://centralpark.guide/events/nyrr-gretes-great-gallop-10k-2026-08-22/), 7:30 AM to noon on the park drives — a members-only race, sold out, closing NYRR's Member Week with a festival at the Bandshell. Good to watch, not to enter, and the drives are busy until midday.

After that it opens up:

- **9 AM–3 PM** — Model Yacht Racing at Kerbs Boathouse on Conservatory Water
- **9 AM–5 PM** — Chess Lecture Simul Series with beginners' workshops at the Chess and Checkers House
- **10 AM–1 PM** — Storytelling at the Hans Christian Andersen statue
- **11 AM** — [Ramble Woodland Walk](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/the-ramble-tour) from Belvedere Castle, and the [Conservatory Garden Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/conservatory-garden) at the north end
- **12–6 PM** — That Guitar Man From Central Park at Hernshead
- **2–7 PM** — Central Park Skate Circle at Dead Road
- **5–8 PM** — Carlos Konig Salsa at Bandshell Plaza
- **6–9 PM** — Tango at the Shakespeare Statue

East Meadow is closed all day for restoration, and again Sunday.

## Sun Aug 23 — Kidjo closes the week

[Angélique Kidjo](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/angelique-kidjo/) headlines Rumsey at 7 PM with Cimafunk and DJ Freshy K — Afrobeat, jazz, reggae and West African rhythm from one of the great live performers working. Before it, at 4:30 PM, [Voices of Power](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/voices-of-power/) runs as a free pre-show panel on African women in media and diplomacy (RSVP required).

Elsewhere Sunday: [Central Park International Folkdancers](https://centralpark.guide/events/central-park-international-folkdancers-2026-08-23/) at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–8 PM, the [Ray Delgado Project](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/ray-delgado-project) at the Dana Discovery Center at 2 PM, the Skate Circle again 2–7 PM, and the Summer Pollinator Walk repeating at 11 AM.

## Quick recap

- **Chance the Rapper, Tue 7 PM at Rumsey** — clear and 85°, the week's best concert weather.
- **Angélique Kidjo, Sun 7 PM at Rumsey**, with a free Voices of Power panel at 4:30.
- **Saturday is the full day** — race in the morning, then model yachts, chess, storytelling, two Conservancy tours, salsa and tango.
- **Summer Pollinator Walk, Fri and Sun 11 AM** at Boys' Gate.
- **Thursday has nothing on it** — the quietest day of the week.

Enjoy the last stretch of summer,
