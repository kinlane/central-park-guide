Hi all,

**Sunday morning the drives belong to NYRR.** The Race to Deliver 4M goes off at 8, and the permit holds ground until 11. After that the week gives you exactly one warm day and then shuts the door on the season.

## Weather this week

Sunday is 62° with rain likely and a downpour overnight, **Monday rebounds to 65° and mostly sunny** — and then it is over: 48° Tuesday, 47° Wednesday, **a 21 mph, rainy 50° Thanksgiving**, 54° Friday. **Monday is the last mild day in the forecast**, so if you have been saving a long ride or a long run, that is where it goes.

## Sun Nov 22 — the loop is unusable until 11

**Race to Deliver 4M**, the 33rd running, benefiting God's Love We Deliver. Race 8–10 AM; **the permit covers the 72nd Street and 102nd Street cross drives and the Center Loop through 11 AM.**

- **Before 8** is clear, but it is 62° and raining — mild for the date, genuinely wet.
- **After 11** the drives come back and the rain is still around.
- **The Reservoir and the bridle path** are untouched throughout.

Worth noting that this race only shows as a loop closure in our data because of a fix we made last week. The permit names two *cross drives* and the *Center Loop* — and cross drives correctly do not count as the loop. "Center Loop" was missing from our vocabulary until Nov 14. Without that one-line addition, Sunday would have read as an open morning in this email.

Also out: **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** 2 PM from Mariners' Gate — the Conservancy walk on the community that lived here before the park, and the best thing in it this week. **Birding Bob at the Boathouse** 7:30 AM. East Meadow closed.

## Mon Nov 23 — 65°, clearing, and nothing in your way

Early showers, then mostly sunny and **65°**. Nothing booked anywhere in the park, no race, no closure on the drives.

**Take this day.** Tuesday is seventeen degrees colder and it does not come back.

**Birding Bob at Strawberry Fields**, 8:30 AM. East 106th Street lawn maintenance starts, Mon–Thu — north end, off the drives.

## Tue Nov 24 – Wed Nov 25 — cold, clear, and a marching band at the Bandshell

48° then 47°, sunny both, wind 8–9 mph. Good hard running weather, cold riding weather, excellent walking weather.

Two permits at Bandshell Plaza you would otherwise never hear about: **Pearland High School's band Tuesday 9–10 AM**, and **the UConn marching band Wednesday 10 AM–3 PM.** Both are marching in Thursday's Macy's parade and both are rehearsing in the open. If your route goes past the Bandshell either morning, slow down.

**Birding Bob: Boathouse Thursday 8:30, Conservatory Garden Friday 8:30** — the Garden stop is his best, and a cold clear morning there is worth the detour.

## Thu Nov 26 — Thanksgiving, and the whole west side is the parade

**The 100th Macy's parade steps off at W 77th and Central Park West at 8:30 AM** and runs south down CPW. Nothing is permitted inside the park, but **the entire western boundary is a parade route** — every west-side entrance from 77th down to Columbus Circle is unusable through the late morning.

**Run or ride the east side, or go north of 86th.** It is 50°, raining (72%), and blowing 21 mph, so it is a day for a short honest effort rather than anything long.

## Fri Nov 27 – Sat Nov 28 — the park comes back

Friday 54°, chance of light rain, nothing booked. Saturday:

- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** 10 AM; **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** 2 PM.
- **Birding Bob at the Boathouse** 7:30 AM. East Meadow closed.
- **No loop impact either day.**

## Worth knowing

- **Bethesda Terrace, the Swedish Cottage lawn, the East 96th Street playground lawn and Harlem Meer are all closed all week.** None of them touches a path you run or ride.
- **I have to correct something I have been telling you.** I reported for a month that these closures all ended on a shared date in December that kept moving. That was an artifact of how the city publishes the data — it only lists a recurring permit about a month out from whenever you ask, so everything appears to stop at that edge. I verified it this week by querying one permit directly and getting exactly 31 days. **There is no visible end date on any of them.** I was reading the edge of a window as a fact about the park.

Have a good week out there,
Kin
