Hi there,

**The best thing this week is hiding in the permit list.** On Tuesday and Wednesday, **two marching bands from the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade rehearse at the Bandshell, outdoors, for free.** No listing, no announcement, no ticket — just a permit.

Then Thursday the parade itself comes down the park's western edge.

## Weather this week

Sunday is 62° and wet with heavy rain overnight, **Monday is a mild 65° and clearing**, and then the season turns: 48° Tuesday, 47° Wednesday, and **a rainy, blustery 50° Thanksgiving**. Monday is the one day to be outdoors without thinking about it; from Tuesday it is coats, and Thursday is a stand-still-in-the-rain kind of morning.

## Sun Nov 22 — Seneca Village, and a race on the drives

- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, **2 PM from Mariners' Gate at W 85th.** Before the park existed, the land from W 82nd to W 89th was Seneca Village — the largest community of African-American property owners in the city, displaced to build this. The Conservancy guides it. **It is the most worthwhile hour in the park right now**, and it runs again Saturday.
- **Race to Deliver 4M**, 8–10 AM on the drives, benefiting God's Love We Deliver. The 33rd running. Pleasant to watch from the 72nd Street area, and the drives are held to 11.
- **International Folkdancers**, King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM. Open circle, no experience needed, and they are there every Sunday.
- **East Meadow closed.** 62° with rain likely — bring the umbrella.

## Mon Nov 23 — 65° and completely empty

Nothing booked in the whole park, showers clearing to mostly sunny, and the warmest day left in the forecast. If there is a walk you have been meaning to take, this is the morning.

## Tue Nov 24, 9–10 AM — the Pride of Pearland at the Bandshell

**Pearland High School's band, from Texas, holds Bandshell Plaza 9 to 10 AM.** They were chosen from more than 100 applicants to march in Thursday's parade — the 100th — and they are rehearsing here first.

A full competition marching band, in the open air, on a bright 48° morning. Free.

## Wed Nov 25, 10 AM–3 PM — and five hours of the UConn band

**The Pride of Connecticut Marching Band** takes Bandshell Plaza **10 AM to 3 PM**, also ahead of Thursday's parade. Five hours at 47° and sunny.

**Wednesday evening is balloon inflation** by the Museum of Natural History on the west side — a long-standing tradition and a serious crowd. Worth choosing deliberately rather than stumbling into.

## Thu Nov 26 — Thanksgiving and the 100th parade

**The parade steps off at W 77th and Central Park West at 8:30 AM** and runs south down CPW to Columbus Circle. Nothing is permitted inside the park itself, but the **whole west side is the route**, so plan the east side or the north end if you want the park at all.

**50°, rain likely at 72%, wind about 21 mph.** The city grounds the big balloons when sustained wind passes 23 mph or gusts pass 34 — **this forecast sits just below that**, so whether they fly gets decided on the morning. Not a prediction, just the number to watch.

## Fri Nov 27 – Sat Nov 28 — the quiet after

Friday has nothing booked at all, 54° with a chance of light rain.

Saturday is the week's second good day out:

- **[Iconic Views of Central Park tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 10 AM from Doris C. Freedman Plaza.
- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** again, 2 PM.
- **Frisbee at North Meadow**, 9 AM–5 PM, and **East Meadow closed**.

## What the permits say

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is closed every day this week**, under two separate overlapping permits with different titles at the same location.
- **Three lawns are closed all week**: Swedish Cottage, the East 96th Street playground lawn, and Harlem Meer.
- **17 field permits** this week, nine at North Meadow — down from 223 at the September peak. The season has effectively ended.

**The permit feed publishes no end date for any of these closures**, so I cannot tell you when Bethesda, the Swedish Cottage lawn, the E 96th playground lawn or Harlem Meer reopen. If you pass a posted notice at one of them, I would like to know what it says — that is the one source a data feed cannot reach.
