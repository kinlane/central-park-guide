Hi,

**Two things worth planning around.** The **Race to Deliver 4M closes the drives Sunday morning**, and on **Tuesday and Wednesday two marching bands from the 100th Macy's parade rehearse at the Bandshell, in the open, for free** — which nobody announces anywhere.

Then Thanksgiving itself arrives wet and windy.

## Weather this week

A warm, wet front sits on Sunday and Monday — **62° with rain likely Sunday and heavy rain overnight**, then a mild 65° Monday — before the real cold arrives: 48° and sunny Tuesday, 47° Wednesday, and **a rainy, 21 mph Thanksgiving at 50°**. Do the outdoor things Monday, take warm layers from Tuesday on, and treat Thursday as an indoor day with a parade attached.

## Sun Nov 22, 8–10 AM — Race to Deliver 4M closes the drives

**The 33rd running**, a four-mile NYRR race benefiting God's Love We Deliver. The race goes off at 8 and finishes around 10; **the permit holds the 72nd and 102nd Street cross drives and the Center Loop until 11.**

Run, ride or walk **before 8 or after 11**, or stay on the bridle path and the Reservoir. It is also 62° with a 62% chance of rain, so this is not a morning to wait out on a bench.

Elsewhere that day:

- **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)**, 2 PM from Mariners' Gate (W 85th). The Conservancy on the Black community that lived here before the park did — and the most worthwhile hour in the park right now.
- **International Folkdancers**, King Jagiello Plaza, 11 AM–3 PM.
- **Birding Bob at the Boathouse**, 7:30 AM. **East Meadow closed.**

**Heavy rain Sunday night** — 99%.

## Mon Nov 23 — 65°, nothing booked, and the last warm day

Showers clear in the morning and it turns mostly sunny at **65°**. Not one public event anywhere in the park.

**This is the warm day. There is not another one in the forecast.** From Tuesday the highs are in the 40s.

**Birding Bob at Strawberry Fields**, 8:30 AM. Lawn maintenance starts at the East 106th Street Lawn, Mon–Thu.

## Tue Nov 24, 9–10 AM — the Pride of Pearland rehearses at the Bandshell

**Pearland High School's band, from Pearland, Texas, has a permit for Bandshell Plaza, 9 to 10 AM.** They were picked from more than 100 applicants to march in **the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade** on Thursday.

So: a full competition marching band, in the park, on a sunny 48° Tuesday morning, for nothing. It is on the permit list and nowhere else.

## Wed Nov 25, 10 AM–3 PM — and the UConn band takes the Bandshell for five hours

**The Pride of Connecticut Marching Band**, also in Thursday's parade, holds Bandshell Plaza **10 AM to 3 PM**. Five hours, 47° and sunny.

Two parade bands in two days is not a coincidence of the calendar — it is what Thanksgiving week looks like in the permit record, and it is the best free music of the month.

**Wednesday evening is also balloon inflation** on the west side by the Museum of Natural History, which is a crowd worth deciding about in advance rather than walking into.

## Thu Nov 26 — Thanksgiving, and the parade comes down the park's west edge

**The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade**, from **W 77th and Central Park West at 8:30 AM**, south along CPW to Columbus Circle. The park itself has nothing booked — but its entire western boundary is the parade route, so the west side is effectively unreachable all morning.

**50°, light rain likely (72%), and wind around 21 mph.** The city's long-standing rule grounds the big balloons when sustained winds pass 23 mph or gusts pass 34. **This forecast sits just under that line**, which does not mean they will fly — it means it will be decided on the morning.

If you want the park on Thursday, go to the **east side or the north end**, and go early.

## Fri Nov 27 – Sat Nov 28 — quiet, cool, and the tours come back

Friday is 54° with a chance of light rain and nothing booked at all.

Saturday:

- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)**, 10 AM, and the **[Seneca Village Tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/seneca-village-tour)** again at 2 PM.
- **Birding Bob at the Boathouse**, 7:30 AM. **East Meadow closed.** Frisbee at North Meadow, 9 AM–5 PM.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain is closed every day**, under two overlapping permits.
- **Three lawns are closed all week** — Swedish Cottage, East 96th Street playground, and Harlem Meer.
- **17 field permits**, nine at North Meadow. The season is nearly over.

**A correction about those closures.** For the last month I have been telling you they all end on the same day in December, and that the shared date kept moving — and I said it looked like something was scheduled. **That was wrong, and the cause was mine.** The city's permit data only lists a recurring permit's days about a month ahead of whenever you ask it. So every one of these closures appears to stop at the edge of that window, and the "end date" moved every week because the window moved with the calendar. I checked it properly this week by querying a single permit directly: it returns exactly 31 days and stops.

**These closures have no visible end date.** I do not know when Bethesda reopens, and neither does this dataset. I should have tested that a month ago instead of building a story on it.

## Quick recap

- **Sunday 8–10 AM: Race to Deliver 4M.** Drives held to 11. Go before 8 or after 11.
- **Monday is 65° and empty** — the last warm day in the forecast.
- **Tuesday 9 AM and Wednesday 10 AM–3 PM: two Macy's parade bands rehearse free at the Bandshell.**
- **Thanksgiving: the 100th parade** down CPW from 8:30, wet and windy, balloons a game-time call.
- **Bethesda and three lawns stay closed** — and I can no longer tell you until when.
