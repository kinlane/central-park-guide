Fall lands this weekend and does not leave. Saturday is partly sunny and 71°; after that the park does not reach 70° again all week, and every remaining day carries a shower chance. Sunday is the sharp one — an 89% chance of rain from dawn to dark, sitting directly on top of a five-hour marathon training run.

## Weather this week

Saturday: 71°, partly sunny, dry. Sunday: 71° and rain, 89%, all day. Then 67°, 63°, 65°, 64°, 66° with showers possible every single day. Get your good miles in Saturday morning, and expect to be wet for the rest of the week — this is the turn, not a passing system.

## Sat Sep 19 — the dry day, with two things on the west side

**The 28th Annual SCTPN Walk for Education and Awareness** stages at **Dead Road North, 9 AM–1 PM** — four hours of a charity walk on the west side. It is not a drive closure, but the Dead Road approaches and the paths around Sheep Meadow will be dense through midday. Worth knowing before you commit to a counterclockwise lap.

The **Osborn Cup** holds Bowling Green 7 AM–6 PM both weekend days, and the **25th Annual Chess-in-the-Park Rapid Open** fills Bandshell Plaza 11 AM–5 PM — neither on your line, both worth slowing down for.

The **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** leaves Doris C. Freedman Plaza at 2 PM. East Meadow is closed, and 40 private bookings — a season record — have the scenic south end thoroughly spoken for.

**Ride or run early.** This is the last dry morning in this email.

## Sun Sep 20, 7 AM–1:30 PM — the loop belongs to the 18-milers, in the rain

**[The TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 18M](https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-18m)** goes off at 7 AM on East Drive and the 102nd Street Cross Drive, permit held to 1:30 PM. Eighteen miles is the long one in the marathon build, and the field will be out there for five hours under an **89% chance of rain**.

**Cyclists: take Sunday off, or ride after 1:30.** There is no early window — the run starts at seven. Runners working around it: bridle path or the Reservoir. Runners in it: dress for a soaking, and know the roads will be slick on the descents.

If you are out for a walk instead, the **International Folkdancers** hold King Jagiello 11–6 and jazz plays the Bandshell 1–4 — but the honest recommendation Sunday is the Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre or somewhere with a roof.

## Mon Sep 21 — not one event in the park

Zero one-off permits. 67°, mostly cloudy, 36% chance of a shower. The drives are completely clear and nobody is on them.

**If you can move a workout to Monday, move it.** An empty park on a cool grey Monday is the best riding and running the week offers, rain risk and all.

## Tue Sep 23 – Fri Sep 25 — cool, damp, and quiet on the road

Nothing touches a drive for the rest of the week. Two concerts at Rumsey — Young the Giant Tuesday, Of Monsters and Men Wednesday — draw evening crowds to the 72nd Street approaches, and Kate Farms Yoga takes East Meadow Wednesday 5–6 PM.

Temperatures sit in the low-to-mid 60s with 22–37% shower chances. Cool running weather, genuinely — the first of the year.

**[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** runs five mornings from Loeb Boathouse at 7:30 and 8:30. Fall migration is peaking right now, and a damp cool week is exactly when the Ramble fills up.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** remains closed all day, every day — the permits run to **October 13**. Another three weeks of routing around it.

East Meadow is closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn is out Monday through Thursday. Neither changes a route.

## Quick recap

- **Saturday morning is the last dry ride or run** in this email.
- **Sunday 7 AM–1:30 PM: 18-mile training run on East Drive, in 89% rain.** No early window.
- **Monday has zero events and clear drives** — the pick of the week if you can take it.
- **Cool running weather arrives:** low 60s Tuesday onward, showers every day.
- **Fall migration is peaking** — Birding Bob five mornings from the Boathouse.
- **Bethesda Terrace closed through October 13.**
