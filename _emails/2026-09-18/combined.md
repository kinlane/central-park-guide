This is the week summer ends. Saturday holds at 71° and partly sunny, and after that the park does not see 70° again — the rest of the week runs 63–67° with a shower chance every single day, and Sunday carries an 89% chance of rain from morning to night. Plan around Saturday, because there is not another dry day in this email.

## Weather this week

Saturday is partly sunny and 71° with no rain in it; Sunday turns to all-day rain at 89%, and then the week settles into a cool, damp pattern — 67°, 63°, 65°, 64°, 66° — with shower chances between 22% and 37% every day. This is the seasonal turn, not a passing front.

## Sat Sep 19 — the only dry day, and it is a full one

**The [25th Annual Chess-in-the-Park Rapid Open](https://centralpark.guide/events/25th-annual-chess-in-the-park-rapid-open-2026-09-19/) takes Bandshell Plaza 11 AM–5 PM** — a quarter-century-old tournament, free to watch, and the best thing happening in the park this week.

Also on:

- **28th Annual SCTPN Walk for Education and Awareness**, Dead Road North, 9 AM–1 PM — a four-hour charity walk staging on the west side. Runners and cyclists should expect crowds at the Dead Road approaches through midday.
- **Osborn Cup** on Bowling Green, 7 AM–6 PM, both weekend days — lawn bowls, and worth ten minutes if you have never watched it.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs 9–3; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7; the **Farmstand and Unity Market** at Dana Discovery Center 9 AM–8 PM.
- **That Guitar Man** at Hernshead noon–6, the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2, **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7, **tango at the Shakespeare Statue** 6–9 PM.

East Meadow is closed, and **40 private ceremony bookings** land Saturday — the heaviest single day we have recorded this season. The scenic south-end spots are gone.

## Sun Sep 20, 7 AM — 18 miles in the rain

**[The TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 18M](https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-18m) goes off at 7 AM**, running East Drive and the 102nd Street Cross Drive, with the permit held until 1:30 PM. The field will be out there for five hours under an **89% chance of rain**.

**The loop is committed all morning.** Cyclists: take the day, or go after 1:30. Runners not in the field: the bridle path and Reservoir are your options. Anyone running it — this is the last long build before the marathon, and it is going to be a wet one.

The afternoon is the family window, weather permitting: **Justin Lees Jazz Group** at the Bandshell 1–4, **[Chess & Checkers Community Play](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/chess-checkers-house-community-play)** from 1, **Pinocchio in the Park** at the Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre 2–3 PM, **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–6, and a **Society Strong workout social** 3:30–6. The Marionette Theatre is indoors, which on this particular Sunday is the point.

## Mon Sep 21 — nothing at all

Not a single one-off event in the park. 67°, mostly cloudy. If you want the place to yourself, this is the day.

## Tue Sep 22 – Thu Sep 24 — Climate Week comes to the park

**[Music for Action](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/music-for-action/)**, a Climate Week concert with **Young the Giant and St. Lucia**, takes Rumsey Tuesday 7–10 PM — raising awareness of climate change and tick-borne disease. Thursday the **[Building Cross-Sector Ecosystems for Climate Action panel](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/24/building-crosssector-ecosystems-for-climate-action-panel-dis)** runs at the Arsenal 6–8 PM.

In between, **[Of Monsters and Men](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/of-monsters-and-men-august-ponthier/)** play Rumsey Wednesday 7–10 PM, and **Kate Farms Yoga** takes East Meadow Wednesday 5–6 PM. A financial-planning workshop sits at Dana Discovery Center Tuesday evening.

## Fri Sep 25 — salsa at the Bandshell

**Carlos Konig Salsa** plays Bandshell Plaza 5–7:30 PM, and the **Iconic Views tour** goes at 2. 66°, showers possible.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day, as it has been for weeks. The permits still run to **October 13**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free — a good rainy-week option.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse, 7:30 and 8:30. Fall migration is at its peak.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday.
- **208 field permits** — 103 at Heckscher, 75 at North Meadow, and only **14 on the Great Lawn**.

## Quick recap

- **Saturday is the only dry day this week.** Use it.
- **Chess-in-the-Park Rapid Open**, Saturday 11–5 at the Bandshell, 25th year.
- **Sunday: 89% rain over an 18-mile training run**, 7 AM–1:30 PM on East Drive.
- **Monday has no events at all** — an empty park at 67°.
- **Climate Week:** Young the Giant Tuesday, climate panel Thursday. Of Monsters and Men Wednesday.
- **Only two SummerStage shows left** after this week; the season closes Oct 1.
