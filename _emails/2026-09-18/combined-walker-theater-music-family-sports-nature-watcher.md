Saturday is the last dry day of the season's turn, and the park has put everything on it: a chess tournament in its twenty-fifth year at the Bandshell, lawn bowls on Bowling Green, model yachts, two markets and a guitar man at Hernshead. Sunday it rains all day. Then Climate Week arrives midweek with two concerts and a panel, and the temperature never climbs out of the 60s again.

## Weather this week

Saturday holds at 71° and partly sunny with no rain in it; Sunday turns to all-day rain at 89%. The rest of the week runs cool and damp — 63–67° with shower chances every day. Saturday is the day to be outdoors, and the indoor options midweek are worth knowing.

## Sat Sep 19 — the fullest day, and the last dry one

**The [25th Annual Chess-in-the-Park Rapid Open](https://centralpark.guide/events/25th-annual-chess-in-the-park-rapid-open-2026-09-19/) fills Bandshell Plaza 11 AM–5 PM.** Twenty-five years of this tournament, free to watch, and a genuinely absorbing thing to stumble into — rows of boards, clocks going, total concentration in the middle of the park.

The rest of the day:

- **Osborn Cup** on Bowling Green, 7 AM–6 PM, both weekend days — lawn bowls, a real curiosity.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate 9–7, and the **Farmstand and Unity Market** up at Dana Discovery Center 9 AM–8 PM.
- **That Guitar Man from Central Park** at Hernshead, noon–6 PM.
- **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2 PM; **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7 PM.
- **Tango at the Shakespeare Statue**, 6–9 PM.
- The **28th Annual SCTPN Walk for Education and Awareness** stages at Dead Road North 9 AM–1 PM, so the west side is busy through midday.

East Meadow is closed, and 40 private ceremony bookings make it the heaviest day of the season.

## Sun Sep 20 — rain all day, so plan indoors

An 89% chance of rain from morning to night. The morning belongs to an 18-mile marathon training run on East Drive; the afternoon has real options if you dress for it:

- **Pinocchio in the Park** at the **Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre**, 2–3 PM — under a roof, which today is the whole argument.
- **Justin Lees Jazz Group** at Bandshell Plaza, 1–4 PM.
- **[Chess & Checkers House Community Play](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/events/chess-checkers-house-community-play)** from 1 PM — casual games, beginners welcome, and the Chess & Checkers House has walls.
- **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello 11–6, **Skate Circle** 2–7, and a **Society Strong workout social** 3:30–6 for the undeterred.

## Mon Sep 21 — an empty park

Not one scheduled event. 67° and cloudy. If you like the park quiet, this is the quietest it gets.

## Tue Sep 22 — Climate Week: Young the Giant at Rumsey

**[Music for Action](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/music-for-action/)** brings **Young the Giant, St. Lucia and a DREEEMY DJ set** to Rumsey Playfield, 7–10 PM — a Climate Week show raising awareness of climate change and tick-borne disease.

Earlier: **Brigadoon on the River** at Bandshell Plaza 2–3 PM, and a financial vision-boarding workshop at Dana Discovery Center at 6.

## Wed Sep 23 — Of Monsters and Men

**[Of Monsters and Men](https://cityparksfoundation.org/events/of-monsters-and-men-august-ponthier/)** play Rumsey 7–10 PM with August Ponthier — the Icelandic band behind "Little Talks." **Kate Farms Yoga** takes East Meadow 5–6 PM beforehand.

## Thu Sep 24 – Fri Sep 25 — a climate panel, then salsa

Thursday the **[Building Cross-Sector Ecosystems for Climate Action panel](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events/2026/09/24/building-crosssector-ecosystems-for-climate-action-panel-dis)** runs at the Arsenal 6–8 PM — indoors, free, and the thinking half of Climate Week.

Friday, **Carlos Konig Salsa** plays Bandshell Plaza 5–7:30 PM, with the **Iconic Views tour** at 2.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is closed all day, every day — the permits run to **October 13**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free and indoors. The right call on a wet week.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse. Fall migration is peaking.
- **208 field permits** — 103 at Heckscher, 75 at North Meadow, and just 14 on the Great Lawn.

## Quick recap

- **Chess-in-the-Park Rapid Open, Saturday 11–5** at the Bandshell — 25th year, free to watch.
- **Saturday is the last dry day**, and everything is on it.
- **Sunday rains all day:** Pinocchio at the Marionette Theatre and the Chess & Checkers House are your indoor options.
- **Climate Week:** Young the Giant Tuesday, Of Monsters and Men Wednesday, climate panel Thursday.
- **Monday has no events at all.**
- **Salsa at the Bandshell Friday**, 5–7:30 PM.

Enjoy the turn into fall,
Central Park Guide
