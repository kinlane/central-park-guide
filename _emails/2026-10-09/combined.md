Saturday is the one good day on the front of this week — 70° and partly sunny — and then **Sunday brings a 93% chance of rain onto the triathlon's final day.** Midweek the park is quiet but not clear: an all-day permit holds **East Drive until 8 PM on both Wednesday and Thursday.** Friday, Open House New York opens at Grand Army Plaza.

## Weather this week

Saturday 70° and partly sunny with no rain in it. Sunday 64° with **periods of rain at 93%**, and 97% overnight. Columbus Day Monday is 69° with a 49% chance. Tuesday warms to 73° before afternoon storms, Wednesday is 66° and mostly sunny, Thursday 63°, and Friday turns wet again at 64° and 43%.

## Sat Oct 10 — the good day, and the Great Lawn is in use

The **[Big Apple Triathlon](https://centralpark.guide/events/big-apple-triathlon-2026-10-10/)** still holds Cherry Hill, Wagner Cove, Center Drive, the Lower Loop, West Drive and East Drive on an all-day permit. **The loop is committed.**

Everything else is worth the 70°:

- **Pilates in the Park on the Great Lawn, 10 AM–12 PM** — the lawn is properly back in use after the festival.
- **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere Castle at 10; **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs, 9 AM–3 PM; **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9–7.
- **Connect Outdoors** with the Appalachian Mountain Club, East 72nd Street Playground, 11 AM–2 PM.
- **Santa's Knights and Gladiators** behind the Met, noon–4 PM — armoured combat.

East Meadow closed, and **38 private ceremony bookings** take the scenic spots.

## Sun Oct 11 — the triathlon's last day, in 93% rain

The triathlon permit runs through Sunday and the forecast gives it **periods of rain at 93%**. The drives stay committed all day either way.

Still on: **NYC Indigenous Day of Remembrance** at Merchants' Gate, 1–4 PM. The **International Folkdancers** hold King Jagiello 11 AM–6 PM, **Skate Circle** runs at Dead Road 2–7 PM, the **Northeast Young Judaea Kickoff** takes The Pool 11 AM–2 PM, and **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere at 10.

## Mon Oct 12 — Columbus Day, and almost nothing booked

69° with a 49% chance of rain. **Skate Circle** at Dead Road North, 2–7 PM, is the only thing on the board. The triathlon permit has lapsed, so the drives are yours again.

## Tue Oct 13 — 73°, nothing scheduled

The warmest day of the week and not one event in the park. Mostly sunny early with a 28% chance of afternoon storms, so go in the morning.

## Wed Oct 14 – Thu Oct 15 — an all-day permit holds East Drive

**A private event holds Cedar Hill, the Hans Christian Andersen Statue and East Drive from midnight to 8 PM on both days.**

It arrives in the permit feed titled only "Party," which badly undersells it: this is twenty hours of East Drive, twice. **Runners and cyclists — plan the east side out of both days**, or ride clockwise and work around it. Wednesday is the better weather of the two at 66° and mostly sunny.

## Fri Oct 16 — Open House New York

**Open House New York Weekend** opens at Grand Army Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM — the city's annual architecture weekend, and the park is one of its sites. 64° with a 43% chance of showers.

## Steady all week

- **[NYC Parks' 44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** is at the Arsenal all seven days — the submission window for the winter wreath show.
- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** — closed all day, every day. The permits now run to **November 9**.
- **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9–5, free.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse. Migration's last weeks.
- East Meadow closed both weekend days; East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday.
- **157 field permits** — 82 at North Meadow, 46 at Heckscher, 18 on the tennis courts. North Meadow has now overtaken Heckscher as the busiest venue.

## Quick recap

- **Saturday is the week's best day** — 70°, partly sunny, and the Great Lawn is hosting pilates.
- **Sunday: 93% rain on the triathlon's final day.** Drives committed regardless.
- **East Drive is held midnight to 8 PM Wednesday AND Thursday** under a permit titled only "Party."
- **Tuesday is 73° with nothing booked** — the pick if you want the park empty and warm.
- **Open House New York opens Friday** at Grand Army Plaza.
- **Bethesda Terrace now closed through November 9.**
