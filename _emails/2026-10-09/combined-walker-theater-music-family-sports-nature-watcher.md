Saturday is the day to be out — 70° and partly sunny, with pilates on the Great Lawn, model yachts, a market and armoured combat behind the Met. Sunday turns to 93% rain but still carries **NYC Indigenous Day of Remembrance** at Merchants' Gate. And Friday, **Open House New York** opens at Grand Army Plaza.

## Weather this week

Saturday 70° and partly sunny, dry. Sunday 64° with periods of rain at 93%. Columbus Day Monday 69° and a 49% chance. Tuesday is the warmest at 73°, Wednesday 66° and mostly sunny, Thursday 63°, Friday 64° and showery. Nights in the low 50s — autumn proper.

## Sat Oct 10 — the full day

- **Pilates in the Park on the Great Lawn, 10 AM–12 PM.** The lawn is back in use after the Global Citizen build, and this is the first public class on it.
- **Santa's Knights and Gladiators** on the lawn behind the Met, noon–4 PM — armoured combat in Central Park, exactly as advertised and genuinely a spectacle.
- **Model Yacht Racing** at Kerbs Boathouse, 9 AM–3 PM, on Conservatory Water.
- **UnityMarketsNY** at Farmers' Gate, 9 AM–7 PM.
- **Connect Outdoors** with the Appalachian Mountain Club at the East 72nd Street Playground, 11 AM–2 PM — free, and built for meeting people.
- **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere Castle at 10; **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** at 2.

The Big Apple Triathlon still holds Cherry Hill and the drives, so the south end carries equipment and crowds. East Meadow closed; 38 private ceremony bookings.

## Sun Oct 11 — Indigenous Day of Remembrance, and rain

**NYC Indigenous Day of Remembrance** at Merchants' Gate, 1–4 PM — on the weekend the city still officially calls Columbus Day, which makes the siting at the park's busiest southwest entrance deliberate.

93% rain, so dress for it. Also on: **International Folkdancers** at King Jagiello Plaza 11 AM–6 PM, **Skate Circle** at Dead Road 2–7 PM, the **Northeast Young Judaea Kickoff** at The Pool 11 AM–2 PM, and **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** at Belvedere at 10.

## Mon Oct 12 – Thu Oct 15 — a very quiet midweek

Columbus Day brings only the **Skate Circle** at Dead Road, 2–7 PM. Tuesday has nothing at all and is the week's warmest day at 73°. Wednesday and Thursday carry a large private event at Cedar Hill that holds ground until 8 PM both days — worth knowing if you walk the east side, but nothing public.

The indoor options carry the midweek: **New Dendrology** at the Arsenal Gallery, weekdays 9 AM–5 PM, free, and the **[44th Annual Wreath Interpretations Open Call](https://www.nycgovparks.org/parks/central-park/events)** is running at the Arsenal all week — the submission window for the winter wreath show, which is a nice thing to know exists.

## Fri Oct 16 — Open House New York opens

**Open House New York Weekend** opens at Grand Army Plaza, 10 AM–6 PM. The city's annual architecture weekend, and the park is one of its sites — worth planning the weekend around if buildings and landscapes are your thing. 64° with a 43% chance of showers.

## Steady all week

- **Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** closed all day, every day — permits now run to **November 9**.
- **[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/)** — five mornings from Loeb Boathouse. Fall migration is nearly done.
- **Columbus Circle Food Market** at Merchants' Gate, 9:30 AM–9 PM.
- **157 field permits** — 82 at North Meadow, 46 at Heckscher, 18 on the tennis courts. North Meadow has overtaken Heckscher as the park's busiest venue for the first time.

## Quick recap

- **Saturday is the day:** pilates on the Great Lawn at 10, armoured combat behind the Met at noon.
- **Indigenous Day of Remembrance Sunday**, 1–4 PM at Merchants' Gate.
- **Sunday is a 93% washout** otherwise.
- **Tuesday: 73° and nothing booked** — the warmest, emptiest day.
- **Open House New York opens Friday** at Grand Army Plaza.
- **Bethesda Terrace now closed through November 9.**
