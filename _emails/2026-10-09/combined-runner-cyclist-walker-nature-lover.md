A week with two separate road closures and a narrow gap between them. **The triathlon holds every drive through Sunday.** It lapses Monday — and then **an all-day permit takes East Drive from midnight to 8 PM on both Wednesday and Thursday.** Your clear window is Monday, Tuesday and Friday.

## Weather this week

Saturday 70° and partly sunny, dry. Sunday 64° with **periods of rain at 93%**. Columbus Day Monday 69° with a 49% chance. Tuesday 73° and mostly sunny before a 28% afternoon storm risk. Wednesday 66° and mostly sunny, Thursday 63°, Friday 64° and 43% showers. Nights in the low 50s.

## Sat Oct 10 – Sun Oct 11 — the triathlon finishes

**The [Big Apple Triathlon](https://centralpark.guide/events/big-apple-triathlon-2026-10-10/) holds Cherry Hill, Wagner Cove, Center Drive, the Lower Loop, West Drive and East Drive** on all-day permits both days. Saturday is 70° and dry; Sunday carries a 93% chance of rain over the whole thing.

**The loop is unavailable both days.** Bridle path and the Reservoir track are untouched, and that is where your weekend miles are.

If you are out on foot: **Pilates in the Park** has the Great Lawn Saturday 10 AM–12 PM, which is the first real sign that lawn is back in business. **[Birding Basics](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/birding-basics-ramble-tour)** meets at Belvedere Castle at 10 both mornings — migration's last good weeks — and the **[Iconic Views tour](https://www.centralparknyc.org/activities/tours/iconic-views-of-central-park-tour)** goes at 2.

East Meadow is closed both days, with 38 private bookings Saturday.

## Mon Oct 12 — the drives come back

Columbus Day, 69°, a 49% chance of rain, and **the triathlon permit has lapsed**. One event in the whole park (skate circle at Dead Road). The loop is clear for the first time since Wednesday the 7th.

Take it even if it drizzles — the next clear day after this is Friday.

## Tue Oct 13 — 73° and nothing booked

The warmest day of the week, nothing scheduled anywhere, drives clear. Mostly sunny in the morning with a 28% storm chance later.

**This is the day.** Go early and go long.

## Wed Oct 14 – Thu Oct 15 — East Drive is held, twenty hours a day

**An all-day permit holds Cedar Hill, the Hans Christian Andersen Statue and East Drive from midnight to 8 PM — both days.**

The feed titles it only "Party," which tells you nothing about the twenty hours of East Drive it actually covers. **Plan the east side out of both days.** A clockwise lap on West Drive and the cross drives still works, and the bridle path is clear.

66° and mostly sunny Wednesday, 63° Thursday — annoying weather to lose.

## Fri Oct 16 — clear road, wet sky

64° with a 43% chance of showers and nothing on the drives. **Open House New York** opens at Grand Army Plaza 10 AM–6 PM, so the southeast corner is busy.

## Steady all week

**Bethesda Terrace and Fountain** is closed all day, every day, and the permits have extended a fifth time — now **November 9**. Eight weeks elapsed.

[Bird Watching with Birding Bob](https://www.centralpark.com/events/bird-watching-with-birding-bob/) runs five mornings from Loeb Boathouse at 7:30 and 8:30. Fall migration is close to done, so these are the last of them.

East 106th Street lawn out Monday–Thursday; East Meadow closed both weekend days.

## Quick recap

- **Triathlon holds every drive Saturday and Sunday.** Bridle path and Reservoir instead.
- **Sunday is 93% rain** on top of it.
- **Monday the drives come back** — the first clear day in five.
- **Tuesday is the week: 73°, nothing booked, clear road.**
- **East Drive held midnight–8 PM Wednesday AND Thursday** under a permit titled only "Party."
- **Bethesda Terrace extended again, now through November 9.**

Monday, Tuesday and Friday are yours,
