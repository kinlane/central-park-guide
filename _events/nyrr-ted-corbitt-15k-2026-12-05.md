New York Road Runners' 15K in Central Park honoring the life and legacy of Ted Corbitt, the first African American Olympic marathoner and NYRR's founding president.

## Event Details

- **Event:** NYRR Ted Corbitt 15K
- **Date:** Saturday, December 5, 2026
- **Time:** 08:00 – 10:30
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/nyrr-ted-corbitt-15k

## Route Impact

The 18th annual running of the race honoring Ted Corbitt (1919–2007), the NYRR founding president the park's 6.02-mile loop is named for. Roughly a loop and a half of the drives from 8 AM on a Saturday morning. Start line and course map are published closer to race day.
