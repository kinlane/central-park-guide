The TCS New York City Marathon, the world's largest marathon — 26.2 miles across all five boroughs, starting on Staten Island and finishing in Central Park.

## Event Details

- **Event:** TCS New York City Marathon
- **Date:** Sunday, November 1, 2026
- **Time:** 08:00 – 17:00
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon

## Route Impact

The marathon starts on Staten Island at 8 AM and runs all five boroughs; its final miles come into Central Park, where the race finishes. The park is given over to the race and its spectators from early morning into the afternoon — treat the drives, the south end, and Central Park West as unusable for running or riding all day. NYRR publishes the closure map and spectator guidance closer to race day.
