New York Road Runners' New Year's Eve four-mile run through Central Park, starting at midnight under fireworks.

## Event Details

- **Event:** NYRR Midnight Run
- **Date:** Thursday, December 31, 2026
- **Time:** 23:59 – 01:30
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/nyrr-midnight-run

## Route Impact

New Year's Eve four-miler that starts as the clock strikes midnight, with a fireworks display over the park to send the field off. A costume party and pre-race dance run in the hours before. The park's south end and the drives carry crowds from late evening through roughly 1:30 AM on New Year's Day.
