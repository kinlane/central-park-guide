The 33rd running of the Race to Deliver, a four-mile Central Park race benefiting God's Love We Deliver, which cooks and delivers meals to New Yorkers living with severe and chronic illness.

## Event Details

- **Event:** Race to Deliver 4M to Benefit God's Love We Deliver
- **Date:** Sunday, November 22, 2026
- **Time:** 08:00 – 10:00
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/race-to-deliver-4m-to-benefit-god-s-love-we-deliver

## Route Impact

A four-mile race on the park drives on a Sunday morning; plan on race traffic on the loop roughly 8–10 AM. NYRR publishes the start line and course map closer to race day.
