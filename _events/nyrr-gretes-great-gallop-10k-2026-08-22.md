An NYRR members-only 10K celebrating the legacy of Grete Waitz, nine-time New York City Marathon champion, run on her old Mini 10K course through Central Park.

## Event Details

- **Event:** NYRR Grete's Great Gallop 10K
- **Date:** Saturday, August 22, 2026
- **Time:** 07:30 – 10:00
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/nyrr-grete-s-great-gallop-10k

## Route Impact

NYRR members-only 10K on the course where Grete Waitz won her five New York Mini 10K titles. Start line and course map are published closer to race day; plan on the park drives carrying race traffic roughly 7–10 AM on Saturday morning.
