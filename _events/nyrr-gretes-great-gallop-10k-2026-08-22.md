An NYRR members-only 10K celebrating the legacy of Grete Waitz, nine-time New York City Marathon champion, run on her old Mini 10K course through Central Park. Closes NYRR Member Week (August 17–22) with a post-race festival. Registration is sold out and the member standby list is full — this is a spectating event unless you already hold a bib. A free Rising New York Road Runners race for kids ages 2 to 11 runs alongside it.

## Event Details

- **Event:** NYRR Grete's Great Gallop 10K
- **Date:** Saturday, August 22, 2026
- **Time:** 07:30 – 12:00
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Bandshell Plaza, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/nyrr-grete-s-great-gallop-10k

## Route Impact

NYRR members-only 10K on the course where Grete Waitz won her five New York Mini 10K titles. The permit covers West Drive, East Drive and the Center Loop from 7:30 AM, with Bandshell Plaza held for the post-race festival until noon — so the loop is compromised for runners and cyclists well past the finish, roughly 7:30 AM to 12 PM, not just the race window.
