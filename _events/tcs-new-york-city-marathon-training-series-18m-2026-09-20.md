New York Road Runners' 18-mile marathon training run in Central Park, the second and longer leg of the TCS New York City Marathon Training Series.

## Event Details

- **Event:** TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 18M
- **Date:** Sunday, September 20, 2026
- **Time:** 07:00 – 12:00
- **Location:** Ted Corbitt Loop, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-18m

## Route Impact

The longest training run of the series — three loops of the park drives, starting 7 AM on a Sunday and running well into the late morning. The single busiest non-race morning on the loop before marathon day. Start line and course map are published closer to race day.
