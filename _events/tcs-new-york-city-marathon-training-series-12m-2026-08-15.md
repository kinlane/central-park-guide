New York Road Runners' 12-mile marathon training run in Central Park, the first of the two-race TCS New York City Marathon Training Series.

## Event Details

- **Event:** TCS New York City Marathon Training Series 12M
- **Date:** Saturday, August 15, 2026
- **Time:** 07:00 – 10:30
- **Location:** East Drive, 102nd Street Cross Drive, Central Park
- **Organizer:** New York Road Runners
- **Official site:** https://events.nyrr.org/tcs-new-york-city-marathon-training-series-12m

## Route Impact

First of the marathon training-series runs — two loops of the park drives at marathon pace, starting 7 AM. Race-Day Central is behind the backstop of Ballfield #8 near East Drive and 102nd Street, so the north end carries staging from around 5:30 AM. Expect the full loop to be busy with runners until mid-morning; the loop is not closed to the public, but it will be crowded and slow for cyclists.
